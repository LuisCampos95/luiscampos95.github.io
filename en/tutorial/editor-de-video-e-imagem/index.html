How to use the video and image editor

From a three-click image to a video with several mobs, animations and camera moves, step by step with pictures.

by OrbisHytale · September 23, 2026

The editor turns any creature or item on the wiki into an image or a video ready to post. It all runs in your browser: nothing to install, no account, no cost.

Part 1 · First time

1. Open the editor

Go to a creature or item page (for example, the Grizzly Bear) and click Edit video and image, below the 3D model. You can also open it from the menu, under Editor.

2. The screen

The editor opens with the creature already moving. It has four parts:

  • Left: the Animations, Background & effects and Adjust tabs
  • Middle: the preview, exactly how the file will look
  • Bottom: the timeline, with the pieces of the video
  • Top corner: the Export button

3. Just want an image? Three clicks

1. Drag the model in the preview to pick the angle
2. Click Export
3. Pick Image, then Save file

Done. The image comes out in high resolution (1080 × 1920 in the vertical format).

4. Pick an animation

In the Animations tab, tap an animation's name to preview it. Like it? Tap + to put it in the video.

  • On ground / In water / In the air: shows only the animations for that place
  • All, Idle, Movement, Attack, Damage: filters by type
  • The gold bars show which animation is playing in the preview

The first animation you pick replaces the "Idle" the editor adds on its own. From the second one on, they play one after another.

5. Pick the format

On the player bar, on the right, are the three formats:

  • 9:16 (tall): Stories, Reels, TikTok and Shorts
  • 1:1 (square): Instagram feed and Discord
  • 16:9 (wide): YouTube, X and Reddit

6. Change the background

In the Background & effects tab, pick a scene. Effects turn on and off with one tap: creature name, stats, stage light, shadow, darken and blur the background.

7. Export

Click Export and pick:

  • Video (MP4): best for social media
  • GIF: for Discord and forums (bigger file, slower to make)
  • Image: what the preview shows right now (pause where you want)
  • Model, transparent: a transparent PNG to use in another program

Part 2 · Going further

The timeline

Each animation becomes a blue block on the timeline. The video plays the blocks from left to right.

  • Change the length: drag the block's right edge
  • Change the order: drag the block somewhere else
  • Split, duplicate, delete: select the block and use the buttons above the timeline
  • Timeline zoom: the − and + slider on the right
  • Start and end: the arrows on both sides of play

Adjusting each block

With a block selected, the Adjust tab changes only that block:

  • Camera movement: still, spin, push in, pull out and pan
  • Speed: from 0.5× (slow motion) to 2×
  • Camera: size, angle and position of the model on screen

Tip: dragging the model in the preview also changes the selected block's angle.

Several mobs and items in one video

In the Animations tab, click Add another mob or item, search by name and tap the result. It goes straight onto the timeline, and its animations show up in the list.

Undo and shortcuts

The arrows at the top undo and redo. On a computer:

  • Space: play and pause
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y: undo and redo
  • Delete: delete the selected block

On a phone

It works the same. The tabs sit on the bottom bar and open over the preview; tap OK to close.

Common problems

  • The video takes a while: normal on simpler phones. A 10-second video takes 10 to 30 seconds. Do not switch tabs while it works.
  • Slow with many mobs: each mob is one more 3D model. On a simple phone, use up to 3 per video.
  • Old browser: the video comes out in lower quality. Update Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
  • Closed it by accident: the editor does not save the project. Closing resets everything.
