Hytale Update 1: what changed

Four days into Early Access, Update 1 added dinosaurs to underground jungles, a backpack at half the leather cost, 14-block bench reach and a large batch of fixes, alongside the January 13 and 15 hotfixes.

Hytale entered Early Access on January 13, 2026. Four days later, Update 1 was out. The studio promised a fast patch cadence, and the first one already brought new content, progression tweaks and a long list of fixes. Before it, two hotfixes had already put out the most urgent launch fires.

Main changes

  • Dinosaurs underground. The underground jungles got dinosaurs and other creatures.
  • Cheaper backpack. The Leather Backpack now comes from a Tier 1 Workbench and costs half the leather.
  • Longer bench reach. Benches pull materials from chests up to 14 blocks away horizontally and 6 vertically.
  • Earlier bedtime. Beds work from 7:30 PM.
  • New avatar faces. Cat, demonic, goat and reptile eyes.

Creatures

The underground jungle dinosaurs arrived with placeholder behavior, and the studio flagged them as a work in progress. Fitting them in required changing the biome environment. That means players on an older world will only find them in areas that haven't been explored yet.

The rest of the list fixes behavior that had been bugging players since launch:

  • NPCs that lacked knockback resistance now have it.
  • Fire creatures are immune to fire damage. For now, they still catch fire.
  • The Earthen Golem got better combat and a reworked spin attack.
  • The Skeleton no longer takes drowning damage.
  • Kweebecs are immune to environmental damage from cactus and brambles.
  • The Trork Elder swapped its placeholder summon animation for a final one.
  • Kweebecs and Trorks in the Whisperfrost Frontiers now have proper loot tables.
  • Livestock no longer climbs on top of the Chicken Coop.
  • Cautious predators such as the Fox only fight the player after being attacked.

Items and equipment

The backpack was one of the early-game bottlenecks, and Update 1 changed it in three ways:

  • The Leather Backpack moved from a Tier 2 Workbench to Tier 1.
  • Its cost dropped from 16 to 8 Medium Leather.
  • The first backpack upgrade now needs a Tier 2 bench instead of Tier 3.

In practice, you get extra inventory space much sooner.

Leather got faster too. Light Hide, Medium Hide and Heavy Hide take 10 fewer seconds to turn into leather.

Farming and food

  • Corn gave 4 Essence of Life at the Farmer's Workbench. A misconfiguration made it better than higher-tier crops. It now gives 1.
  • Mature Cauliflower and Mature Pumpkin no longer drop regular seeds when broken by hand or with a tool.
  • Fully grown Eternal crops also stopped dropping Eternal seeds when broken.
  • Raw fruits and vegetables only grant Health Regen I. The other bonuses are reserved for cooked food, which gives cooking a real purpose.

Wood and tools

  • Logs now show their wood type. The Oak Log, for example, reads as hardwood.
  • Recipes that asked for a "trunk" now correctly ask for a "log".
  • When a recipe needs a specific wood type, the tooltip lists which trees provide it. Softwood, for example, comes from Banyan Log, Beech Log and Aspen Log.
  • The Woodcutter's Block no longer shows "Dev" quality and lost a processing interaction that did nothing. It is now a decorative furniture piece, crafted at the Furniture Workbench.
  • Torches and other handheld lights can be placed straight from the utility slot while holding a pickaxe or hatchet.
  • Pickaxes and hatchets only swing with the primary action.
  • Processing benches show item tooltips in the upgrade requirements panel.
  • Dropped items no longer get stuck on the side of blocks.

World and blocks

  • Devastated Lands sewers now have proper atmospheric effects and creature spawns.
  • Areas beneath volcanoes use volcanic environmental effects.
  • Feran villages had entity placement, textures and interiors fixed.
  • An invisible filler block behind the bench in the Forgotten Temple was removed.
  • Water flows correctly when sources are stacked.

Gameplay

  • You can sleep from 7:30 PM.
  • Weapon attacks now check line of sight. Hitting through walls got much harder.
  • Regular Burn deals less damage. Burn from lava is unchanged.
  • Cactus and brambles now deal the Environmental damage type.
  • The first time you use The Heart of Orbis, a panel explains the Memories system. It can be reopened from the "?" in the top-left corner of the Memories screen.

Interface and quality of life

  • Toggle Fullscreen, Switch HUD and Take Screenshot can no longer be bound to mouse buttons.
  • The game warns when launching a pre-release build or opening a world from another version.
  • Sprinting field of view is capped at 160° to avoid the "hyper-drive" effect.
  • Cancelling LAN server discovery works again.

Avatar

  • New eyes: Cat Eye, Demonic Eyes, Goat Eye and Reptile Eye.
  • New mouths: Large Mouth and Tiny Lips.
  • New hair color: Lavender.
  • The Bangs hairstyle was reworked, and Dark Brown got brighter.
  • The Featherbound Hood shows up again for Supporter edition owners.

Audio and visual effects

  • Stone and ores got new hit and break sounds. Ores also got new breaking particles.
  • The Horse plays its running sounds again while mounted.
  • The torch burn loop is softer.
  • Volumes were adjusted for breaking cocoons, the Pig, the Piglet and the Warthog.
  • Sword swings vary less in pitch.
  • Eating fruit now shows particles. Other foods will get the effect later.
  • A particle appears above an NPC's head when you absorb its memory.
  • The Health Potion got glitter, glow and spiral effects.
  • The Rope and several plants now sit correctly in the hand.

Performance and stability

Update 1 closed a series of crashes:

  • When using screenshot folders with non-English names.
  • When picking certain hairstyles with variants.
  • With corrupted cached avatar previews.
  • When the Memories plugin failed to load.
  • When updating the player model.

"Waiting for chunks" took too long with view distance below 128, and that was fixed. A localization loop hitting Norwegian players is gone too, along with a session refresh bug on singleplayer servers.

January 13 hotfix

Shipped on launch day and aimed at what kept people from playing:

  • A crash when picking certain hairstyles and then eyebrows in character creation.
  • A crash when taking screenshots with a non-English folder name.
  • Norwegian players could not launch the game.
  • A crash when using the Wooden Bucket. It broke some workbenches, which need to be destroyed and placed again.
  • The client now switches to offline mode when the session becomes invalid.
  • The launcher warns when the computer clock is out of date, a cause of some login failures.
  • A vulnerability let players spoof their username. The effect was purely visual, and it is now patched.

January 15 hotfix

  • If authentication fails, the game switches to offline mode instead of getting stuck.
  • "Invalid token" errors caused by a slightly out-of-sync system clock were fixed.
  • The client runs lighter thanks to better background task scheduling.
  • Dropped items no longer get stuck inside blocks.
  • A server crash in specific situations was fixed.

Notable fixes

  • Charged attacks could skip their stamina cost with a well-timed hotbar swap.
  • Signature attacks could be used without signature energy.
  • The signature-ready sound played at the wrong time in adventure mode.
  • Some items had a longer interaction range than intended.
  • Memory rewards unlocked too early and did not match the required number of memories.
  • Crops like the Tomato did not grow properly. Chunks are now saved when a farming block updates.

For servers and modders

  • /tp <player> <targetPlayer> had its argument order fixed.
  • /give accepts item durability.
  • Whitelist and ban commands accept online usernames or offline UUIDs.
  • The server now uses BBR congestion control for QUIC.
  • Authentication and session token error handling improved.
  • The interaction system handles server lag spikes better.
  • A race condition in instance teleports removed and re-added entities. It is fixed.
  • Regular expression parsing in /replace was fixed.
  • Since the January 13 hotfix, the server prints a message on boot when authentication is required.
