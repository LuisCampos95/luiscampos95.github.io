What the Goblin Scientist and the hidden art reveal

Goblin Scientist, Voidtaken Goblin, new trorks, armor sets and a bone glider: what sits in the files with no NPC or item, and what it may hint at.

This is a theory: speculation based on official information, marked as such throughout the text. Nothing here is confirmed by Hypixel Studios unless a source says so.

Not everything in Hytale's files shows up in the game. Between builds, the studio leaves 3D models ready, with textures and parts, but with no creature or item using them. The wiki keeps these models under an "art only" tag. Some have been there since launch. Others arrived just now, alongside Chapter 1.

This article gathers the most curious ones and keeps three things apart:

  • Official: what the studio said on hytale.com.
  • In the files: what exists in the game data, with no interpretation.
  • Theory: our reading. It may be wrong.

The goblin scientist

Update, 09/24. The theory partly came true: in the 09/24 pre-release the Goblin Makinomagistra became a real NPC, with 100 health and the name Makinomagistra, alongside the goblin lab and the Goblin Tinkerer's Workbench, the bench that crafts runes. Details in Update 7, Part 4.

In the files. The Goblin Makinomagistra showed up in the 09/03 pre-release, the first one of Update 7. The model sits in the goblin attachments folder and comes with four parts of its own: a mechanical arm with a wrench at the tip, a backpack carrying an explosive mushroom, a screw earring and magnifying glasses. There is no NPC tied to it. No health, no behavior, no drops.

Official. In the Chapter 1 blog, the studio calls goblins tinkerers and says their inventions end up in your hands. The same post says not every goblin wants you dead: some are friendly, and one of them runs a shop.

In the files. The Update 7 gadgets are crafted at a goblin lab workbench. A placeholder text describes rune shards used to craft runes at a "Goblin Tinkering Bench", dropped by dangerous enemies in the Guttering Hollows, the Chapter 1 goblin world. There is also a placeholder "Portal Console" that keeps the Guttering Hollows open longer in exchange for goblin materials.

Theory. A goblin with magnifying glasses, a tool arm and an explosive backpack looks like the one running the lab. Our guess is that the Goblin Makinomagistra becomes the NPC behind the rune bench or the portal console. Nothing in the files links them yet.

The shop goblin

In the files. The Goblin Merchant is also model only for now. It uses the regular goblin body and carries a merchant barrel as an attachment.

Theory. If a Chapter 1 goblin runs a shop, as the official blog says, this is the most obvious candidate.

The goblin taken by the Void

In the files. The Voidtaken Goblin arrived in the same 09/03 pre-release. It has its own texture and four parts: eyes, hood, tunic and foot wraps, all tagged Voidtaken. It has no NPC either.

In the files. The word Voidtaken is not alone. The Memories book has two Void categories: Voidspawn and Voidtaken. The Voidheart description explains the difference: not every creature is born of the Void, but those taken and corrupted may carry the same heart.

Official. In Update 7 Part 2, Void creatures became hostile toward goblins, and most goblins run away when outnumbered.

Theory. Goblins fleeing the Void plus a corrupted goblin model tell a simple story: not all of them get away. The first Voidtaken with an NPC was the hunt bear, in the 09/17 pre-release. The goblin looks next in line.

The trorks nobody has seen

In the files. The Exceptional Trork and the Trork Hatchling landed in the files on 06/22. The first has its own model, an extra armor layer, separate eyes and eyebrows, and four haircuts: braids, cross, mohawk and samurai. The second is a trork youngling with its own model and texture.

Official. The Chapter 1 blog says every faction will be reworked in future chapters. Goblins came first.

Theory. Swappable haircuts and a youngling point to trorks with more visual variety and camps with families. If goblins got distinct roles in Chapter 1, trorks may be next. This is a bet, not an announcement. Want more on them? Read the factions guide.

Armor with no item

In the files. There are whole armor sets that no item in the game uses:

  • Ninja: Ninja Hood, Ninja Tunic, Ninja Wraps and Ninja Pants. The same loose model list has a Shuriken.
  • Orbis Knight: Ancient Knight’s Cuirass, Ancient Knight’s Gauntlets and Ancient Knight’s Greaves. The set's shield exists as an item, the Ancient Knight's Shield, but the wiki finds no recipe or creature that drops it.
  • White Mage: only White Mage Robe and White Mage Boots.
  • Necromancer: Necromancer Robe, Necromancer Shirt, Necromancer Gloves and Necromancer Pants.
  • Trailer Mage: Trailer Mage Hat, Trailer Mage Robe, Trailer Mage Pants, Trailer Mage Cape and Trailer Mage Short Cape.

There are loose capes too: Borean Cape, Raven Cape, Long Cape, Long White Cape, Short Cape and Fur Cape, plus the Raven Leather Cape and the Ornate Bronze Cape, which would complete sets that already exist as items. Outside armor there is a Banjo, a Brush and two Prisma weapons, the Prisma Daggers and the Prisma Spear, still wearing the magenta checkerboard placeholder texture.

Theory. The name Trailer Mage suggests an outfit made for a showcase video, not for the game. The Orbis Knight looks halfway there: the shield became an item, the other pieces did not. Ninja plus shuriken also stands out, but it may just be old tests.

The bone glider

In the files. The Bone Glider arrived in the 09/03 pre-release with two models, one open and one closed, and its own texture. The only glider that exists as an item is the goblin Scrap Glider, added in Update 7.

Theory. Bone weapons already belong to the Feran in the game, like the Feran Bone Axe and the Feran Bone Daggers. The Windrider Valley portal, tied to the Feran, carries a placeholder gliding tag. Put together, a Feran bone glider makes sense. None of this has been announced.

A shooter mode in storage

In the files. The Items/Hypixel/Minigames folder holds a whole shooter mode. Five weapons are registered as test items: the Rifle, the Handgun, the Frag Grenade, the Test Sniper Zoom and the Test Rifle Zoom. The rifle parts exist as models only: Reflex Sight, ACOG Scope, Rifle Red Laser, Handgun Red Laser and the Bullet. Next to them sit a space trooper armor (Trooper's Helm, Trooper's Cuirass and Trooper's Pants) and team clothes, the Team Cape and the Team Tunic, with red, blue, green and yellow textures. All of it has been there since the stable build.

Official. The studio promised minigames and a Hypixel-style network, covered in Official Hytale minigames.

Theory. A rifle with sights, capes by team color and a folder named Minigames could be leftovers from old prototypes or groundwork for the promised minigames. There is no way to tell.

Other models waiting their turn

In the files. The list of models with no NPC is long. Some highlights:

  • Void Dragon, a Void dragon.
  • Void Guardian Golem, a Void guardian golem.
  • Void Necromancer, a Void necromancer.
  • Goblin Boss, a goblin boss.
  • Cat, Kitten, Dog and Corgi, the last one with a red scarf.

The Void has an article of its own: Is Varyn coming back? The Void in Chapter 1. Pets are covered in The animals of Orbis.

Want to see it all together? The wiki keeps this material in two lists: every model-only item and every model-only creature.

Official, files and theory, side by side

  • Official: tinkering goblins, a goblin with a shop, factions reworked in later chapters, Void creatures against goblins.
  • In the files: models for the scientist, the corrupted goblin, the goblin merchant, two new trorks, four armor sets and a bone glider, all with no item or NPC.
  • Theory: the scientist runs runes or the portal, the corrupted goblin becomes an enemy, trorks are the next faction rework and the bone glider is Feran.

A model in the files is not a promise. A lot of art sits for months, and some of it never ships. When any of these pieces becomes an NPC or an item, its wiki page changes right away.
