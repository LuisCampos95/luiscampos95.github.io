Hytale Update 6: Hardcore, spectators and in-game mods

Update 6 brought Hardcore mode with five rule sets, spectating, an in-game mod browser and friend connections without a VPN.

Update 6 shipped on August 27 after 13 weeks of pre-release, from May 28 to August 22. In July, the studio had promised four things about three weeks out: spectating, Hardcore, a mod browser and player collision with entities. All four arrived, along with the largest set of Creative tools since the Trigger Volume and a networking change that fixes an old headache for people playing with friends.

It was also the first cycle with community code accepted into the game. The studio thanked the contributors and welcomed one of them, MelodicAlbuild, to the team.

Before updating, two heads-ups. Hytale now requires OpenGL 4.1 and closes immediately if your system lacks it. Updating your graphics driver almost always fixes it. And server owners and plugin authors need to rebuild, because the network protocol moved from hytale/2 to hytale/3.

Main changes

  • Mod browser on the main menu, with featured mods, search, filters and a library to update or remove what you already have.
  • Hardcore mode, chosen at world creation, with five life rules.
  • Spectator mode for players who die in Hardcore, with a free camera or following whoever is still alive.
  • Play with friends without a VPN: the STUN and TURN protocols connect players who used to hit a wall because of their NAT type.
  • Collision with rotated entities: players can stand on and be pushed by entities with hard collision.
  • Gyro aiming on controllers such as the DualSense and the Switch Pro.
  • Color Tool and palette presets in Creative.
  • Builder tools handle selections of up to 16 million blocks.
  • Much faster world generation and packet decoding 2 to 4 times faster.

Hardcore and spectating

Hardcore can only be turned on when creating a new world, through the Customize button, and can't be changed later. There are five options:

  • Off: a normal world, no Hardcore.
  • Permadeath: one life per player. Death is permanent.
  • Three Strikes: three lives per player. After strike three, you're out.
  • Nine Lives: nine lives shared by the whole group. On the ninth death, the run ends for everyone.
  • Soulbound: everyone's fate is tied together. If any player dies, the run is over.

In Three Strikes and Nine Lives, the number of lives can be raised or lowered at world creation. Zero is not allowed. Remaining lives appear above your health.

Players who die drop everything they carry and become spectators. Spectators are invisible, intangible and ignored by NPCs, so a dead friend never pulls aggro onto the group. They can swap between living players or fly the camera freely. Spectators don't count toward events that need a player count and don't keep dungeons open. The studio noted the system also lays groundwork for minigames.

Mods without leaving the game

The mod browser opens from the main menu with featured, popular and recently updated mods. You can filter by category or search names and descriptions. Each mod page shows its description, screenshots, download count, supported versions and the install button. The My Library screen lets you view, update or remove installed mods, and new installs go into a queue.

The studio promised there will be no paid mods in the browser. The plan is to later let players support creators directly and receive exclusive cosmetics made by the official Hytale team in return.

Hotfixes improved world mods. A world mod now creates as many worlds as you want, each a separate copy, and removing the mod keeps the worlds you already made. The game asks for confirmation first, because a world can carry mods that run code on your computer.

Playing with friends

Players who couldn't join a friend's world because of their NAT type no longer need Tailscale or a VPN. The game uses ICE with STUN and TURN. When no direct path exists, the connection goes through a relay server, which only works over UDP. "Double NAT" still doesn't connect, and the team is aiming for a fix in the Update 7 pre-releases.

Joining a friend's world is much faster, connections get more time to complete and opening a world is more reliable on Wi-Fi and on computers with VPN software installed. If automatic port forwarding isn't available, a message warns that players outside your network may not be able to join. In Settings, the experimental "Host With Quiche Transport" option switches the network transport for hosted worlds, and "Use Legacy Network Stack" was removed.

Entity collision

Players can now stand on entities with hard collision and get pushed by them smoothly. Creators can build moving walls and platforms to ferry friends around or trap trespassers. With the Entity Tool, the "Hard - Rotated" option gives an entity a rotated collision box that players can even slide down. For now, the change only affects players. Other NPCs and entities will come later. According to the studio, some blocks in the showcase come from Chapter 1.

Building tools

  • Color Tool: found in the Specialized Tools section of the Creative Inventory. Coloring mode swaps the blocks you aim at while keeping shape variants, Gradient paints smooth transitions and Shading applies lighter and darker tones based on a light source, surface angle or custom palette.
  • Palette presets: a side tab in Quick Settings saves and loads block palettes. The Palette Editor creates, saves, deletes and restores palettes, with a color picker and a block eyedropper.
  • Giant builds: selections of up to 16 million blocks, saved in a compact format. Copying, cutting, pasting and saving are much faster.
  • Point Tool: places named, tagged points that work as teleport targets, persist in the world and can be saved inside prefabs.

Creatures

  • Scaraks such as the Scarak Fighter and the Scarak Defender are now immune to poison and no longer take damage from environmental hazards such as brambles.
  • Every Zombie variant generates Memories again, and dungeon variants give the right Memories.
  • The Hedera no longer shows red eyes and mouth.
  • The Goat, Mouflon and Turkey got their own sounds.
  • Tamed Mosshorn are drawn to the Feed Bag.
  • The Trork Hunter without its Hunting Wolf no longer gets stuck in a combat loop.
  • Tamed animals no longer despawn when many of the same kind are nearby.
  • Flying and swimming creatures appear more often where you'd expect them.
  • Several NPCs got new death visual effects.
  • Opening chests and using objects alerts nearby creatures again, and neutral creatures react when damaged.
  • Ranged NPCs attack even when their target is close, and their shots no longer drift to one side.
  • Charging NPCs no longer stop abruptly when blocks are placed and no longer climb blocks that aren't there.
  • Invulnerable creatures no longer take knockback from projectiles, and enemies stopped attacking encounter managers.
  • Movement of other players and NPCs is smoother across frame rates and on unstable connections.
  • NPCs no longer lose active effects or fully heal when the world reloads.
  • Merchants no longer accept a single item when they asked for several.
  • The Moose Bull once again notices players the moment they join. The studio says it is "investigating solutions".

Items and equipment

Combat

  • Stamina starts refilling half a second sooner after guarding with a weapon or shield.
  • The first two hits of the dagger combo, with weapons such as the Iron Daggers, deal slightly more damage.
  • Melee attacks no longer connect through walls or beyond normal reach. Doors and statues get hit where you aimed.
  • Charged sword thrusts land more consistently at max range.
  • Multiple resistances stack properly again, and fractional resistance values are no longer rounded down.
  • Traps and other deployables deal their configured damage type instead of always Physical.
  • Sliding no longer costs stamina, and attacking with a mace such as the Iron Mace no longer instantly restarts stamina regeneration.
  • Arrows can no longer be used to guard, and stuck projectiles fall when their supporting block breaks.

Items and recipes

  • A new Bastion cape, unlocked by enabling two-factor authentication on your Hytale account.
  • The Ancient Gateway has a button to close the active portal. A failsafe stops it from working while someone is still on the other side. If the destination has no valid spawn point, the device shows an error and refunds the key.
  • You can hold a torch in the off-hand while carrying any seed.
  • Thrown Poop no longer deals damage.
  • The Gold Brick crafts instantly instead of taking around 10 seconds.
  • Every gravel type and its half slab is crafted at the Farmer's Workbench. Gravel needs Stone Rubble, Shale Gravel needs Shale Rubble and Marble Gravel needs Marble Rubble, instead of any rubble.
  • At the Builder's Workbench, Dirt turns into two Dirt - Half Slab blocks.
  • Fully upgraded workbenches apply their crafting speed bonus again, and "Craft All" in Creative crafts as much as your inventory holds.
  • Several duplication exploits were closed, including canceling a crossbow animation to keep ammo and using an item as an ingredient to copy itself.
  • The Storm Thistle no longer drops the wrong items when harvested.
  • Larger soft blocks such as the Spider Cocoon can be shot down.
  • Deployables meant for the ground no longer cling to the ceiling.
  • Chests combined while open no longer vanish.
  • The Kweebec Plushie got sounds when hit, broken or used.
  • Texture bleeding on the Mithril Cuirass was fixed.
  • Item descriptions with dynamic values show the real numbers instead of blank text.

World and blocks

World generation

  • New mage towers and revamped landmarks in the Sandstone, Shale and Volcanic biomes.
  • Terrain and foliage colors now fade gradually across biome borders.
  • A new tracking system tags areas as "Near Home" or "Wilderness" based on player beds.
  • Grass in flat worlds is darker, and accidentally mirrored terrain was fixed.
  • Multi-blocks such as doors, roofs and furniture spawn with the right rotation in World-Gen V2.
  • Lava meeting water no longer wipes solid terrain, and prefab fluids keep their defined depth.
  • Cubic worlds support breaking and placing blocks, fluids and farming tools outside the old range.

Blocks, farming and nature

  • New Full Leafy Soil, a fuller take on Leafy Soil.
  • Grass no longer spreads underwater, and grass that was already submerged slowly turns back into dirt.
  • Arid Palm Leaves and Giant Palm Leaves drop 1 to 5 Palm Tree Log.
  • Eternal Corn yields 2 to 4 per harvest, and newly planted Aubergine and Lettuce return their seeds.
  • Tilled soil turns back into Dirt after harvest, and crops keep their growth stage when the world reloads.
  • Saplings no longer get stuck for lack of root space.
  • Dozens of flowers grew to their intended size, floating water plants no longer sink and dead coral such as the Dead Coral Bush lost its solid collision.
  • Livestock produce properly again, and coop spaces free up when the occupant despawns.
  • Breaking Mossy Stone Rubble drops the block itself, which now works in recipes.
  • The Large Kweebec Chest counts as wood, so a hatchet is the most effective tool.
  • A new Green Build Lightsource in the Creative Inventory.
  • Snowy Fir Leaves rotate like other fir leaves, and the Feed Bag turns to face you when placed.
  • Block spawners can be configured in the world through a panel, with their own permission. The new "Resolve Block Spawners" world setting controls whether they turn into their target blocks, such as chests.
  • Loopholes to bypass placement restrictions were closed. In worlds with placement disabled, slabs don't stack and buckets can't place fluids.

Portals, sleep and teleporting

  • Portal worlds no longer close when you walk away from the Ancient Gateway. They stay open, and the portal reconnects when you come back.
  • Time left in a portal world can no longer exceed its starting amount, and portals opened with the Fragment: Orbis - Dragonspire Weald appear faster.
  • The Teleporter no longer resets its cooldown too early, works with capital letters in warp names and no longer renames warps when you open its settings.
  • Dying near a Teleporter no longer teleports you, and world map teleports no longer leave your character facing north.
  • Sleeping through the night gets you out of bed. Waking up no longer blocks future sleep, the "Zzz" screen no longer sticks and the night isn't skipped if someone is woken up.

Visuals

  • Stars that, according to astronomers quoted by the studio, "had begun to break" were fixed.
  • A hole in the Forgotten Temple that trapped players was closed.
  • Art fixes: the Hardwood - Fence texture, the Green Mushroom Mycelium model, the Small Tavern Chest clipping through geometry, the Raw Clay Brick - Half Slab texture and the Stone Brick - Ornate icon.
  • Z-fighting is gone on the Redwood - Fence, the Farmer's Workbench, the Blacksmith's Anvil and the Crude Hoe. Redwood fences also got more consistent variants.
  • The Wind Temple Window lost the gaps in its model, the Crude Table preview renders correctly and the Greenwood - Fence is held at a more comfortable angle.

Interface and quality of life

  • Search in Settings, across every tab, and on the controls screen.
  • Connection error screens explain the problem instead of showing only a code. Kicks show the reason sent by the server, and singleplayer worlds that fail to start show the real error.
  • Share codes work again on routers such as the BT Smart Hub.
  • A slightly fast system clock no longer blocks login. When the clock does stop you, the game shows how far off it is and asks you to sync.
  • A corrupt Settings.json is restored from its backup instead of crashing the client.
  • Server Discovery supports custom server images and controller navigation. Servers with more than four tags show the first three plus an overflow indicator. The "EU" region is now "Europe".
  • Discord friend lists with more than 500 people load fully.
  • The in-game plugin list has a search bar.
  • In Creative, items dropped into the library are deleted, and right-clicking drops one item at a time from a stack.
  • The prefab browser remembers your last folder and search.
  • The view distance setting shows the server's configured maximum. The "Environment" Quick Settings category is now "Personal Environment".
  • World creation has a bigger Customize button and a "Create World" button on the settings screen.
  • On the map, lava looks opaque, water over lava blends correctly and the faint dark dots are gone.
  • A new water quality option, which "has no impact on the purity of water", the studio joked. The particle quality option was removed because it didn't change anything.
  • Bug reports accept video.
  • More gamepad models are recognized, filter and search panels work with a controller and keybinds follow your keyboard layout.
  • Emotes no longer override server camera locks.

Performance and stability

  • World generation is considerably faster. World-Gen V2 prioritizes nearby chunks and spends less CPU on distant ones, and more chunks load per frame.
  • On Apple Silicon Macs, world generation uses fewer cores to protect the frame rate.
  • Frame pacing is steadier, with less stutter, especially at capped frame rates.
  • Packet decoding is 2 to 4 times faster on client and server.
  • Downloaded assets stay cached between sessions, and the on-disk cache no longer grows without limit.
  • Worlds with lots of open sky keep far less lighting data at high view distances.
  • Servers with many Trigger Volumes run better, and moving volumes no longer drops the tick rate.
  • Dedicated servers recover on their own when the main world crashes. Before, a crash kicked everyone until someone restarted.
  • World data is no longer lost if the server shuts down mid-save, and worlds load again after a mod is removed.
  • A rare pathfinding bug, where a creature's route was so troublesome that "time itself would come to a standstill", was fixed.
  • A new transparency algorithm improves glass over water and overlapping surfaces on Windows and Linux. Shadows no longer leak through blocks.
  • The launcher now uses Java Temurin JRE 26.0.2+10. Dedicated servers running Shenandoah GC should update to that version.

Notable fixes

Hotfixes ran from 0.6.1 on launch day to 0.6.6 on September 14.

  • 0.6.1 (Aug 27): world mods show up in your list through the CREATE WORLD button, and each mod creates as many worlds as you like. Connections prefer a direct path over adapters such as Hamachi, Radmin and Teredo, which improves latency. The Online Play screen explains in plain words whether friends can join and got a Copy Network Report button. A launch crash on NVIDIA GeForce 400 to 700 series cards was fixed.
  • 0.6.2 (Aug 27): Memories from your last session are no longer lost on exit. Worlds with a view radius above 32 in config.json start again, capped at 32 chunks. Installing a mod already sitting in the Mods folder works.
  • 0.6.3 (Aug 31): NPCs cast shadows again, and transparent parts of textures no longer cast them.
  • 0.6.4 (Sep 7): automatic port forwarding is more reliable, and two PCs on the same network no longer remove each other's forwards. Entities with block models saved in Update 5 keep their position and scale when upgraded. Ambient loops stop when you leave a world.
  • 0.6.5 (Sep 10): memory leaks when joining worlds and switching instances were closed. Bans now also apply to the asset editor. Lifting a block no longer triggers explosions.
  • 0.6.6 (Sep 14): a random crash on Intel integrated graphics was fixed. Leaving or canceling a join no longer freezes the game, and retrying sets up port forwarding again.

For servers and modders

Server

  • The network protocol moved from hytale/2 to hytale/3. Servers and plugins need a rebuild.
  • Defaults.HardcoreMode accepts None, PerPlayer or Global, and existing Hardcore worlds migrate to PerPlayer. The PlayerLives component can be read and written by any mode or plugin.
  • /spectate has toggle, watch, target, free and exit options, with its own permissions. Underneath sits a data-driven GameModeType layer, and DeathConfig.GameModeTypeOnDeath sends dying players into the chosen mode.
  • The CrashRecovery policy, per server or per world, accepts None, Reload or Shutdown. Reload uses MaxAttempts, RetryDelaySeconds and a Fallback.
  • The whitelist is now the hytale.server.join permission, and an old whitelist.json becomes whitelist.json.migrated. Plugins can replace ban handling with BanProvider or BanStorageProvider.
  • /maxviewradius sets the server's max view radius from 1 to 512 chunks. The default is 32.
  • The server console has tab-completion, and /help prints a formatted command list.
  • Changing another player's model, giving armor to another player and editing another player's recipes got their own permissions.
  • The server stops if a core plugin fails to load instead of running on broken.
  • A flaw that let a privileged user write files outside the pack folder was fixed.

Trigger Volume

  • Always-active rules: Creative Placement, Damage Multiplier, Fly, No Block Tick, No Build, No Destroy, No Door Open, No Harvest, No Heal and No Use. The Modify Always Active Rules effect toggles them.
  • New events: On Block Used, On Entity Died, On Volume Create and On Signal Received. On Block Broken also reacts to blocks destroyed by the environment, such as fire.
  • New conditions: Block Used and Entity Count. Combined with On Entity Died, Entity Count builds encounters where every enemy must fall before you move on.
  • New effects: Spawn NPC, Play Animation, Time, Cancel VFX and Remove / Kill Entities. Teleport can send players to another world, and Place Block rotates blocks on all three axes.
  • /triggervolume tag set, tag remove, tp and rename commands.

Commands and tools

  • /floodfill, /locate dungeon, /prefabpreview, /worldgen2 concurrency, /blockanimspeed, /tpcinematic (with FLYOVER, ARC, ORBIT and DOLLY paths), /worldevent, /encounter add and /fragment toggleui and toggletimer for fragment worlds.
  • Builder tools work outside Creative with SurvivalAllowed: true and the tool's permission.
  • Prefabs of 300,000 blocks or more are saved in the binary .lpf format.

Content

  • A World Events system for multi-stage events across the world, and Encounter Managers that script fights with several NPCs and control the participants' music.
  • Zoom on any item through the interaction system, with test items such as the Test Sniper Zoom, the Test Rifle Zoom and the Test Bow Zoom.
  • Armor can change speed, jump height and air control through MovementSettings.
  • Items can become music tracks, and blocks can play music from their own position.
  • FirstSpawnItems gives items on a player's first spawn.
  • Blocks run interactions when broken through OnBreak, and map markers can stay hidden until discovered.
  • Zombies now use Template_Predator. The Foog Cheese was removed.

API

  • BlockAccessor is gone, and everything moved to WorldChunk. BlockOperations.setBlock is the future replacement for WorldChunk.setBlock, which may misbehave in cubic worlds.
  • NPC support objects became ECS components. Custom sensors, actions and motions need updating.
  • A camera API with CameraSequenceBuilder and CinematicTeleport.play.
  • A voice API with openEntityVoice, openPositionalVoice and openDirectVoice, using 48 kHz mono Opus audio, plus PlayerVoiceInterceptor to intercept player voice.
  • A built-in Points plugin, with PointManager and PointSpatialIndex.
  • Plugin permissions with spaces in the name now use underscores, and the ClientPlaceBlock packet was removed.
  • Hytale.Protocol can be built from the shared-source repository with the .NET 10 SDK.
  • Documentation moved to docs.hytale.com, with the pre-release version at pre-release.docs.hytale.com.
