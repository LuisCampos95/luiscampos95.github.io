Hytale's game modes: Exploration, Creative, Hardcore and what is still coming

Exploration, Creative, Hardcore and spectator: what changes in each of Hytale's modes, using the official notes and the game files.

Hytale launched into Early Access with two modes: Exploration and Creative, both with multiplayer. In August, Update 6 added Hardcore as a world option, together with spectator mode. The story-driven Adventure Mode and official minigames have not arrived yet. This guide explains what changes in each one, using the official patch notes and the pre-release files from 17 September 2026.

First: Adventure, Exploration and the name in the code

This is where most of the confusion lives. The survival mode you play today is called Exploration in the menu and in the studio's posts. Under the hood, though, it runs on the mode called Adventure. In the protocol files, the game only knows two base modes: Adventure and Creative. That is why the notes sometimes say "Adventure mode" when they talk about Exploration rules, such as dropping items or teleporting from the map.

The Adventure Mode promised since 2018, with story, deeper progression and dungeons with bosses, is something else, and it does not exist yet as a separate mode. Part of it is arriving through the chapters.

Beyond those two, the Entity/GameMode folder in the files holds five data-driven mode types: Adventure, Creative, Spectator, HardcoreSpectator and SpectatorInteractive. Even switching between them has its own flair: entering Creative plays the "Avatar Powers Enable" sound and flashes a quick pink and cyan glow on your character, and going back to Adventure plays the powers-off sound.

Exploration

This is Early Access's main mode. The launch post defines it as an exploration-focused experience built with the content and systems that exist today. The world is infinite and procedurally generated, with biomes, ancient structures, friendly creatures and dangerous monsters. You explore, build a home, grow crops, craft items and gear and play with friends. The studio itself warns that balance will not always feel right and that some systems are unfinished.

What there is to do

  • Forgotten Temple: where the first hints of the Cursebreaker arc show up. In Chapter 1 it becomes the story's starting point.
  • Mantling: your character grabs ledges and climbs, which gives more freedom to move around the world.
  • Unified progression: expandable inventories, upgradable workbenches that pull from nearby storage, and builder stations with hundreds of blocks, furniture and decorations.
  • Fragments of Orbis: the Ancient Gateway is crafted and placed at your base and takes you to temporary, unstable worlds. Each fragment is opened with a key, such as the Fragment: Orbis - Dragonspire Weald.
  • Memories: you collect Memories by exploring and interacting with creatures and factions, and bring them to The Heart of Orbis. The rewards are permanent and apply to everyone in the world.
  • Combat and farming: weapons with their own abilities, and crops that become food and potions.

The default rules, according to the files

The GameplayConfigs/Default.json file holds Exploration's rules. A few numbers that help explain the game:

  • Death: the config describes losing 50% of the amount of carried items and 10% durability.
  • Items on the ground: they disappear after 600 seconds, or 10 minutes.
  • Day and night: the day lasts 1,728 seconds and the night 1,152. A full cycle takes 48 minutes.
  • Respawn points: up to 3 per player, within a 500-block radius.
  • Memories per level: 10, 25, 50, 100 and 200.

Inside the Fragments, the rules change. The Portal.json file removes item loss on death, and dying takes you out of the instance. The Void event escalates in stages: at 420 seconds the weather shifts and invasion portals start to appear, and at 540 seconds the weather gets even more intense.

How the mode changed across updates

  • Update 1: dinosaurs in the underground jungles and a cheaper Leather Backpack, made at the Workbench.
  • Update 3: animal taming and map markers.
  • Update 5: controller support, the social sidebar and Server Discovery.
  • Update 7: the first wave of Chapter 1, with goblins, runes and gadgets. In the pre-release, that content is mostly reached through Creative.

For your first steps in Exploration, the first steps guide covers everything from the first stick to the first night.

Creative

Creative gives you unlimited blocks, flight and world-editing tools. In November 2025, the mode's engineer, Ktar, showed the toolset that shipped at launch:

  • Scripted brushes: asset-defined brushes that create mountains, grassy paths and procedurally generated ruins. They can be edited in the Node Editor.
  • Quick settings: flight speed, inertia and options that change with the item in your hand.
  • Model changer: more than 100 models to use as your character or spawn in the world. In the video, Ktar turned into a Corgi.
  • Prefabs: a browser, an editor inside an empty world and the Paste Tool, which places structures precisely.
  • Machinima: keyframed cameras to record scenes.
  • Shape brush, selection, ruler and laser pointer: to sculpt, copy, move and point things out to friends.

The hub and Creative's rules

The old Creative Hub became Crossroads in Update 3, with a button to return to it from the creative worlds map. Since Update 2, the hub portal creates flat worlds with WorldGen V2, and the files include the Flat Worldgen Portal for that. In the hub itself, the CreativeHub.json config blocks breaking, gathering and placing blocks.

Some rules only apply in Creative. Ammo is not consumed (Update 4). Holding the drop button and hovering over the inventory drops whole stacks in Exploration, but in Creative the same gesture destroys the items (Update 4). Several block sets, such as the sci-fi Cybercity blocks, live only in the Creative inventory.

What arrived later

  • Update 5: Trigger Volume, new placement modes and 3D prefab previews.
  • Update 6: the Color Tool, block palettes, selections of up to 16 million blocks and the Point Tool.
  • Update 7: a creative inventory search that tolerates typos and ignores accents, and an option to walk through your own triggers without activating them.

Hypixel Studios said it also intends to build a more casual creative experience, separate from Adventure and Exploration, focused on playful interactions rather than an editor feel. There is no date yet.

Hardcore

Hardcore arrived in Update 6 on 27 August 2026. It can only be enabled when creating a new world, under the Customize button, and cannot be changed later. There are five options:

  • Off: a normal world.
  • Permadeath: one life per player. Death is permanent.
  • Three Strikes: three lives per player.
  • Nine Lives: nine lives shared by the whole group. On the ninth death, it is over for everyone.
  • Soulbound: everyone's fate is linked. If anyone dies, the run ends.

In Three Strikes and Nine Lives, the number of lives can be adjusted when creating the world, but zero is not allowed. Remaining lives show above the health bar.

Whoever dies drops everything they carry and becomes a spectator. According to the studio, the first version is pure permadeath, with no difficulty changes. Hypixel Studios said it plans Hardcore challenge modes that go further.

Spectator

Spectators are invisible, intangible and ignored by NPCs, so a dead friend never pulls monsters onto the group. They do not count toward events that need a number of players, and they do not keep dungeons open.

The files show how it is built. The Spectator mode turns on flight, no-clip and invulnerability, blocks the inventory and emotes and uses its own voice channel, called Spectator. HardcoreSpectator, used after dying in Hardcore, maps the primary and secondary buttons to switching players and the first ability to freeing the camera. SpectatorInteractive keeps the hotbar and the reticle.

Spectating also exists outside Hardcore, as a server feature. The /spectate command has the toggle, watch, target, free and exit options, each with its own permission. The studio noted that the system also prepares the ground for official minigames.

Multiplayer and servers

Every mode has multiplayer. Even alone, you play on a local server of your own. That is why world rules and server rules are the same thing in Hytale.

  • With friends: the social sidebar arrived in Update 5. In Update 6 the game started using STUN and TURN, and players with NAT problems no longer needed a VPN.
  • Rules apply instantly: in Update 7, server rule changes take effect without leaving and rejoining. That includes health bars, combat text and block placing and breaking rules.
  • Portals inherit rules: also in Update 7, worlds opened through the Ancient Gateway follow the rules of the world they came from, such as fall damage and PvP.
  • Hardcore on servers: Defaults.HardcoreMode accepts None, PerPlayer or Global.

How to set up a server, install mods and use the official tools is covered in mods, servers and tools.

What has not arrived yet

  • Adventure Mode: story, deeper progression and dungeons with bosses. The pieces are arriving through the chapters, inside Exploration.
  • Official minigames: Hypixel Studios showed 14 party micro-games in July, with a dedicated tab planned. No date yet.
  • Casual Creative: the lighter creative experience, without the editor feel, is still being planned.
