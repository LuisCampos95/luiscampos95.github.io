Those who refused the flame

One line on the Essence of Ice says the Outlanders were once part of another people. The split, the war between brothers and the irony of who survived it.

The Outlanders are Zone 3's masked people, with green fires, priests and hunting wolves. Nothing about them suggests they are anyone's family. But one line tucked into a crafting item says otherwise: before the ice, they were part of another people, and they left on bad terms.

What the game says is in quotes. Our own reading is marked Theory:.

The line that changes everything

Essence of Ice

"When the Outlanders rejected the flame and split from their brethren, they turned instead toward the colder regions and the fragile strength found in the advancing sheets of ice."

*Brethren*. Not an ally who turned their back, but people from the same house.

Two details stand out. They didn't reject "fire", they rejected the flame, with an article, the way you'd refuse a faith or an oath. And whoever wrote it calls the ice a "fragile strength". This text is told from the side that kept the flame, and it doesn't hide the contempt.

Who kept the flame

The game never names the brethren. But only one people in Orbis carries fire in its name: the Enkindled. The Portuguese localization went the same way and calls their metal blocks "Burning Iron", like the Enkindled Iron. And an Enkindled journal from the pre-release mentions a Rite of Enkindling, a ceremony that seems to set someone alight. It has its own article: The rite that set people alight.

Theory: "the flame" the Outlanders refused was that rite, or everything that came with it. Being Enkindled meant accepting the flame. Those who said no became Outlanders, literally "the ones outside".

The cold wasn't empty

The Outlanders built a whole identity as the opposite of fire. Even their metal got a legend: according to the Cobalt Ingot, cobalt is said to be cold to the touch even when molten.

But the cold already had an owner. The Statue of The Watcher says the whispers weren't why they moved, but they were already there when they arrived. And Shadoweave Scraps was devised "prior to their first communion with the Void". Their story has a before and an after, and the after has the Void in it. The rest of Outlander culture is in Hytale's factions.

The strangest detail comes from 2019. Hypixel said that at night the fires of the most devout Outlanders burn green. The people who refused the flame never stopped lighting fires. They just changed the color.

From brothers to enemies

The split didn't stay a family quarrel. The Old Enkindled Journal: The Rite was written by someone left on watch while almost everyone was away "to hold the line against the Outlanders". The roads hadn't been safe "since the war", and few were left.

On the other side, the Revenant Guardian Memory says the Guardians helped win the Great War.

Theory: the Great War was the war between the brethren. The Enkindled won, with the Guardians on the front line, and the Outlanders stayed in the ice for good. Zone 4's soundtrack has a track called "Lost Brethren", the same word the essence uses.

The losers are the ones still here

Here's the irony. If the theory is right, the Outlanders lost the war. And today they are the ones with villages, priests and camps. All that's left of the Enkindled is burnt journals, a sigil and undead Guardians. The flame one side accepted looks like the same one that scoured Crucible.

Theory: refusing the flame saved the Outlanders. When the catastrophe came, they were already far away, in the cold nobody wanted.

But maybe they didn't escape anything. The Outlander Priest and the Outlander Sorcerer drop Essence of the Void. The Outlanders ran from one power and fell into the arms of another.

Open questions

  • Was the flame they refused the rite, a faith or a technology?
  • Was the Great War really between the Enkindled and the Outlanders, or were the Outlanders just one front?
  • Are the green fires another flame, or is that the Void burning?
  • If the Outlanders remember where they came from, why does none of their lore mention the brethren?
