Purple sky, Enkindled journals and goblin paintings: the hidden lore of Part 4

Seven texts and six paintings landed in the 09/24 pre-release without showing up in the official notes. The Enkindled war, magic versus technology, goblins who hate the purple sky and what it may hint at.

Update 7 Part 4 brought seven lore texts and six goblin paintings that the official notes never mention. That was not an oversight: the studio said a good part of what arrived on 09/24 was left out of the notes on purpose, to be found while playing. The texts sit in the pre-release files, and the wiki has already catalogued each one.

Everything is below, text by text. What the game says appears as a quote. What we are guessing is marked as Theory:.

The Enkindled journals

Four texts were written by someone from the Enkindled. The name was already in the files: the Enkindled Sigil speaks of a civilization lost to a calamity that left Orbis scarred, and the Fragment: Orbis - The Guttering Hollows Fragment sits in the ruins of a lost Enkindled settlement. So these journals come from before the fall.

Old Enkindled Journal: The Rite

"Maybe I should have gone with them. The roads haven't been safe since the war. But someone has to stay to maintain the watch and there are so few of us left. With everyone gone to hold the line against the Outlanders, who can say when the Trorks will grow bold enough to strike. Ah, how I wanted to be there to witness her Rite of Enkindling."

The Enkindled were at war and sent nearly everyone to hold the line against the Outlanders. Whoever stayed on watch feared a different enemy, the Trorks. The text also mentions the Rite of Enkindling, a ceremony that seems to give the people their name.

Old Enkindled Journal: Vermin

"I hear them down there. Every. Day. The clanking doesn't stop. What are they? Rats? They have to be bigger than that. I hope it's not spiders. The Guardians were meant to go down there but they've been in disarray since a messenger arrived from Crucible yesterday. Something about a Councilor and the Scaraks - I didn't quite hear it. I just hope they do something about it soon."

Someone hears metal clanking underground every single day. The Guardians, who were supposed to go down and check, have been in disarray since a messenger arrived from Crucible, a name the text never explains. The message was about a Councilor and the Scaraks, another people mentioned only by name.

Enkindled Trade Ledger

"A short note is scrawled in the margins of this ledger. Take heed: never again are you to offer the Kweebecs cut wood. I spent years earning their trust, and I'll not watch you throw it away over a woodpile."

The Enkindled traded with the Kweebecs, and earning their trust took years. Offering cut wood to a people who live in the trees nearly ruined it.

Enkindled Letter: Workshops of War

"Only a small scrap of this charred letter survives....they said we shouldn't build it at all. We should focus on magic. Not technology. Magic is pure. Add mechanism to magic and the magic is tainted. So once said a wise old sage. Those simple words have given us trouble for so long. Yet now they say we should build it for war. I don't..."

This is the key text of the group. The Enkindled had an old rule: magic and technology do not mix, because mechanism taints magic. The war changed that, and someone started pushing for "it" to be built. The letter burned before saying what it was.

Who is the Makinomagistra

In the files. Makinomagistra is not a name, it is a title. The Goblin Makinomagistra Memory explains that only goblins whose obsession with technology surpasses even that of the stigmatized Enkindled engineers take it. The current holder is a goblin woman, fascinated by the derelict Ancient Gateways in the oldest ruins of Orbis. She was the first goblin to escape Orbis through her own inventions, and the text calls her a Duke. The Memory ends with a warning: perhaps her critical failure lies in the origins of that fascination.

Theory: "Makino" suggests machine and "magistra" is Latin for a female master. Something like "Mistress of Machines". The Goblin Painting: The Veil of Void painting, which says "a Makinomagistra sees work through", would be about her.

The goblins

The other three texts are goblin, and the tone shifts a lot.

Goblin Commission: Abandoned Parts

"It's in the Deepworks. Beyond the great bridge. It's heavy. Big. Not the whole thing - just the core. Break it if you have to. Might be easier to build it again from the scraps."

It is a job order. Someone wants the core of a big machine left in the Deepworks, past the great bridge. Whoever wrote it does not mind if it breaks on the way, since it can be rebuilt from scrap.

Feastmaster's Menu of the Day

"First: Soup of iron shavings Second: Roasted cow meat with potatoes Second: Round disk painted red with wood and strange leaves (new recipe: recommended by Trorks!!) Third: Sock, aged well and baked into pie"

The menu belongs to the Goblin Feastmaster, the biggest goblin in Chapter 1 and a dungeon boss in Part 4. Iron soup, beef with potatoes and, for dessert, an aged sock baked into a pie.

Theory: the round disk painted red, with wood and strange leaves, recommended by the Trorks, is almost certainly the goblin take on a pizza.

Why Purple?

"Why the purple sky? Stories say it was red or blue before. Some of the plants are starting to turn purple too. And the grass stops looking like grass. We don't like purple. Purple feels wrong. At least the flames make it black. Black is much better."

This is the one that stands out most. The goblins keep stories of a red or blue sky, watch the plants turn purple and hate every bit of it. Their answer is fire: flames turn everything black, and black is better.

The paintings tell a story

There are six paintings, each with a short caption. The game does not say which order to read them in. This is the order that makes the most sense to us:

  • Goblin Painting: The Breaches: "The other goblins come too. Not all of them friendly. There are new things here. It's safer here. But the parts we need are over there."
  • Goblin Painting: The Failed Excursions: "Too many failed attempts. The purple barrier repels all. There has to be a way. It can't block us in forever."
  • Goblin Painting: The Strange Place: "There are Kweebecs here but this isn't Orbis. Similar, but not the same. The sky isn't purple either."
  • Goblin Painting: The Veil of Void: "Everything is wrong. Sky is too purple. Shouldn't stay here. A Makinomagistra sees work through."
  • Goblin Painting: The Required Repair: "The gate doesn't work anymore. There's no way to go onward. The parts are on the other side. Back beneath the purple sky."
  • Goblin Painting: The Traveling Goblin: "Works!"

Read this way, the captions become a journey: goblins cross the breaches, hit a purple barrier, reach a place with Kweebecs that is not Orbis, have to go back for parts and, in the end, get the gate working.

Three details from the files help read the paintings:

  • The The Goblin Gateway "breaches the Veil of Void that seals Orbis". The Veil is more than a painting title.
  • The Ancient Gateway mentions a curse that keeps Gaia's Orbis locked away, and ways to pierce the Void that veils it.
  • Makinomagistra, named in the Veil painting, is the name of the Goblin Makinomagistra, who became an NPC in this same pre-release.

What it could mean

From here on it is our reading. None of this has been confirmed by the studio.

Theory: the purple sky is the Veil of Void seen from the inside. Why Purple? says the sky used to be another color, and the Veil painting (named Goblin_Painting_Orbis in the files) shows a sky that is "too purple". If the Veil seals Orbis, it makes sense that it came with the calamity that ended the Enkindled, and that the purple plants are the Void spreading across the ground.

Theory: the goblins are stuck outside Orbis and trying to get back. The Strange Place painting has Kweebecs and a sky with no purple, and its file name is Goblin_Painting_ForgottenTemple. The Forgotten Temple is exactly where the official notes tell players to start Part 4, and where "something is happening": the bed stopped working. If the goblins call that place "not Orbis", the world the player walks in may be a Fragment rather than the real Orbis.

Theory: the goblin lab is the gate repair. The Required Repair painting says the gate no longer works and the parts are on the other side. The Goblin Breach Console adds time with every repair, and the Deepworks job order asks for the core of a machine. Whoever fetches those parts from the purple side is, by all signs, the player, on the Makinomagistra's orders.

Theory: goblins live off the technology the Enkindled were afraid to use. The Workshops of War letter talks about building "it" for war, and the Guttering Hollows sit in the ruins of an Enkindled settlement. Tinkering goblins living off scrap in the ruins of a people who swapped magic for mechanism would close that story neatly.

Theory: the Guardians in the journal are today's undead. The Revenant Guardian, the Revenant Guardian, keeps the Enkindled Sigil "close to where their hearts once sat". The Guardians who never went down to check the noise may have become those skeletons.

For everything else in Part 4, read Update 7, Part 4. The goblin models that are still not an item or NPC are in What the Goblin Scientist and the hidden art reveal.
