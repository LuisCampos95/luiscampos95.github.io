Dungeons and portals: a guide to the Fragments of Orbis

Ancient Gateway recipe, the five keys, each Fragment timer, the Void Breach, the Forgotten Temple and the Chapter 1 Goblin Breach.

Today, "dungeon" in Hytale means two things. The first is the Fragments of Orbis, pieces of the world you visit through a portal that close after a few minutes. The second arrives with Chapter 1: the Forgotten Temple and the Goblin Breach, which leads to the goblin world. This guide covers both, with recipes, timers and what is inside.

Recipes, timers and creatures come from the pre-release files cataloged by the wiki. What the studio said on hytale.com is marked as official. The cover shows the inside of the Forgotten Temple. Image: Hypixel Studios (hytale.com).

How to open a Fragment

It all starts with the Ancient Gateway. You craft it at the Arcanist's Workbench with:

  • 30 Cobblestone
  • 10 Thorium Ingot
  • 10 Essence of Life
  • 10 Essence of the Void

Place the gateway in your base, hold a Fragment key and use the gateway. The key is consumed. The item description sums it up: the curse keeps Gaia's Orbis locked away, but there are ways to pierce the Void that veils it. To come back, use the Return Portal on the other side.

What changed in recent updates:

  • Update 6: the gateway got a button to close the active Fragment, locked while someone is still on the other side. If the destination has no valid spawn point, the key comes back to you.
  • Update 6: the Fragment no longer closes when you walk away from the gateway.
  • Update 7: each portal can cap how many times each player enters. Past the cap, you get a notice that you can no longer join that Fragment.
  • Update 7: Fragments can stay open with no time limit, and they follow the source world's rules, such as fall damage and PvP.

The rules inside a Fragment

In the files. The portal gameplay config sets:

  • Dying inside a Fragment does not cost you items. You respawn outside, in your world.
  • The map does not show the spawn point.
  • Veilbound items cannot leave the Fragment. The name arrived in the 09/17 pre-release, replacing "Cursed".

The Void Breach. In Fragments with the invasion switched on, the Void steps in at the 7 minute mark. The sky darkens, a track of its own plays and invasion portals appear, the Void Invasion Portal. Each one releases up to 5 Void Crawler, 1 Void Eye or up to 4 Void Larva at a time. At 9 minutes, the weather gets even heavier. In the files, only The Dread Wade has this invasion on.

The five Fragments of Orbis

Every Fragment lasts a fixed time and, by default, allows one entry per player. All of their descriptions end with the same notice: the energies inside have yet to take their final form. In other words, lore, function and looks may change.

The Dread Wade

  • Key: Fragment: Orbis - The Dread Wade, with 10 Adamantite Ingot and 30 Essence of Fire at the Arcanist's Workbench.
  • Time: 10 minutes.
  • Boss: the The Hedera, with 226 health.
  • Fauna: Frog, Raven, Marsh Snake and Crocodile.
  • In-game tips: a ravenous menace lurks at the heart of the swamp, and the murky shallows are home to dangerous fauna.
  • Void Breach: on. In Update 7, the event started escalating at fixed intervals.

Dragonspire Weald

  • Key: Fragment: Orbis - Dragonspire Weald, with 10 Cobalt Ingot and 30 Essence of Ice.
  • Time: 7 minutes.
  • In-game tip: a mythical beast sleeps there, bound in an eternal slumber.
  • Detail: in Update 4, redwood biomes started showing up in this portal's worlds. In Update 6, these portals began opening faster.

Windrider Valley

  • Key: Fragment: Orbis - Windrider Valley, with 10 Thorium Ingot and 30 Essence of Life.
  • Time: 6 minutes.
  • Lore: this is where the Feran hid in the darkest years of their history, sheltered by the canyon walls.
  • In-game tip: on occasion, a Mage Tower may be found there.
  • Fauna: Flamingo, Tortoise, Sand Lizard, Frog, Black Piranha, Black Trilobite, Parrot and Vulture. The predators are the Sabertooth Tiger and the Crocodile.
  • Chests: the loot table has copper and iron armor and weapons, gold, iron, silver and thorium bars, lesser potions, cooked food and seeds.

Silent Henges

  • Key: Fragment: Orbis - Silent Henges. The wiki lists no recipe.
  • Time: 5 minutes, the shortest.
  • Lore: the description asks the question that matters: who built the Ancient Gateways, and why?

Tal'Kesi Refuge

  • Key: Fragment: Orbis - Tal'Kesi Refuge. No recipe in the wiki either.
  • Time: 12 minutes, the longest.
  • Lore: a primordial haven thriving beneath the wastelands left by an ancient civilization.

The files also hold the name of a sixth Fragment, Howling Sands, with no portal type or key.

The Forgotten Temple

Official. The Chapter 1 journey starts inside the Forgotten Temple. The studio says the temple will serve as the hub for your progress through the Early Access chapters and will change with each release.

In the files. The temple is an instance of its own, subtitled "Edge of the Echo". You enter through the Forgotten Temple Gateway and leave through the Forgotten Temple Exit. There is an objective called "Temple Entrance" with a single task: kill the Golem. The environment is split into exterior, heart and inner halls.

The Goblin Breach

Official. Once you answer the chapter's opening call at the Forgotten Temple, the Goblin Breach event can trigger from time to time. It opens a temporary portal somewhere in your world. Look around or open the map to find it. On the other side is an unstable fragment of Orbis, taken over by goblins, with a growing Void presence.

Official. That world is regenerated on every visit, sometimes with content you have not seen before. While you are inside, the Void presses in and eventually ejects you. The goal is to explore, find good loot and hold out as long as you can. The studio lists the first activities: predators to track and trap, Void assassins that hunt you down, chests that spawn enemy waves when you go for the loot, and puzzles that lock off places you cannot just dig into.

In the files. The goblin world is called The Guttering Hollows. It has surface, cave, deep cave and Void cave environments. The Goblin Scrapper, the instance version of the weakest goblin, has 100 health instead of 20.

In the files. You can earn time inside a portal world. Void anomalies are crystals protected by a shield. Clear the creature waves around them, the shield drops, and destroying the crystal adds time to the clock:

  • Void Crystal: waves of Void Larva and Void Tentacle. Adds 2 minutes.
  • Void Crystal: waves of Void Tentacle. Adds 4 minutes.
  • Void Crystal: Void Eye on the inside and Void Tentacle on the outside. Adds 8 minutes.

Theory. The files do not say which world these anomalies appear in. Given the official idea of holding out against the Void, the goblin world is the natural candidate.

In the files. There is also a "Portal Console", with placeholder text, that extends the Breach duration by levels in exchange for goblin world materials. And rune shards, dropped by dangerous enemies in the Guttering Hollows, are used to craft runes at the goblin bench.

Dungeons still sitting in the files

This part is in the files only. Being there does not guarantee a release or a date.

  • Dungeon structure folders: cursed crypt, goblin lair, labyrinth with a boss room and a prep room, magic ruins, an Outlander temple in three stages, a rift, a sewer and challenge gates for four climates.
  • Dungeon instances ready for testing, including a goblin one, an Outlander one and a combat arena.
  • An item called "Dungeon Portal" in the game's text file.

Official. In 2019, the studio described dungeons as chambers and encounters joined by passageways, often ending in a final room with special rewards. It also showed the concept of portal dungeons, with their own rules and limits on block breaking. In the Chapter 1 blog, the stated plan is procedural dungeons with WorldGen V2, further down the line.

Quick tips

  • Carry spare Essence of the Void. The gateway needs 10 to craft.
  • For the swamp, gather Essence of Fire. For the taiga, Essence of Ice. For the valley, Essence of Life.
  • Dying inside a Fragment costs no items, but it costs the visit.
  • In a portal world, Void crystals are worth time. The biggest gives 8 minutes.
