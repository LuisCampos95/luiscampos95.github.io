Best armor for each stage of Hytale

The full set for each stage of Hytale, with physical resistance, extra health and the summed cost of all four pieces, from the game files.

The numbers below add up the four pieces of each set (helmet, chestplate, gloves and leggings): physical damage resistance, extra health and total material cost. Every set comes from the Armorer's Workbench, and metal sets from Thorium on need the bench at tier 2 or 3.

Early game: Light Leather (level 15)

The light leather set already gives 25% physical resistance and +46 health, the same as iron, for just 15 Light Leather, 7 logs and 9 Bolt of Wool. It is the best early deal. Copper, at level 10, falls behind: 18% and +25 health for 31 Copper Ingot.

  • Chestplate: Light Leather Cuirass

Mid game: Iron (level 20)

The Iron Cuirass and the other iron pieces repeat leather's 25% and +46 health, but rarity goes up to Uncommon. Set: 45 Iron Ingot, 20 light leather and 16 Linen Scraps.

The jump: Thorium and Cobalt (levels 30 and 35, bench tier 2)

Protection really climbs here: 32% physical resistance and +61 health, at Rare rarity.

  • Thorium Cuirass: the set takes 55 Thorium Ingot, 20 Medium Leather, 20 linen scraps and 10 Venom Sac. It is the only one with poison resistance.
  • Cobalt Cuirass: 62 Cobalt Ingot, 20 Heavy Leather and 20 Shadoweave Scraps.

Endgame: Adamantite and Mithril (levels 40 and 50, bench tier 3)

Both reach 40% physical resistance and +68 health, the cap for craftable armor.

  • Adamantite Cuirass: 77 Adamantite Ingot, 24 heavy leather and 20 Cindercloth Scraps.
  • Mithril Cuirass: 62 Mithril Ingot, 20 Storm Leather and 200 Essence of the Void. Same numbers as Adamantite, but Epic and level 50.
