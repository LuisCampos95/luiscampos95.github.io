Varyn, the Void and the Echo: the community's theories on Hytale's lore

Is the Echo a copy of Orbis? Did Varyn cast the curse? Community theories, with what Hypixel and in-game text support or knock down.

This is a theory: speculation based on official information, marked as such throughout the text. Nothing here is confirmed by Hypixel Studios unless a source says so.

Hypixel tells Hytale's story in pieces. There are no cutscenes and no narrator. There are item descriptions, ruined statues and place names. Joe, who looks after the lore, calls this approach archaeology and says nothing in the world exists without intent. The result was predictable: the community turned into archaeologists.

Here are theories that circulate on forums, fan wikis and videos, with what supports each one and what works against it. Same rules as always:

  • Official: said by Hypixel or written inside the game, with the source.
  • In the files: exists in the game files but was never announced.
  • Theory: the community's interpretation or ours. Nothing here is confirmed.

The official starting point

Official, from the January lore post:

  • Gaia and Varyn are still canon. Tessa and Kyros are coming back.
  • Gaia is the Avatar of Orbis, but not every faction knows it.
  • Alterverses should return as a base for long-term content.
  • The old and the redesigned Scaraks will both fit into the lore, in a way Joe chose not to spoil.
  • Entire factions were cut or changed a lot, and much of the old concept art no longer applies.
  • There is a narrative tied to the move from the V1 generator to V2. The hint Joe left was a single word: Cursebreaker.

Official: in February, Simon wrote on X that the curse is quiet for now, that this changes as the first chapter begins, and that players will face difficult choices with lasting consequences for the world and for themselves. The statement was reported by PCGamesN.

Theory 1: the Echo is not the real Orbis

The idea: the world we play in today is an echo, a trapped version of Orbis. A curse separates this echo from the real Orbis, and the Cursebreaker arc ends when that barrier falls.

What supports it:

  • Official: Hypixel calls the Early Access world the Echoes of Orbis. In-game, the Forgotten Temple carries the subtitle "Edge of the Echo".
  • Official (in-game text): the Ancient Gateway description says the curse keeps Gaia's Orbis locked away and that the Void veils it.
  • Official (in-game text): The Heart of Orbis speaks of a voice from beyond the barriers, from the original world.
  • Official: the Update 3 notes say the final chapter puts Orbis within reach, and only then does the game leave Early Access.
  • Official: in July, the community spotlight said something is brewing that will affect the Echo. Builds stay intact, but the world around them may change significantly.

What works against it:

  • No official text explains what the Echo is. It could just be the story framing for a technical generator swap.
  • V1 worlds stay playable after the swap. If the Echo were a prison to be broken, it would be odd for it to keep existing.

In our reading, this is the theory on this list with the most support in official text. What nobody knows is the "how".

Theory 2: Varyn cast the curse

The idea: Varyn, the villain from the old art, cursed Orbis and commands the Void. Fan wikis like the Hytale Wiki on Fandom describe him as the leader of the Void's creatures, based on concept art released since 2018. An older version of the theory says Gaia and Varyn fought long ago, Gaia won, and Varyn has been imprisoned until now.

What supports it:

  • Official: Varyn is still canon.
  • Official (in-game text): it is the Void that veils Gaia's Orbis, according to the Ancient Gateway.
  • Community theory: the purple of Varyn's old art is the same purple as the Void creatures in the game.

What works against it:

  • In the files: the name Varyn does not appear in any in-game text. We searched the language files of both the stable build and the pre-release. Zero hits.
  • Official: Joe warned that a lot of concept art became outdated, to the point that entire sheets were dropped.
  • No in-game text says who cast the curse.

Theory 3: the Void isn't born, it takes

The idea: the Void is not a species. It is a corruption that takes ordinary creatures and changes them.

What supports it:

  • Official (in-game text): the Voidheart says not every creature is born of the Void, and that even those taken and corrupted may carry the same heart.
  • Official (in-game text): the Essence of the Void admits nobody knows whether the creatures produce the essence or need it to exist.
  • In the files: the Voidtaken Grizzly in the pre-release and the Voidtaken Goblin art follow the same pattern: a familiar creature, in its taken version.
  • Official: in the Update 7 pre-release, goblins started fleeing Void creatures like the Void Crawler and the Void Spectre. To them, the Void is a threat, not an ally.

What works against it:

  • No text says where the Void comes from.
  • Some creatures, like the Void Fiend, look born of it rather than converted.

Theory 4: the Outlanders worship Gaia without knowing it

The community's older theory says the Outlanders serve Varyn. Wikis and an old Hypixel forum thread point to the purple banners with a diamond as his mark. A newer reading goes the other way.

What supports the Void link:

  • Official: in 2019, Hypixel introduced the Outlanders as a breakaway human faction exploring darker magic, with green campfires and priests said to have mastery over death.
  • Official (in-game text): the Shadoweave Scraps mentions the Outlanders' first communion with the Void.
  • Official (in-game text): the Statue of The Watcher says the whispers were already in the cold lands when the Outlanders arrived.

What supports the newer reading:

  • In the files: the Outlander Deity Statue has Gaia in its internal ID. In-game, it only shows up as the Outlanders' deity. The Statue of a Silent Deity, called a silent deity, follows the same pattern.
  • Official: Joe wrote that not every faction knows Gaia is the Avatar of Orbis.
  • Official (in-game text): the Essence of Ice says the Outlanders rejected the flame and split from their own people.

Theory: the Outlanders would worship Gaia under another face, while something in the cold lands pulls them toward the Void. Against it: internal IDs can be just the team's working nicknames, and the Outlander deity could be someone else.

Theory 5: Orbis lives in cycles of collapse

The idea: Orbis has had several advanced civilizations, and all of them fell to the same kind of mistake. Today's world is what was left.

What supports it, all from in-game text (official):

  • The Bone Fragments says Orbis' most advanced civilizations shared a hubris that led to their downfall.
  • The Crude Torch says that, for the last peoples of Orbis, fire was both creation and ruin.
  • The Cindercloth Scraps talks about a cursed flame that scoured the city of Crucible. It drops from burnt skeletons like the Burnt Skeleton Knight, and the description says it comes from the charred bones of a former resident. The Adamantite Ingot adds that little remains of Crucible's splendor.
  • The Statue of the Founding Builder mentions fallen Scaraks and the atrocities of the first and last Scarak Empress.
  • The Crude Sword and the Heavy Leather mention an age of heroes, when a few banded together to hunt creatures of myth.

More clues:

  • Official: in 2019, Hypixel wrote that Zone 3 hosted some of the darkest moments in Orbis' history.
  • In the files: there is a model of a broken Gaia statue with a burnt texture in the human ruins set. No block in the game uses it.

What works against it:

  • The texts point to different causes: hubris, fire, an empress. Nothing ties these collapses to the current curse.
  • Official: Joe warned that archaeology tells stories from different perspectives, which don't always share the same understanding of Orbis. An item description may be wrong on purpose.

Theory 6: the Adventurer is an Avatar from elsewhere

The idea: the player is not just any human. They are an Avatar, like Gaia, from somewhere else and bound by the rules of a domain that isn't theirs.

What supports it:

  • Official: Hypixel often calls the player character an Avatar, from the Avatar Creator to the posts about runes.
  • Official (in-game text): the Workbench says even Avatars need time to hone their craft, especially when bound by the rules of another's domain.
  • Official (in-game text): the Arcanist's Workbench says the arcane arts are the closest mere mortals ever came to Avatar powers.
  • Official (in-game text): the Harvest Trophy speaks of the favor of Orbis' Avatar. Per the January post, that Avatar is Gaia.

What works against it:

  • Avatar is also just the technical name for the player character. The coincidence may be only that.
  • No text says where the player comes from.

Theory 7: the two Scaraks are before and after

The idea: the old and the redesigned Scaraks would be the same people at two moments, before and after the fall of their empire. Another version talks about two castes.

What supports it:

  • Official: Joe confirmed both designs will be part of the lore and that there is an explanation, without saying what it is.
  • Official (in-game text): the Statue of the Founding Builder mentions fallen Scaraks, a forgotten Founding Builder and an Empress who was both the first and the last.
  • Official: the game already has a visible hierarchy, with the Scarak Broodmother and royal guards like the Scarak Imperial Guard.

What works against it:

  • Nothing official ties the empire's fall to the change in design. The castes and the before-and-after are guesses.

Theory 8: Tessa, Kyros and the Fragments

Two smaller ideas come up often.

The first says Tessa and Kyros, adventurers from the old concept art, could be heroes from the age of heroes that items mention. Official: both are coming back, according to Joe. In the files: neither name appears in any in-game text. For now, the link is just community wishful thinking.

The second says the Fragments opened by the Ancient Gateway are pieces of other Alterverses. Official: in the world generation post, Simon wrote that Hypixel wants to explore alternate worlds and new dimensions beyond Orbis. Against: the game itself calls them Fragments of Orbis, and keys like the Fragment: Orbis - Tal'Kesi Refuge carry the Orbis name. They are pieces of Orbis, not of another world.

What to expect

Official: Simon said the curse wakes up with the first chapter, and the Ancient Gateway already talks about piercing the Void that veils Orbis. Theory: if Chapter 1 brings new text, it should touch theories 1, 2 and 3 first. Until then, remember what Joe wrote: some canon foundations are very unlikely to change, but everything above them stays flexible.
