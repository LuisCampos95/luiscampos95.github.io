Hytale Update 2: pickaxes, farming and auto-updates

A week after Update 1, the second patch gated adamantite behind thorium and cobalt pickaxes, made farming less punishing, added a necromancy grimoire and gave servers auto-update. The January 28 hotfix went after stuttering on NVIDIA cards.

One week after Update 1, the second Early Access patch touched structural systems. Mining got a stricter progression ladder, farming became less punishing and server owners received an automatic update system. Along the way came a necromancy item and a batch of accessibility and interface options.

Main changes

  • Adamantite gets gated. Adamantite Ore now requires a Thorium Pickaxe or a Cobalt Pickaxe.
  • Necromancy. The Grimoire: Rekindle Embers raises skeletal minions from nearby Pile of Bones.
  • Hideable armor. Each equipped piece has a toggle to hide its look.
  • FPS counter in the settings.
  • Ophidiophobia mode, which swaps snakes for another model.
  • Auto-update for dedicated servers, with its own commands.

Creatures

  • The Polar Bear got stronger.
  • The Bison, Boar, Cow, Horse and Warthog now drop Medium Hide instead of Light Hide. That hide is what becomes Medium Leather, the leather the Leather Backpack has asked for since Update 1.
  • The Magma Rhino Toad had its tongue damage cut significantly. In exchange, it gained a headbutt and now alternates between both attacks.
  • The Cave Rex received every basic sound it was missing.
  • With ophidiophobia mode on, the Cobra, Marsh Snake and Rattlesnake turn into the Hatworm.

Items and equipment

Necromancy

The Grimoire: Rekindle Embers raises skeletal minions from nearby Pile of Bones. It has a chance to drop from the large Burnt Skeleton Praetorian. Update 3 would come back to improve its summons.

Pickaxes and mining

Mining progression was reworked. Low-tier pickaxes deal much less damage to high-tier ore. Moving up to a better pickaxe, on the other hand, cuts the number of hits considerably.

The most visible case is Adamantite Ore. Only the Thorium Pickaxe and the Cobalt Pickaxe can mine it now. Skipping steps got much harder.

Hoes

Hoes had their recipe costs adjusted and now unlock at different Farmer's Workbench tiers:

  • The Copper Hoe moved from Tier 4 to Tier 2.
  • The Iron Hoe moved from Tier 8 to Tier 4.
  • The Thorium Hoe was added at Tier 6.

Petals and cloth

Petals are now crafted at the Furniture Workbench, under the Textiles category. Since Yellow Petals can be farmed by planting the Sunflower Seed Bag, every petal color is now a renewable resource. With them, each cloth block got a lighter variant.

  • The Furniture Workbench also received more decorative light recipes.
  • Refilling containers targets the closest water block. Before, you had to aim at the block behind or under the water.
  • Some items showed an oversized placement preview. Fixed.

World and blocks

  • Ore placement was reworked in every zone. The result is more ore, especially underground in the Devastated Lands. On older worlds, the change only shows up in new chunks.
  • The Creative Hub portal now generates a flat world with WorldGen V2. That means clear weather and little fog, but only in new creative worlds.
  • The Emerald Wilds dungeon and other environments were fixed. Soundtracks that never played now do.
  • A broken Howling Sands cave track was repaired.

Gameplay

Farming is where daily play changed the most:

  • Eternal crops no longer break from accidental weapon hits.
  • Harvesting a fully grown Eternal crop gives its seeds back.
  • Tilled soil lasts longer: between 1.2 and 1.5 days.
  • Soil no longer decays under fully grown crops.
  • You can hold a torch in your offhand while using a hoe or seeds.
  • Harvested crops can be placed on the ground while crouching.

Other gameplay changes:

  • The Teleporter applies an offset on arrival to avoid accidental collisions.
  • The Rail Cart is now consumed and placed correctly.
  • Bed respawning in unloaded chunks works again. Collision checks failed whenever no chunk was loaded.
  • Mantling detection was fixed, along with many unintended behaviors.
  • Weather transitions are less jarring. The studio said more improvements are coming.
  • In Creative, middle-clicking a block with a full hotbar replaces the current slot with the picked block.

Interface and quality of life

Hotbar, inventory and map

  • The active hotbar slot has more contrast and a diamond indicator. It is easier to spot in bright places like the Howling Sands.
  • Double-clicking in the inventory is more responsive.
  • Shift + clicking a piece of armor equips it directly if that slot is empty.
  • Q, E, R and T are now shortcuts for the quick actions: put all, pull all, quick stack and sort. The shortcuts appear in the button tooltips.
  • The FPS counter lives under General > User Interface and shows in the top-right corner.
  • Player markers appear on the compass, with a different icon for players above or below you.
  • The "Player:" prefix was removed from map markers, and player names always render.
  • The map's player list has toggles to show or hide each player on the map and compass.
  • Memory categories in The Heart of Orbis are sorted alphabetically.
  • Cleared keybinds stay cleared across sessions.

Accessibility

Ophidiophobia mode lives under Gameplay > Accessibility. It swaps snake models for the Hatworm.

Avatar and armor

Toggles next to each equipped piece, in the character panel, hide the armor's look. Server hosts can force or allow the option to fit PvP or immersion rules.

New editor options, available to everyone:

  • Bandit Mask, with a Side Knot variant.
  • Blindfold, with a Thick variant.
  • Bandage Blindfold, with Thick, Left Eye and Right Eye variants.
  • Vampire, Cute and Orc mouths.
  • White for the fantasy cotton colors.

Six haircuts got updated visuals: Single Side Pigtail, Hair Stick Bun, Simple Bobcut, Fighter Buns, Smart Elven and Windswept. Capes animate better while running, and the editor tooltip now names each color.

Audio and visual effects

  • The bones drag-and-drop inventory sound was updated.
  • Every food particle got new tints.

Performance and stability

  • The server received major stability, validation, ECS, localization and timeout improvements.
  • Shadow rendering was improved to fix several issues.

January 28 hotfix

Four days after the update, a hotfix went after performance and leftover bugs:

  • Stuttering and input lag on NVIDIA graphics cards were reduced.
  • Shadow display issues were fixed.
  • Crashes related to weather, equipping armor and disconnecting are gone.
  • Login is more reliable and the server more stable.
  • The compass points to the right place again.
  • Block placing in Creative improved.
  • The Teleporter no longer traps players in an endless teleport loop.
  • The crossbow stopped misfiring.

Notable fixes

  • Map teleporting now requires permission. Before, a modified client could send packets to the server and teleport anywhere without being an operator.
  • Player marker updates were sent while the map was closed. Not anymore.
  • Player markers flickered when hovered on the map.
  • Other players' names are more readable when zoomed in on the map.
  • The Berserker haircut texture tiled incorrectly.
  • Glasses lenses inherited the cosmetic color on their highlights.

For servers and modders

The technical highlight is server auto-update. It arrived as the core UpdateModule plugin, which hooks into boot and checks for, downloads and applies updates while notifying players.

  • UpdateConfig, inside HytaleServerConfig, sets whether updates are enabled, the check interval, notifications, patchline, backups and the auto-apply mode.
  • The /update commands check, download, apply and manage updates, with detailed messages and a warning before auto-apply.
  • The new ShutdownReason.UPDATE flags a restart to apply a staged update.
  • The release now includes the start.sh and start.bat launcher scripts.
  • WorldGen V2 was prepared for public documentation, and the Default_Flat and Default_Void templates were added.
  • Hosts can force or allow armor visibility.
  • The Asset Editor shows documentation in tooltips, and editor block descriptions improved.
  • Scripted brushes got fixed filters, new decoration and cave brushes, and lost the redundant ones. The JumpIfBlockType node uses a single offset instead of a list.
