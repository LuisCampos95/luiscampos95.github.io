Gaia, Varyn and the Void: what is official in the mythology of Orbis

The Avatar of Orbis, the villain from another world, the curse of the Void and the Echo: what Hypixel confirmed and what is only interpretation.

Hytale still does not tell its story through cutscenes. In January 2026, Joe, who looks after the game's lore, explained that in Exploration mode almost everything would be uncovered through "archaeology": structures, items and objects leaving clues across the world. So we went digging. We put together what Hypixel has published since 2019 and the texts that live inside the game, and carefully separated what is official from what is our own reading.

To keep things clear, two labels apply to the whole article:

  • Official is what Hypixel said in a post, video or artwork, or what is written in the game.
  • Possible reading is interpretation by the OrbisHytale team. It may be wrong.

What Hypixel confirmed in 2026

Joe's post revealed no secrets, and he said so right at the start. But it laid out the skeleton of the mythology:

  • The team went back to an earlier draft of the story, more compatible with the game being shipped. Whole factions had been cut or changed in the years before.
  • Gaia and Varyn remain canon.
  • Gaia is the Avatar of Orbis. And not every faction knows it.
  • Tessa and Kyros, characters from the old artwork, are coming back.
  • The concept of Alterverses will also be revisited.
  • There is a narrative for players to piece together, tied to the switch from world generator V1 to V2. Its name: Cursebreaker.

At launch, the "Hytale is finally here" post added: find the Forgotten Temple and uncover the first elements of the Cursebreaker story arc, which will unfold in future updates.

Gaia, the Avatar of Orbis

Official. Gaia is the Avatar of Orbis. That is what Joe wrote, and it is all Hypixel has defined in words. The rest comes from the game, and the game says a lot.

The center of it all is The Heart of Orbis, the statue in the Forgotten Temple. Its description speaks of a bodiless voice heard around it, coming from a being on the other side of the barriers, from the original world. When you unlock the Memories system, the game explains the rest: a deity rests within The Heart of Orbis, and each memory of Orbis' creatures helps restore her will. The text says "her". The game never writes Gaia's name on that screen, but the Ancient Gateway does: a curse holds Gaia's Orbis shut away, but the Void covering it can be broken through.

Official, in the files. The statue models tell a parallel story:

  • Statue of a Slumbering Deity and Statue of a Silent Deity use the same 3D model, and the file is called Gaia_Statue_01. It is a figure with long hair, a dress and a globe in her hands.
  • Outlander Deity Statue has "Gaia" in its item ID. But its model has a part named Varyn-Mark and another shaped like a diamond.
  • The Howling Sands soundtrack has a track called "Gaia's Lament", which plays during the day.
  • The Harvest Trophy takes a form that reflects the favor of Orbis' Avatar.

Possible reading. The statues match Joe's line about the factions. Each people seems to have its own version of the deity: one slumbering, one silent, and one the Outlanders worship. That the Outlander statue carries Varyn's mark is the most unsettling detail in the game so far. It could mean they worship Gaia through a corrupted lens, or that they confuse one with the other. None of this is confirmed.

The Temple of Gaia

In 2019, in the first world generation post, Hypixel showed a portal dungeon called the Temple of Gaia, with an area called the Garden of Elements.

Official, in the files. The Env_Temple_Of_Gaia environment still exists in the game, and it inherits its settings from the Forgotten Temple's base environment.

Possible reading. The Forgotten Temple may be what remains of the Temple of Gaia idea. The shared environment hints at a link, but proves nothing.

The curse and the Echo

This is where the pieces fit best. Every point below is official, from the game or from hytale.com:

  • The Early Access mode is called Echoes of Orbis.
  • The region where you spawn is called First Gate of the Echo.
  • The Forgotten Temple's subtitle is Edge of the Echo.
  • The Heart of Orbis holds the echo of a being from the original world.
  • The Memories screen says the Void's curse keeps Orbis locked away, but there may be a way to break it. And it invites you to explore the many versions of Orbis.
  • The Ancient Gateway exists to tear down the barriers and travel to other Fragments.
  • In the Update 3 notes, Hypixel explained that chapters tell the Cursebreaker arc, one by one, until the final chapter puts Orbis within reach and the game reaches full release.
  • In July 2026, a post about the V2 generator called the current world, made by V1, The Echo. The same post warned that something is brewing that will affect the Echo. What you built stays intact, but the world around it may change a lot.

Putting it together: the world you explore today is the Echo, and the real Orbis is not yet within reach. Hypixel has said that plainly. Breaking the curse is the thread of the Cursebreaker arc, and one of the game's editions carries the same name: the Cursebreaker Edition.

Possible reading. Inside the story, the Echo would be a kind of reflection of Orbis, stuck outside the barriers. The original Orbis, Gaia's Orbis, would be locked behind the Void, and the Fragments would be pieces of it leaking through the cracks. That would explain why the voice in The Heart of Orbis comes from the original world, and not from the one you are standing in.

Varyn

Official. Varyn is Hytale's chief antagonist. That is what Hypixel called him in April 2019. In the Varyn artwork published in February of that year, artist Thomas "Xael" Frick explained how he created him:

  • Varyn is not from Orbis, so he had to look different from everything else in the game.
  • The idea was something demon-like, not exactly humanoid, with a spectral side.
  • The green skin and the diamond-shaped eye are the mark of Varyn and his minions.
  • The orb in his hand represents Void magic. In the final piece it became purple-pink, the color of corruption in the world.
  • Most of the time, Varyn is a distant threat.

In its 2019 post about monsters, Hypixel went further: the creatures of the Void make up Varyn's armies. Their curving horns symbolize Varyn, and diamond shapes signal his presence.

Official, in the files. Varyn's name does not appear in any text in the game. The only mention is the name of that model part, Varyn-Mark, on the Outlander deity statue.

Possible reading. The game talks about the Void's curse and about those who commune with it, but never about who cast it. That fits a story told through archaeology: the peoples of Orbis may not even know Varyn exists.

The Void in the game

Almost everything here is official, because the Void is the part of the lore you actually fight.

Born and taken. The Memories system splits creatures into two categories: Voidspawn, born of the Void, and Voidtaken, taken by it. The Voidheart explains the difference: it comes from a powerful Void creature and is still beating, and even a creature that was not born in the Void, only corrupted by it, can end up with the same withered heart. Meanwhile, Essence of the Void admits nobody knows whether these creatures produce the essence or need it to exist.

The creatures. Void Larva, Void Crawler, Void Eye and Void Spectre appear at night across the world. The Void Fiend and the Void Guardian Golem round out the roster. The Chapter 1 pre-release brought the taken: the Voidtaken Goblin and the hunt bears, Voidtaken Grizzly, Voidtaken Scarback and Voidtaken Duskmane.

Crystals and matter. The Void leaves a physical trail: Void Crystal Shards, Voidstone, Void Tar and Void Liana. In the pre-release, Void anomalies make crystals such as the Void Crystal sprout, alongside the Void Stutter.

In the portals. The Ancient Gateway screen shows a Void Corruption warning. Veilbound items cannot leave the portal. And before the fragment closes comes the Void Breach, a Void invasion. In Chapter 1, according to Hypixel, the Void keeps pressing in until it throws you out of the goblin world.

Even goblins are scared. Since the Update 7 pre-release, Void creatures are hostile toward goblins, and most goblins run from them when outnumbered.

The Outlanders. The Shadoweave Scraps description mentions the moment the Outlanders first communed with the Void. Back in 2019, Hypixel already said they explore darker forms of magic than those found elsewhere.

The story of the Outlanders and the other peoples is in Factions of Hytale.

The player's role

Official. The game calls you an Avatar. It is on the Crude Bedroll, which lets an Avatar set their respawn point, and in the Chapter 1 post, which talks about giving your Avatar new abilities. The character editor is called the Avatar Creator.

Two bench texts go further. The Workbench says creators are made, not born, and that even Avatars must practice, especially when playing by the rules of someone else's domain. The Arcanist's Workbench says the arcane arts are the closest mere mortals have come to Avatar powers.

The word "adventurer" shows up too. The Campfire mentions adventurers who call a campfire home, and the Crude Sword recalls that, in the age of heroes, making your first weapon was a rite of passage on the road to becoming an adventurer.

In practice, your role in Early Access is this: find the Forgotten Temple, gather memories from creatures, bring them back to The Heart of Orbis and open Fragments with the Ancient Gateway.

Possible reading. Gaia is the Avatar of Orbis, and you are an Avatar too, but playing by the rules of someone else's domain. You would be an outsider, playing in her world. Hypixel has not explained this yet.

The ancient history of Orbis

The item texts form a timeline full of gaps. Everything here is official, but stitching the pieces together is our reading.

  • Civilizations fell to pride. The Bone Fragments say Orbis' most advanced civilizations shared the hubris that brought them down.
  • Fire built and destroyed. The Crude Torch says that, for the last peoples of Orbis, fire lifted their civilization to new heights and also caused its undoing.
  • Crucible. A city whose fire magic gave form to the dreams of its artisans, according to the Essence of Fire. The Adamantite Ingot says the metal was key to the advances of Crucible's people. The Cindercloth Scraps say the cursed flame swept through the city and changed everything it touched.
  • Those who refused the flame. The Essence of Ice says the Outlanders rejected fire and split from their brethren, heading for the cold lands. In 2019, Hypixel called the Outlanders a breakaway human faction.
  • The age of heroes. It appears on the Crude Sword and the Heavy Leather, which mentions the few heroes who banded together to hunt creatures of myth.
  • Knights and alchemists. The Bandage mentions the knights of a lost civilization, and the Alchemist's Workbench the alchemists who chased eternal life and may have reached it in a form they never expected.
  • The Empress. The Statue of the Founding Builder mentions atrocities committed under the first and last Scarak Empress.
  • Ownerless structures. The Silent Henges fragment description asks who built the Ancient Gateways, and why.

Possible reading. Crucible would be a great human civilization of fire, in today's Devastated Lands. The Outlanders would be the branch that refused the flame before the catastrophe. Zone 4's soundtrack backs the idea with tracks named "Lost Brethren", "March of the Turned" and "Eternal Life". The furniture of the human ruins is called Lost Civilization, and one of its statues is the Bearer of Fire Statue. And alchemists who found a strange kind of eternal life could explain all the skeletons walking around.

Official or interpretation: the summary

Official:

  • Gaia is the Avatar of Orbis, and not every faction knows it
  • Varyn is the chief antagonist, is not from Orbis and commands the creatures of the Void
  • A curse of the Void keeps Gaia's Orbis locked away
  • A deity rests in The Heart of Orbis, and memories restore her will
  • The current arc is called Cursebreaker, is tied to the V1 to V2 switch and ends with Orbis within reach
  • The current V1 world is called The Echo
  • The player is called an Avatar
  • Tessa, Kyros and the Alterverses are coming back

Possible reading:

  • The Echo is a reflection of Gaia's Orbis, stuck outside the barriers
  • The deity in The Heart of Orbis is Gaia
  • The Outlander statue shows Gaia seen through Varyn
  • The Forgotten Temple inherited the Temple of Gaia idea
  • Crucible and the Outlanders come from the same human civilization
