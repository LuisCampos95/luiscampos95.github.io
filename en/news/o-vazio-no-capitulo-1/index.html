Is Varyn coming back? The Void in Chapter 1

Shielded crystals, tentacles, corrupted bears and the Breach at 7 minutes: how the Void grows in the files and what it says about Varyn. Theory marked.

This is a theory: speculation based on official information, marked as such throughout the text. Nothing here is confirmed by Hypixel Studios unless a source says so.

Varyn has been Hytale's big villain since the first trailers. With Chapter 1 coming, the question is back: does he show up now? The short answer is that nobody has confirmed it. The long answer is in the files: the Void, the force tied to him, grows with every pre-release. It got new creatures, crystals, tentacles, corrupted bears and an invasion on a timer.

This article carefully separates what is official, what is in the files and what is theory. The base story of Gaia and Varyn is in Gaia, Varyn and the Void. Here the focus is what changed in the game. The cover shows the Void in a fight in the goblin world. Image: Hypixel Studios (hytale.com).

What is official

  • 2019, monster design. The studio explained that Void creatures make up Varyn's armies. They have pale flesh with purple accents, glowing green eyes and curved horns that symbolize Varyn himself. Diamond shapes also hint at his presence. Void Spawn are among his most dangerous soldiers.
  • January 2026, lore post. Gaia and Varyn remain canon, as do Tessa and Kyros.
  • February 2026, Update 3. Chapters tell the Cursebreaker arc in Exploration mode, one at a time, up to the full release.
  • July 2026, Chapter 1 blog. The goblin world has a growing Void presence. Inside, the Void presses in and eventually ejects you. The activities include Void assassins that hunt the player.
  • September 2026, Update 7. Void creatures became hostile toward goblins. Most goblins flee, unless they have enough allies nearby.
  • In the game. The Ancient Gateway description says the curse keeps Gaia's Orbis locked away, but there are ways to pierce the Void that veils it.

What the files show

The usual Void, at night

In the files. The Void Crawler, the Void Eye and the Void Larva spawn only at night, between 19:00 and 05:00 on the game clock, and only in dark places. By day, they vanish. The odds change with the moon phase. Biomes are split into three tiers: plains, savanna and tundra in the first; forests, mountains and plateaus in the second; swamps, deserts, oases and glaciers in the third. The Void Spectre only shows up in the third tier.

In the files. The Void also runs through the Outlanders. The Outlander Initiate, the Outlander Priest and the Outlander Sorcerer drop Essence of the Void. To learn who they are, read the factions of Hytale.

What arrived in Update 7

In the files. The 09/03 pre-release brought a whole Void package:

  • Void Fiend: 500 health. It sleeps trapped inside blocks, wakes when someone gets close and always drops 1 Voidheart. It replaced the old Void Spawn in the files.
  • Void Tentacle: 100 health. It got spawn animations, and its bite animations now have left and right sides.
  • Three crystals: the Void Crystal, with 25 health, the Void Crystal, with 75, and the Void Crystal, with 150.
  • Void Stutter, with 1 health.

In the files. The crystals are the heart of the Void anomalies. They start with a shield that makes them invulnerable. Around them, waves of tentacles, eyes or larvae appear. Once the waves are cleared, the shield drops. Destroying the crystal adds 2, 4 or 8 minutes to a portal world's timer, depending on size.

The Void Breach

In the files. In Fragments with the invasion switched on, the Void arrives at 7 minutes, with dark weather, a track of its own and invasion portals, the Void Invasion Portal. Out come Void Crawler, Void Eye and Void Larva. At 9 minutes, the weather gets heavier. Details are in the dungeons and portals guide.

Corrupted bears and hunts

In the files. The 09/17 pre-release turned the hunt bosses into bears taken by the Void. Step by step, from the game texts:

  • A notice says a hunting ground has appeared in the wild. Look through a Spyglass to reveal where it is.
  • At the site there are signs of the predator, like Predator Tracks and Claw Marks. The game asks you to place a trap.
  • With the trap armed, leave the area. The predator catches your scent and comes after you.
  • If you win, the wilds calm down. If you take too long, the bear slinks away and the hunting ground grows cold.

The three bears are the Voidtaken Grizzly, with 300 health, the Voidtaken Scarback, with 400, and the Voidtaken Duskmane, with 500. The stronger it is, the more Essence of the Void it drops: up to 2, up to 4 and up to 6. There is also the Void Tentacle, with 24 health.

Official. The Chapter 1 blog listed predators to track and trap as one of the first goblin world activities.

Born and taken

In the files. The Memories book has two Void categories: Voidspawn and Voidtaken. The Voidheart description explains: not every creature is born of the Void, but those taken and corrupted may carry the same heart.

Ready, but on hold

In the files. Some Void models exist with no NPC:

  • Voidtaken Goblin, a corrupted goblin, since 09/03.
  • Void Dragon, a Void dragon. A particle effect named after it is used when the invasion portals appear.
  • Void Guardian Golem, with an NPC role that only has a placeholder 400 health and no behavior.
  • Void Necromancer, a Void necromancer.

In the files. The name Varyn does not appear in any of the game's server texts.

Theory: is Varyn coming back?

Everything from here down is theory. It is our reading of the facts above.

Theory 1: the Void comes in three layers. Those born of the Void, like the larva and the spectre. Those taken, like the bears and, soon, the corrupted goblin. And the invasions, like the Breach in Fragments. Chapter 1 seems to test all three at once.

Theory 2: goblins are the next victims. Update 7 made goblins flee the Void. A corrupted goblin model is ready. Put together, goblins that do not flee in time become enemies in the goblin world.

Theory 3: the Void dragon is a future boss. It has a model, an empty drop list in the files and an effect used in the invasions. That is the kind of creature that closes a chapter, not opens one.

Theory 4: the golem at the Temple entrance. The Forgotten Temple has an objective that tells you to kill the Golem. The Void Guardian Golem has an NPC role, but it is still empty. In 2019, the Garden of Elements concept art showed a "corrupted golem". It may be the same idea coming back.

Theory 5: what about Varyn? Nothing in the files points to him in Chapter 1. The chapter belongs to the goblins, and his name is not in the game texts. Our bet: Varyn stays offstage for now, and the Void acts for him, growing with each update until the day he shows up himself.

Official, files and theory, side by side

  • Official: Void creatures are Varyn's armies; Gaia and Varyn remain canon; the goblin world has a growing Void presence; Void creatures attack goblins.
  • In the files: shielded crystals, tentacles, the 500 health entombed spawn, the Breach at 7 minutes, three corrupted bears, the Voidspawn and Voidtaken categories, and idle models of a Void goblin, dragon, golem and necromancer.
  • Theory: the Void grows in three layers, goblins become victims, the dragon is a future boss and Varyn stays behind the scenes in Chapter 1.
