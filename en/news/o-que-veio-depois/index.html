What came after the victory

The Revenant Guardian Memory says they won the Great War but were powerless against what followed. Three suspects for a catastrophe bigger than the war.

Revenant Guardians are huge skeletons with ruined swords, guarding ruins in the late game. They look like just another tough enemy. Their Memory says they were once the greatest heroes in Orbis.

What the game says is in quotes. Our guesses are marked Theory:.

The Memory

Revenant Guardian

"The term 'Guardian' was once synonymous with safety to towns and villages throughout Orbis, and the elite amongst their numbers were revered by the populace for generations. It was in part through their valor that the Great War was won - though even they were powerless in the face of what followed."

Three things jump out. The Guardians protected all of Orbis, not a single city. They were revered for generations, so this was an old institution. And the game separates two disasters: the war, which they won, and what followed, which nobody did.

The game never says what it was. But it leaves three suspects.

Suspect 1: the cursed flame

The obvious one. The Guardians carry the Enkindled Sigil, the sigil of a civilization lost "to a calamity that left Orbis scarred". And a cursed flame scoured Crucible, the city of fire artisans. Its story is in Crucible: the city the fire erased.

The problem: one burnt city doesn't explain, on its own, a calamity that scarred all of Orbis.

Suspect 2: the purple sky

The goblins keep a memory the Guardians lost. In a pre-release note, they say the stories talk about a red or blue sky before this purple one. Today the Veil of Void seals Orbis, and the The Goblin Gateway says so itself.

If the sky changed color, the whole world changed with it. That fits a calamity that scarred Orbis far better than a fire. The notes are in Purple sky, Enkindled journals and goblin paintings.

Suspect 3: eternal life

The third is the darkest. The Alchemist's Workbench says:

"The alchemists of old long sought the secrets to eternal life. Some might say they achieved that grand goal, though perhaps not in the form they imagined."

A revenant is "one who returns". The Guardians never quite died: they are still standing, sword in hand, keeping the sigil where their hearts once beat.

Theory: what followed didn't kill the Guardians, it trapped them. The catastrophe turned Orbis' protectors into restless dead, and that's why neither swords nor valor could stop it.

Putting the three together

Theory: the three suspects may be the same thing seen from different sides. The cursed flame burned Crucible, the world was scarred, the sky turned purple, and those on the front line weren't even allowed to die. You win a war with soldiers. Not a catastrophe like this.

Who the Guardians fought in the Great War is another story, told in Those who refused the flame.

Open questions

  • How much time passed between the victory and what followed?
  • Do today's Guardians know who they are, or do they just repeat their last order?
  • If all of Orbis was scarred, why are Kweebecs, Trorks and Outlanders still alive?
  • Did the purple sky arrive with the flame, or long after?
