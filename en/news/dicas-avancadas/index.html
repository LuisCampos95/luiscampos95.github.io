Advanced Hytale tips: mechanics the game never explains

Glider, taming, eternal crops, Update 7 runes, Update 6 Hardcore and hidden shortcuts, each tip backed by the official note that confirms it.

After day one, Hytale starts hiding things. A lot of useful mechanics only show up in a patch note or an item description. We gathered the best ones, each with its source: the official notes for Updates 1 to 7, summarized on the site, and the game files. Anything from the Update 7 pre-release is flagged and may still change.

Let your benches work on their own

  • Benches pull materials from chests up to 14 blocks away horizontally and 6 vertically (Update 1). Build a workshop with chests in the middle and forget about your inventory.
  • Since Update 4, benches keep processing while the chunk is unloaded and use world time. Fill the Furnace with ore and go explore.
  • Chests have stacked to 25 items since Update 5.
  • Kits like the Repair Kit work on items stored in the backpack (Update 5).

Farming that pays off

  • The Empty Watering Can makes crops grow 2.5 times faster. Charged, it waters a 3x3 area, and it holds 50 uses of water (Update 4).
  • The Fertilizer Bag doubles growth speed, according to the item description.
  • Eternal seeds, like the Wheat Seed Bag (Eternal), give the seed back on harvest and do not break from stray weapon swings (Update 2).
  • Tilled soil lasts 1.2 to 1.5 days and does not decay under mature crops (Update 2). After harvest it turns back into dirt (Update 6), so till it again.
  • Sickles, like the Crude Sickle, harvest several crops in one swing.
  • Blood, storm and azure crops need soil crystallized with the Crystal Fertilizer Bag (Update 4). That soil cannot be watered or fertilized.
  • Opening and closing doors breaks crops in the way (Update 5). Do not plant right against a door.

Taming

Taming arrived in Update 3. Some friendly animals can be tamed by holding their favorite food. After that, they accept petting with an empty hand, need food now and then and only flee if they get hit. Some give milk or wool, and others can be ridden.

  • The Feed Bag attracts some tamed animals. A tamed Mosshorn has followed the Feed Bag since Update 6.
  • The Capture Crate captures small animals so you can take them home. Since Update 5, its icon shows who is inside.
  • Captured chickens can live in the Chicken Coop.
  • The Wooden Bucket milks cows, and the Crude Shears shears sheep.

Glider and goblin gadgets (pre-release)

The Update 7 pre-release brought gadgets made at the GoblinLab Workbench.

The Scrap Glider costs 20 Medium Leather, 20 Light Leather, 50 Iron Scrap Chunks and 1 Goblin Stool. Flight was rebuilt: pressing and releasing the sprint key switches between diving and climbing. The glider also keeps the speed you had when you opened it, so open it mid-sprint or mid-fall. In Part 3 it stopped climbing from repeated jumps.

  • Scrap Drill: digs tunnels and breaks several blocks at once. Metal blocks come out whole, but you need to get closer than with regular tools.
  • Hookshot: pulls you to the targeted block or pulls the target to you. The Hook Anchor works as an anchor point, and 5 Iron Ingot make 5 of them.
  • Rocket Boots: a rocket-blast double jump, +13 health and 7% physical and projectile resistance.
  • Spyglass: zooms in so you can watch what is far away.
  • The Goblin Boom Barrel can be picked up and carried, and in Part 3 it became throwable with the primary button. Hatchets also detonate explosive blocks (Part 2).

Runes and the Effigy (pre-release)

Runes are slotted into the Runebinder's Lattice and grant combat abilities. The five on the pre-release:

  • Enrage: raises damage dealt and damage taken for a while. Use it when the enemy is almost down.
  • Fireball: a fireball that explodes on impact. Remember that fire creatures are immune to fire damage.
  • Imbue Poison: poisons your weapon, and the poison stacks. It does nothing to Scaraks, immune to poison since Update 6.
  • Ground Slam: raises a stone spike that launches enemies into the air. In Part 3 it started working with battleaxes, like the Iron Battleaxe.
  • Wind Strike: your next three sword swings release wind arcs that pierce targets.

On a controller, abilities use RB held plus a face button: RB + Y fires the weapon's signature move, RB + X the first ability, RB + B the second and RB + A the fourth. In the July Chapter 1 preview, the studio showed abilities on the E and R keys, noting it would still change. In Part 3, runes were split into Ability Runes and Modifier Runes. The five current ones are ability runes.

Memories

Exploring and meeting creatures and factions earns Memories. You turn them in at the The Heart of Orbis to unlock permanent rewards and upgrades for everyone in the world. The inventory shows how many discovered Memories are still waiting to be restored (Update 5).

In Part 3 of the pre-release, some creatures started releasing their Memory only once defeated. Getting close is no longer enough for them. And Memories from a session are no longer lost when it ends.

Portals and fragments

The Ancient Gateway is made at the Arcanist's Workbench for 30 Cobblestone, 10 Thorium Ingot, 10 Essence of Life and 10 Essence of the Void. Placed at your base, it opens temporary worlds, the Fragments of Orbis. Each key, like the Fragment: Orbis - Dragonspire Weald, leads to a different fragment.

  • Since Update 6, the gateway has a close button, locked while someone is still on the other side. If the destination has no valid spawn point, it gives the key back.
  • Portal worlds no longer close when you walk away from the device (Update 6).
  • On the pre-release, each portal can limit how many times each player enters, some stay open with no time limit and all of them follow the original world's rules, like fall damage and PvP.

For fixed routes, the Teleporter comes in pairs from the same bench. Place two to create the link. Since Update 3, you have to step away a little before using it again.

Hardcore (Update 6)

Hardcore is only chosen when you create the world, under the Customize button, and it cannot be changed later:

  • Permadeath: one life per player.
  • Three Strikes: three lives per player.
  • Nine Lives: nine lives shared by the whole group. On the ninth death, the run ends for everyone.
  • Soulbound: if any player dies, the run is over.

In Three Strikes and Nine Lives, you can change the number of lives when creating the world, and the remaining lives show above the health bar. Whoever dies drops everything they carry and becomes an invisible spectator that NPCs ignore. Spectators do not keep dungeons open.

Shortcuts the game does not tell you

  • X: emote wheel (Update 4).
  • Q, E, R and T: inventory quick actions (Update 2).
  • Shift + click on armor equips it directly if the slot is empty (Update 2).
  • Shift + drag moves items between inventories (Update 3).
  • Holding the drop key and hovering in the inventory drops whole stacks (Update 4).
  • Crouching while placing a block attaches it next to leaves and other non-solid blocks without replacing them (Update 5).
  • Rolling on landing reduces fall damage, and sprint carries over when you crouch-slide (Update 5).
  • Tab in chat completes commands, and Shift + Tab shows variations (Update 5).
  • F7 twice opens the debug overlay, rebuilt on the Update 7 pre-release.
  • Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo Switch controllers have worked since Update 5, and gyro aiming arrived in Update 6.

Small secrets

  • The Alchemist's Workbench makes morph potions: Potion of Dog Morphing, Potion of Frog Morphing, Potion of Mouse Morphing and Potion of Pigeon Morphing. All of them take 10 Essence of Life and a themed ingredient, like Cheese for the mouse.
  • The Grimoire: Rekindle Embers raises skeletal minions from bone piles of species tied to fire. They vanish after 5 minutes or when you die, and each minion uses up one grimoire. The Burnt Skeleton Praetorian has a 50% chance to drop it.
  • The Deployable Turret treats whoever placed it as an ally (Update 5), and the Healing Totem heals 5 health per second for 10 seconds.
  • Healing brews like the Health Potion lose their effect if you take damage. Drink away from the fight.
  • Proximity voice chat is off by default. Turn it on in the audio settings (Update 4).
  • Since Update 6, friends can join your world without Tailscale or a VPN.

Just starting? Read the first steps, the guide to every bench and the one on dangerous creatures first. The full notes are in Update 6 and Update 7.
