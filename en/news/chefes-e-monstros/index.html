The bosses and strongest monsters of Orbis

Who has the most health, what each boss drops and where it lives: dragons, Hedera, Void bears, golems and the first Chapter 1 multi-phase boss.

Hytale wants to be an open world RPG, and every RPG needs bosses. For now, the list of strong creatures in Orbis is short and scattered: two dragons, a giant plant, crystal golems, bears taken by the Void and a skeleton that comes back to life mid fight. Chapter 1 brings the first handcrafted multi-phase boss.

The health values and drops below come from the pre-release files cataloged by the wiki. What the studio said on hytale.com is marked as official. Only want the creatures that kill you most early on? Read the dangerous creatures guide.

The first multi-phase boss

Official. In the Chapter 1 blog, the studio announced its first handcrafted dungeon and, inside it, the first multi-phase boss fight. This article's cover shows that fight. Image: Hypixel Studios (hytale.com).

The tech behind it is called the Encounter Manager, and it is already in the pre-release. It coordinates phases, enemy waves and arena changes. Encounters are authored as content, not engine code, so the team can build new ones faster. The studio says players will be able to make their own too.

The skeleton that comes back at full health

In the files. The only complete boss encounter already running in the pre-release belongs to the Revenant Guardian. It has 226 health. When its health drops to 10%, or when your hits on its shield break its stamina, it turns invulnerable for a moment and returns in Phase 2, at full health again. In practice, that is 452 health to bring down.

What it can drop:

  • 3 to 6 Essence of Fire, always.
  • 5 to 8 Pile of Skulls, always.
  • 1 to 3 Thorium Ingot, 70% chance.
  • 5 Grimoire: Rekindle Embers, 50% chance.
  • 1 to 3 Cobalt Ingot, 30% chance.
  • Each piece of the ancient steel armor, like the Ancient Steel Cuirass, 3.8% chance.

Official. In Update 7 Part 3, its Flowing Slashes attack got faster, and its attacks and footsteps got new sounds.

Who has the most health in Orbis

The list below skips test creatures. Health is the base value in the files.

  • 500: Voidtaken Duskmane and Void Fiend.
  • 400: Ember Dragon, Frost Dragon, Voidtaken Scarback, Cave Rex, Shadow Knight, Aberrant Zombie and the peaceful Humpback Whale.
  • 350: Goblin Feastmaster.
  • 341: Large Aberrant Zombie.
  • 300: Voidtaken Grizzly.
  • 283: Ember Golem and Werewolf.
  • 249: Thunder Spirit.
  • 226: Yeti, The Hedera, Revenant Guardian and Burnt Skeleton Praetorian.
  • 224: Frost Golem and Firesteel Golem.

A curious detail: we found no natural spawn rule for the Shadow Knight, the Werewolf or either version of the Aberrant Zombie in the files. They exist and have high health, but for now they do not show up in the world on their own.

The two dragons

The Ember Dragon and the Frost Dragon are the only creatures in the wiki's boss group. Each has 400 health and its own spawn marker. The wiki lists no drops for either.

In the files. The Dragonspire Weald key, the Fragment: Orbis - Dragonspire Weald, opens a Fragment with a tip: a mythical beast sleeps there, bound in an eternal slumber. The files also have a structure folder just for dragons, with a frost version.

Theory. The tip does not name the beast. Given the place name and the ice, the Frost Dragon is suspect number one.

The swamp plant

The The Hedera has 226 health and a spawn marker of its own. It is the boss of The Dread Wade Fragment, opened with the Fragment: Orbis - The Dread Wade. The portal tip warns that a ravenous menace lurks at the heart of the swamp, and that the murky shallows hide dangerous fauna.

Official. In Update 7 Part 1, the Void event in its lair was reworked and now escalates after fixed amounts of time. In the files, this is the only Fragment with the Void invasion switched on. The step by step is in the dungeons and portals guide.

The Void bears

The 09/17 pre-release brought three bears taken by the Void, one per hunt tier:

  • Voidtaken Grizzly: 300 health. Drops 1 to 2 Heavy Hide, 2 to 3 Raw Wildmeat and 1 to 2 Essence of the Void.
  • Voidtaken Scarback: 400 health. Same drops, with 2 to 4 Essence of the Void.
  • Voidtaken Duskmane: 500 health. Same drops, with 4 to 6 Essence of the Void.

Alongside them there is the Void Tentacle, with 24 health. How to find and lure these bears is covered in Is Varyn coming back? The Void in Chapter 1.

The glutton goblin

Official. The Chapter 1 blog treats some goblins as elites. The example given is the Goblin Feastmaster: it calls in reinforcements mid fight, charges at you and wrecks its surroundings.

In the files. It has 350 health and drops 2 to 4 Wool Scraps. In Update 7 Part 2, it got a shout that keeps nearby goblins quiet for a while.

Golems: the gem source

Crystal golems are not the strongest, but they pay the best. Each one drops a gem:

  • Ember Golem: 283 health. 25 Cobblestone, 15 Red Crystal Shards, 1 to 3 Cindercloth Scraps and 1 Ruby.
  • Frost Golem: 224 health. 25 Cobblestone, 15 Blue Crystal Shards and 1 Sapphire.
  • Sandswept Golem: 193 health. 25 Cobblestone, 15 Cyan Crystal Shards and 1 Zephyr.
  • Thunder Golem: 193 health. 25 Cobblestone, 15 Yellow Crystal Shards and 1 Topaz.
  • Earthen Golem: 160 health. 14 to 28 Cobblestone, 3 to 7 Green Crystal Shards and 1 Emerald.

The Firesteel Golem is different. It has 224 health, drops 2 to 5 Essence of Fire and 1 to 2 Iron Ingot, and shows up hostile in volcanic caves in zones 1, 2 and 4.

Other heavyweights

  • Cave Rex: 400 health. Hunts in the zone 4 jungles. Drops 2 to 3 Heavy Hide and 2 to 3 Raw Wildmeat.
  • Thunder Spirit: 249 health. Appears at the elemental circles of the Howling Sands. Drops 2 to 3 Essence of Lightning.
  • Yeti: 226 health. Drops 2 to 4 Heavy Hide and 2 to 3 Essence of Ice.
  • Emberwulf: 193 health. Lives in the zone 4 volcanoes. Drops 1 to 2 Heavy Hide and 2 to 3 Essence of Fire.
  • Burnt Skeleton Praetorian: 226 health, with the same drop list as the Revenant Guardian.
  • Scarak Broodmother: 145 health. Has a boss spawn marker of its own. Drops 2 to 3 Venom Sac and 4 to 6 Sturdy Chitin.
  • Trork Chieftain: 124 health. 15% chance to drop the Stone Trork Battleaxe and 1.3% per trork armor piece.

The toughest Void creature

The Void Fiend shares the top of the list, with 500 health. It sleeps trapped inside blocks and only wakes up when someone gets close. When it falls, it always drops 1 Voidheart.

Official. In 2019, in the post about monster design, the studio presented Void Spawn as some of Varyn's most dangerous soldiers.

A retired boss

The Goblin Duke is still in the files, with 226 health, but inside a folder marked as deprecated. There is even a variant named Phase 3. Everything suggests it was the old goblin boss, before the Chapter 1 goblin rework.

Quick summary

  • Most health: Voidtaken Duskmane and Void Fiend, at 500.
  • Longest fight today: the Revenant Guardian, which comes back at full health.
  • Best for gems: crystal golems.
  • Portal boss: the The Hedera.
  • First handcrafted multi-phase boss: arrives with Chapter 1.
