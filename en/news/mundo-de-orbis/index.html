Orbis from end to end: zones, biomes and what lives in each one

Emerald Wilds, Howling Sands, Whisperfrost Frontiers and Devastated Lands: regions, climate, ores, creatures and structures of every zone.

Orbis is the planet where almost all of Hytale's story takes place. That is how Hypixel Studios introduced it in January 2019, in its first post about world generation: a planet split into zones, each with its own terrain, blocks, weather, creatures and peoples. Seven years later, with the game in Early Access, that idea still stands. And the game files show the map in more detail than the blogs ever did.

The names changed

If you have followed Hytale since the reveal, you know the zones as Emerald Grove, Howling Sands, Borea and Devastated Lands. In April 2019 the team warned that those names were not final. On the current in-game map, the list looks like this:

  • Zone 1: Emerald Wilds. The old Emerald Grove. Temperate, with plains, forests and swamps.
  • Zone 2: Howling Sands. Kept its name. Hot, with savanna, plateaus and desert.
  • Zone 3: Whisperfrost Frontiers. The region the community called Borea. Cold, with taiga, tundra and glaciers.
  • Zone 4: Devastated Lands. Also kept its name. Scorched earth, volcanoes and dead forests.
  • Oceans. The seas between the zones.
  • The Void. The Void also shows up in the map's list of zone names.

The 2019 blog already said the deep ocean works as a zone of its own, with its own dangers and rewards. In the files it even got a number: ocean environments are treated as Zone 0. According to the same post, past the oceans lie the "infinite lands", which the story never visits but the generator keeps creating.

Every zone has tiers of danger

In December 2019 Hypixel said it had dropped the idea of making players cross the zones in order. Each zone would instead hold content at several difficulty levels. That is exactly what the generator does today: each zone is cut into three regions, from the calmest to the deadliest, plus the shore and the shallow sea. These are the names shown on the map:

  • Emerald Wilds: Drifting Plains, Seedling Woods and The Fens
  • Howling Sands: Golden Steppes, Badlands and Desolate Basin
  • Whisperfrost Frontiers: Frostmarch Tundra, Boreal Reach and The Everfrost
  • Devastated Lands: Cinder Wastes and Charred Woodlands, marked in the files as tiers 4 and 5
  • Oceans: Crystalline Depths

Two regions are unique. You spawn at First Gate of the Echo. Close to it, the generator reserves a region called Temple, home to the entrance of the Forgotten Temple.

Climate follows the same logic. The zone map works with three bands: temperate for Zone 1, cold for Zone 3 and hot for Zones 2 and 4.

Zone 1: Emerald Wilds

The starting zone, and the most varied one. Birch plains give way to aspen forests, flower woods and tall-grass gorges. The harder regions bring autumn forests, moss forests, swamps and the Azure forests, source of Azure Log.

Creatures. The plains hold Cow, Sheep, Horse, Stag, Fox and Plain Mosshorn, but the Black Wolf hunts there too. The forests belong to the Grizzly Bear, the Boar and the Spider. Swamps are another story: Mosshorn, Marsh Snake, Fen Stalker and Piranha in the rivers. The hills have Goat and Hawk. Undead such as the Skeleton Fighter roam the whole zone, while the Skeleton Mage prefers the swamps.

Peoples. Kweebecs and Trorks share Zone 1. The generator has versions of the same plains and forests "with Kweebec" and "with Trork", and that decides who you meet in each stretch. Both peoples get their own article in Factions of Hytale.

Resources. Zone 1's underground holds Copper Ore, Iron Ore, Silver Ore and Gold Ore.

Structures. Goblin-infested caves with Goblin Thief and Rat, the lair of the Goblin Duke, earth-aligned mage towers, the earth elemental circle, abandoned mines and a dedicated graveyard environment.

Zone 2: Howling Sands

Zone 2 starts mild and ends hostile. Golden Steppes has boab savanna, scrubland, plateaus and mudflats. Badlands adds red and rocky deserts, hot springs, hidden oases and tar pits. Desolate Basin closes the zone with barren deserts and a mushroom desert.

Creatures. On the savanna: Antelope, Warthog, Meerkat, Hyena and the Sabertooth Tiger. In the desert: Camel, Sand Lizard, Cactee and the Scarak Seeker. On plateaus and scrubland: Armadillo, Vulture, Scorpion, Skrill and Rattlesnake. The oasis has Crocodile, Flamingo and Tortoise. The dead here are sand skeletons such as the Sandswept Skeleton Guard, the Sandswept Skeleton Assassin and the Sandswept Skeleton Mage.

Peoples. This is the land of the Ferans and the Scarak hives. In the 2019 desert key art, the tower looming behind the player started out as a dungeon and became a Scarak nest.

Resources. Besides copper, iron, silver and gold, Zone 2 is the only one with Thorium Ore. The Thorium Ingot description says the Ferans keep watch over these deposits, saved for the day old enemies rise again.

Structures. Air-aligned mage towers, Scarak hives, ancient tombs, outposts and a trading post in the hardest region.

Zone 3: Whisperfrost Frontiers

In 2019 Hypixel described Zone 3 as forested valleys, tall mountains and deep caves. Each zone has an affinity with an element, and this one's is water in all its forms: river, snow and ice. The same post called the region a place of natural beauty that hosted some of the darkest moments in Orbis' history.

Frostmarch Tundra has fir, redwood, tundra and hot springs. Boreal Reach swaps that for cedar, frozen plains and glaciers. The Everfrost is pure ice, with frozen forests and the Outlander villages.

Creatures. In the forests: Moose Bull, Mouflon, Grizzly Bear and Cobra. On the ice: White Wolf, Polar Bear, Snow Leopard, Penguin, Bison, Snowy Owl and Frost Snail. Caves and cold waters hold Snapjaw, Frostgill and Trilobite. Among the dead are the Frost Skeleton Knight and the Frost Skeleton Mage.

Peoples. Outlanders such as the Outlander Berserker and the Outlander Hunter rule the far end of the zone. Kweebecs live in the redwood forests, and there are Trork camps in the hardest region. One of Zone 3's lake biomes is named after The Hedera, with its own environment and music.

Resources. Zone 3 is the only one with Cobalt Ore. According to the Cobalt Ingot, Outlanders say this metal stays cold even when molten.

Structures. Ancient battlefields, quarries, ice-aligned mage towers and the Outlander dungeon. In the caves, bioluminescent mushrooms light the way.

Zone 4: Devastated Lands

The last zone is a portrait of a catastrophe. Cinder Wastes has ghost forests, geyser wastes, swamps and even abandoned towns and villages. In Charred Woodlands the fire has already won: ash deserts, burned forests, root forests, lava fields and volcanoes.

The files also list environments with names that stand out: Crucible, sewers, villages, volcanoes and jungles. Crucible is the city the game's texts describe as destroyed by a cursed flame. Its story is in Gaia, Varyn and the Void.

Creatures. In the volcanoes: Emberwulf, Magma Rhino Toad, Magma Slug, Magma Snail, Burnt Zombie and the Living Spark. In the wastes: the Wraith, the Skeleton Horse, the Undead Cow and the Burnt Skeleton Lancer. In the dead forests: the Bleached Hound, the Undead Pig and the Incandescent Skeleton Head. And in the jungles, prehistoric beasts: Cave Rex, Cave Raptor, Trillodon, Pterodactyl, Archaeopteryx and the Snapdragon.

Resources. Iron, silver and Adamantite Ore, which only appears here.

Structures. Villages, outposts and fire-aligned mage towers. The zone's soundtrack has a track called "Sloth Village", which points to the villages of the Slothian.

Oceans

The seas come in three climates: cold, temperate and warm. Each one mixes kelp forests, reefs, trenches, stone pillars, islands and even sunken cities. In open water swim the Humpback Whale, the Hammerhead Shark, the Moray Eel and the Salmon. Near the beaches you find jellyfish such as the Man Of War, plus Clownfish and Lobster. Shipwrecks are part of the scenery too.

When night falls

Zones 1, 2 and 3 share one danger: at night, in dark places, Void creatures appear. Void Larva, Void Crawler and Void Eye show up in all three. In the hardest regions, such as swamps, deserts, oases and glaciers, the Void Spectre joins them. At dawn, they vanish.

Structures and dungeons

Beyond what belongs to each zone, the game files include several kinds of structures spread across the world:

  • The Forgotten Temple, the heart of progression, home to The Heart of Orbis
  • Mage towers of four elements: earth, air, ice and fire
  • Elemental circles of earth, fire, frost, poison and sand, combat arenas Hypixel showed in 2019
  • Dungeons such as the goblin lair, the Outlander temple, the cursed crypt, the labyrinth, the magic ruins, the rift and the sewers
  • Outposts and camps of varying tiers in every zone
  • Mines, shipwrecks and treasure rooms

The Fragments of Orbis

With the Ancient Gateway, you open portals to the Fragments of Orbis, unstable and temporary worlds. Each key leads somewhere different, and its description drops a hint:

  • Fragment: Orbis - Windrider Valley: the windy valley where the Ferans hid during the darkest years of their history
  • Fragment: Orbis - Tal'Kesi Refuge: a primordial haven hidden beneath lands ruined by an ancient civilization
  • Fragment: Orbis - Dragonspire Weald: frozen forests not even Outlanders enter, with a mythical beast asleep inside
  • Fragment: Orbis - The Dread Wade: the swamp of The Hedera
  • Fragment: Orbis - Silent Henges: stone circles over a frozen land

These fragments are not just decoration. In January 2026, world generation engineer Dan explained that content from the new generator, V2, would first be visited through these portals while the new Orbis is being built.

What exists today and what comes next

Early Access runs Exploration mode, called Echoes of Orbis, on the old generator, V1. That is what creates the four zones and the oceans described above. V2 is being used to rebuild Orbis from scratch. When it is ready, according to Hypixel, players will step into the new world of Orbis, and old worlds will remain accessible.

Chapter 1 is arriving in waves through the pre-release since Update 7. It brings the goblin world of The Guttering Hollows, opened by an event called the Goblin Breach, hunts for Void-corrupted bears such as the Voidtaken Grizzly, and the rune system. All of it is still in development and may change before release.
