Hytale's chapters: what they are and what comes after the goblins

Chapter 1 is arriving in waves through Update 7. What chapters are, what has been shown and what Hypixel Studios plans for Chapter 2.

Hytale's Early Access has a map, and it is called a chapter. Each chapter is a big content drop that pushes the Cursebreaker arc forward. The first one, built around the goblins, is arriving piece by piece through the Update 7 pre-release. Below is everything Hypixel Studios has said about the format, about Chapter 1 and about what comes next. Anything that is a plan, and not finished content, is flagged as a plan.

What a chapter is

The official definition arrived with the Update 3 patch notes on 17 February 2026. Beyond regular updates, the studio is building chapters: larger content drops that unfold the Cursebreaker arc inside Exploration Mode. The game moves forward chapter by chapter until the last one puts Orbis within reach and Hytale steps into full release.

In May, the Update 5 notes drew a clear line between the two kinds of release:

  • Updates bring systems, tooling and polish between the bigger releases. They ship after weeks of pre-release.
  • Chapters are the big moments, with new content, new mechanics and the Cursebreaker lore slowly kicking in.

The studio has not said how many chapters there will be. What it has given is a time frame. When it announced the game's return in November 2025, Hypixel Studios said Early Access should last at least a few years, with no rush to reach 1.0.

The chapter screen

Every chapter will have objectives. According to the July preview, the goal is for the chapter screen to work as a roadmap of your progress through Early Access. You complete objectives by playing, finish the chapter and unlock cosmetics as rewards. For Chapter 1, those rewards are goblin cosmetics.

Why the content took a while

Anyone playing since 13 January noticed it: the first updates were mostly fixes and groundwork. That was a choice. In the "First Look: Chapter 1 and More" post from 16 July 2026, Simon Collins-Laflamme explained that after buying the game back, the team had to return to a build more than four years old. Most of the work since then went into catching up, rebuilding tools and growing the team. Simon even admitted there were not many reasons to come back and play at that point.

The original plan was to keep each chapter hidden until a full reveal. He changed course because of the project's history: asking the community to wait in the dark again did not feel right. The saga that led here is covered in the history of Hytale.

Chapter 1: the goblin chapter

The studio picked the goblins to open Early Access because the systems being built lined up with them. Their tech is janky on purpose, and Hypixel Studios calls them a fun faction to start with.

It all starts at the Forgotten Temple

The Forgotten Temple has been in the game since Early Access launched, as the place where the first pieces of the Cursebreaker arc show up. In Chapter 1, your journey starts inside it. From now on, the temple becomes the hub for your progress through the chapters, and it should change with every release.

The pre-release files show the temple is treated as a place of its own. It has its own instance, Forgotten_Temple, an entrance, the Forgotten Temple Gateway, and its own gameplay rules: you cannot break, gather or place blocks there, and neither players nor NPCs take damage.

The Goblin Breach

Once you answer the chapter's opening call at the temple, a new world event starts to trigger from time to time: the Goblin Breach. It opens a temporary portal somewhere in your world. You can find it by looking around or checking the map.

On the other side is an unstable fragment of Orbis, a set of biomes taken over by the goblins, with a growing Void presence. That world is regenerated every time you enter, sometimes with things you have not seen yet. The Void keeps closing in until it throws you back out. The goal is to explore, find good loot and push back to stay as long as possible.

The studio listed the first events and activities in that world:

  • Predators you can track and trap.
  • Void assassins that hunt you down.
  • Treasure chests that spawn enemy waves when you go for the loot.
  • Puzzles that lock off places you cannot simply dig into.

The idea did not come out of nowhere. Since launch, the Ancient Gateway has taken players to temporary worlds called Fragments of Orbis, such as the one opened by the Fragment: Orbis - The Dread Wade, the lair of The Hedera. The Goblin Breach brings that format into an event inside your own world.

The first dungeon and the first boss

Chapter 1 brings Hytale's first handcrafted dungeon, with the first multi-phase boss fight. The tech behind it is the Encounter Manager, which coordinates phases, enemy waves and arena changes. The key detail for modders: encounters are authored as content, not as engine code. That means the team can keep making them quickly, and so can the community.

The studio has not named the boss. The official video is called "Goblin Boss Preview". The pre-release files already contain an instance called Dungeon_Goblin, and the Goblin Duke shows up with per-phase variants, mentioned in the Update 7 notes.

The goblin faction

Goblins come in many shapes and sizes, with different roles and personalities. They hang out in groups and, even when they look disorganized, something is always going on under the chaos. Some are elites with tougher fights. The studio's example is the Goblin Feastmaster, which calls in reinforcements mid-fight, charges at you and wrecks its surroundings.

Not every goblin wants you dead. Some are friendly, and one of them runs a shop. In the files, that role appears as the Goblin Merchant.

In the pre-release, the team reworked six goblins: the Goblin Scrapper, the Goblin Miner, the Goblin Guardian, the Goblin Lobber, the Goblin Burner and the Goblin Feastmaster. In Part 2 they became enemies of Void creatures such as the Void Spectre. Health, drops and recipes for each one are in the Update 7 guide. Where the goblins fit among the peoples of Orbis is covered in Hytale's factions.

Runes, gadgets and boats

The rune ability system is one of the centerpieces. An ability rune decides which ability you get, and modifier runes change what it does or how it behaves. The studio said it intends to start Chapter 1 with roughly six ability runes and a similar number of modifier runes, bound to the E and R keys. The pre-release already has five ability runes, slotted into the Runebinder's Lattice: Enrage, Fireball, Imbue Poison, Ground Slam and Wind Strike. Runeforging, the system for finding, crafting and expanding your rune collection, comes later. Enchantments are not planned for Chapter 1.

The goblins also bring gadgets: Rocket Boots, Hookshot, Scrap Glider, Scrap Drill and Spyglass. Outside the goblin theme, the preview showed a basic boat, and the files already include the Wooden Boat. The chapter also adds a mechanic for carrying large objects, such as chests and the Goblin Boom Barrel, plus real transparency, which makes stained glass possible.

When Chapter 1 releases

On 16 July, the estimate was "within the next 2-3 months", with an exact date to be announced closer to release. On 3 September, the Update 7 pre-release notes called the package the first wave of Chapter 1 content, the first of several over the following weeks. At this stage the content is mostly reached through Creative Mode, and the rune system is still expected to change a lot. As of Part 3 of the notes, on 17 September, no official date had been announced.

What Hypixel Studios plans for the next chapters

Chapter 2: new crafting and companions

The July preview was direct: Chapter 2 should bring the crafting rework and Companions, "if all goes well".

  • Crafting: the studio said it intends to reduce the number of crafting benches and move part of the work into in-world activities, such as wood chopping and metalworking. Crafting areas are meant to grow and change with each player's choices.
  • Companions: characters you invite to move into your home. You invest some resources to make them comfortable, and afterwards they help with a range of tasks. Each has its own traits and quirks, works on its own schedule and routine, and some are better suited to certain jobs.

The stated goal is for you to build a village with your companions, a place that feels alive. The prototype works, but it still needs effects, animations, models and a lot of tech.

Further out

Other plans mentioned in the preview, with no chapter attached:

  • The UI will be reworked gradually over the next few chapters.
  • Every faction, including the Kweebecs, will be reworked in future chapters. Work has already started with new behaviors for the Kweebec Seedling and the Kweebec Sapling.
  • Livelier animals: the Fox should follow you and sleep by the Campfire, and birds such as the Brown Owl hunt smaller creatures.
  • Runeforging, to give runes more depth.
  • Hardcore challenge modes that go beyond permadeath.
  • Carrying a downed friend, using the same mechanic as carrying objects. The studio said this comes after Chapter 1.

Zones and story: still under wraps

Hypixel Studios has not announced which zones or factions will headline the chapters after the second. On the Cursebreaker plot, the official line is in the Update 3 notes: the studio will not spoil the story. What is known is that the whole world is being redesigned with WorldGen V2, work the studio itself calls much bigger than the goblin chapter. The complete, restored Orbis as the default world has not arrived yet. What is known about the regions is in the world of Orbis, and the story's background is in Gaia, Varyn and the Void.

Chapters and Adventure Mode

Hytale has promised an Adventure Mode since 2018. The announcement FAQ described it as a journey through a procedurally generated fantasy world, solo or with friends. At the Early Access launch on 13 January 2026, the studio listed Adventure Mode among the things not yet in the game, with story, deeper progression and dungeons with boss fights.

The chapters are delivering exactly those pieces, but inside Exploration Mode. The first handcrafted dungeon, the first boss fight and the start of the story all arrive through Chapter 1. In November 2025, Hypixel Studios said it intends to shift hard into Adventure Mode and minigames once the world feels alive and the systems are stable. The studio has not yet explained whether Adventure Mode will exist as a separate mode or whether the chapters are the road to it. How each mode works today is covered in Hytale's game modes.

Chapter timeline

  • 17 Nov 2025: Hypixel Studios buys Hytale back and says minigames and Adventure Mode will come after launch.
  • 13 Jan 2026: Early Access begins, with the Forgotten Temple and the first hints of the Cursebreaker arc.
  • 17 Feb 2026: Update 3 defines chapters as Early Access's road to full release.
  • 26 May 2026: Update 5 separates Updates from Chapters and confirms Chapter 1 is in production.
  • 16 Jul 2026: the "First Look" post shows goblins, the dungeon, runes and gadgets, and previews Chapter 2.
  • 27 Aug 2026: Update 6 uses Chapter 1 blocks in a showcase.
  • 3 Sep 2026: the Update 7 pre-release brings the first wave of Chapter 1 content.

Everything in the pre-release can still change before the chapter officially launches.
