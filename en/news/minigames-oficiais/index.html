Hytale's official minigames: from the 2018 promise to 14 party games

The studio came from Hypixel, yet Hytale has no official minigame. Everything announced, the 14 micro-games shown in July and what is missing.

Hytale was born inside the Hypixel team, the people behind Minecraft's biggest minigame server. Even so, as of September 2026 the game has no official minigames. The promise dates back to 2018, disappeared from the Early Access launch and came back strong in July 2026, when Hypixel Studios showed its first fast party games. Here is what has been announced, with sources, and what is still missing.

Where the expectation comes from

The FAQ published with the game's announcement on 13 December 2018 explained where the studio came from. The Hytale team had split off from the Hypixel server team more than two years earlier. Hypixel Inc. kept running the server, and Hypixel Studios was formed to make Hytale. The same FAQ promised the Hypixel server would stay active and that no progress from it would carry over to the new game.

In that FAQ, Hytale had three pillars:

  • Adventure Mode: a journey through a procedurally generated fantasy world, solo or with friends.
  • Minigames: session-based experiences to test your skills in PvP and co-op.
  • Creative tools for every kind of creator.

The studio said the game was built from the ground up with minigames in mind. That would allow almost anything, "even a top-down shooter", plus anti-cheat and client optimizations made for minigames. About the trailer, the FAQ admitted PvP was only touched on lightly, on purpose, because the team wanted community input first.

The announcement trailer passed 32 million views in a month, according to the official blog in January 2019.

The 2019 game page

The 2019 "The Game" page had a whole section for minigame players, titled "From the team behind Hypixel". It said the team had been making the most popular minigames around for more than five years and promised:

  • Anti-cheat built in from the start, to make multiplayer fairer.
  • Tools with full control over camera position, game assets and basic mechanics for minigame creators.
  • Parental controls to limit online play and custom content.
  • "A wide range of official Hytale servers", alongside community servers.

The same page showed a row of game mode icons, six drawn and two with question marks, with no names. Fans linked the drawings to well-known Hypixel modes such as SkyWars and Bed Wars, in lists on the Hypixel forums. Hypixel Studios never published an official list with names.

The 2019 server browser

In January 2019, the post about server technology described an in-game browser. The idea was to filter by type of game, such as adventure mode or minigames, co-op or competitive, with tags and favorites. The browser would be moderated, and every operator, "from large minigame networks to smaller community servers", would follow a lightweight terms of service.

Then came the Riot Games years, starting in 2020, and the cancellation in 2025. That part of the story is in the history of Hytale. One record remains from the Riot period: the April 2020 FAQ talked about exploring minigames and events with other universes and included minigame images.

2025: minigames miss the launch

When it bought the game back on 17 November 2025, Hypixel Studios was blunt. Official minigames would not be in the Early Access launch. The studio acknowledged they were a big part of the original vision, but said the infrastructure and the games were far from ready. A dedicated team would handle them once the core game was stable. In the meantime, the community could build its own minigames, and the studio said it intends to share its assets when the official ones launch.

Three days later, in the modding post, technical director Slikey detailed the plan for a first-party server network. Hypixel Studios plans to launch, after Early Access begins, a network run by an internal team with classic Hypixel Network-style minigames. At the time, work on it had not even started. The stated reasons:

  • Bring back familiar minigames, rebuilt for Hytale, and create new ones only Hytale allows.
  • Use the same tools modders use, to improve minigame creation, live ops and server management.
  • Learn how Hytale servers behave at scale and lower hosting costs for everyone.
  • Release part or all of the minigames' code and assets so the community can study and reuse them.
  • Make sure there is playable content early on, until community servers ramp up.

The studio also said what it does not want: to outcompete community servers, to pull focus from Adventure Mode, which would have its own team, or to bias the roadmap around its own servers.

In the first FAQ, on 3 December 2025, minigames came up next to money. The studio said it may offer extra cosmetics when it introduces minigames, and never pay-to-win. It also promised a 0% cut for two years for servers and modders who monetize their work, and a cap of 20% after that.

On 13 January 2026, at launch, the list of things not yet in the game included "Official Minigames", described as fun social games and competitive multiplayer modes. Work would begin in the coming months.

The pieces that arrived along the way

No official minigame has shipped yet, but several updates laid the groundwork:

  • Update 4 (26 March): the studio said voice chat, emotes and the social improvements are the first pieces of a much larger social layer that includes minigames. In the same notes, Cybercity blocks might stay Creative-only or become part of a minigame.
  • Update 5 (26 May): Server Discovery arrived. When listing a server, "Minigames" is one of the nine available types. A developer also showed a tower defense interface as an example of a custom server UI.
  • Update 6 (27 August): spectator mode arrived with Hardcore. The notes say it also prepares the ground for minigames. In the July preview, the studio explained that a minigame server can drop eliminated players straight into spectating without writing a single line of camera code.
  • Cubic worlds: cubic chunk tech allows "infinite" height and depth. The studio said it probably will not use that in Exploration Mode, but minigames and servers will.

What was shown in July 2026

The "First Look: Chapter 1 and More" post on 16 July 2026 gave the first concrete look. The studio is building its first experience outside Exploration and Creative: a collection of minigames to play with friends. The work was shown alongside Chapter 1, but it has its own timeline.

  • Party micro-games. The first package is a collection of fast games. At the time of writing, there were 14.
  • Short rounds. Each one lasts 60 to 120 seconds. Variety is the point, because the team uses these games to push Hytale's systems in crazy directions and evolve the tech.
  • A tab of its own. The plan is a Minigames tab: open Hytale, hit Minigames and be in a lobby with friends seconds later. Hosting can be peer-to-peer or dedicated.
  • More formats later. Long term, the studio said it wants persistent games, PvP games and much more. This first package is about exploring and building strong foundations.
  • Creators invited. Modders and server operators will be invited to make minigames with the studio.
  • An open recipe. Hypixel Studios said it will share the code and assets behind all minigames. That includes libraries with a simpler API for the server and a toolkit of paradigms shaped by 13 years of the Hypixel Server, with more than 45 million unique players.

The estimate given in the post was "within the next 2-3 months". The images show arenas with colorful floors, race lanes and quick player clashes, but the names of the 14 games were not revealed.

Where things stand now

As of September 2026, official minigames have not arrived. The three parts of the Update 7 pre-release notes, published on 3, 10 and 17 September, focus on Chapter 1 and do not mention minigames. No date has been announced. The official game page still shows the minigames section as "under construction" and says official servers "may come later".

Anyone who wants minigames today relies on the community. Minigame servers show up in Server Discovery, and Orbis Hytale's server list mirrors that official catalog. How to build and host one is covered in mods, servers and tools.

What is still unknown

  • The names of the 14 micro-games.
  • Whether classic Hypixel modes return, and which ones. The studio talks about "classic Hypixel Network-style minigames" but has never listed modes.
  • Whether the Minigames tab and the first-party server network are the same thing.
  • The release date.
  • How minigame-related cosmetics will work.

The modes already in the game are explained in Hytale's game modes.
