After Chapter 1: what Hypixel promised and what the game files are hiding

Official schedule, art-only goblins and dungeons on hold: what could come to Hytale, with fact, game files and guesswork kept apart.

This is a theory: speculation based on official information, marked as such throughout the text. Nothing here is confirmed by Hypixel Studios unless a source says so.

Nobody outside Hypixel Studios knows when the next update lands. But you can get close. The studio has already published a rough schedule, the pre-release shows Chapter 1 arriving in waves, and the game files hold creatures, dungeons and weather that players can't see yet.

Every piece of information here carries a label:

  • Official: said by Hypixel, with a link to the source.
  • In the files: exists in the game files but was never announced. Being in the files does not mean it will ship.
  • Theory: our reading or the community's. It can be wrong.

The schedule Hypixel already gave

Official: on July 16, game director Simon published the Chapter 1 first look with a rough schedule. Chapter 1 was listed for the next two to three months. Minigames, carryable objects and the new Machinima had the same window. The crafting rework was pushed to Chapter 2. Runeforging, volumetric clouds and the new Asset Editor got no date.

Official: the same post promises a precise date for Chapter 1 once release gets close. As of September 24, that date has not been announced. Machinima is meant to be used to record a teaser or trailer for the chapter.

Official: since September 3, the Update 7 pre-release carries the first wave of Chapter 1 content. The notes say it is the first of several waves over the coming weeks, and that the rune system will still change a lot.

Theory: history says be patient. In the July post, spectator mode, Hardcore and the mod browser were two to three weeks away. All three shipped in Update 6 on August 27, about six weeks later. Going by the post's own math, Chapter 1 would land between mid-September and mid-October. A similar delay would not be a surprise.

Chapter 1: what is confirmed

Official, according to the July first look:

  • The first handcrafted dungeon, with the first multi-phase boss. The phases, waves and arena changes are run by the Encounter Manager, which creators can already use.
  • The goblin faction, with elite types like the Goblin Feastmaster, which calls in reinforcements mid-fight.
  • The Goblin Breach, an event that opens temporary portals to unstable fragments of Orbis, taken over by goblins and by an advancing Void. The Forgotten Temple becomes the hub of your progression through the chapters.
  • Gadgets: Rocket Boots, Hookshot, Scrap Glider, Scrap Drill and Spyglass. Plus boats, still basic.
  • Objects you carry in your arms, like chests and the Goblin Boom Barrel.
  • Runes: roughly six ability runes and a similar number of modifier runes at first, on the E and R keys.
  • Real transparency, with stained glass and windows.
  • Goblin cosmetics, unlocked by completing the chapter.

Official: the Update 3 notes explain the logic. Chapters tell the Cursebreaker arc, and the last one puts Orbis within reach and marks the game's full release. Update 5 adds: updates bring systems and polish, chapters bring content and story.

Goblins that haven't shown up yet

Official: Simon wrote that not every goblin wants you dead. Some are friendly, and one of them runs a shop.

In the files: the September 3 pre-release added three goblin looks that no creature uses:

  • Goblin Merchant: it has a configured model, with an apron, a monocle, a magnifying glass, scrap earrings and a boot for a hat. There is no NPC role or shop tied to it.
  • Goblin Makinomagistra: art only. A mechanical arm with a wrench, a metal leg, magnifying goggles, electrified hair, a lampshade skirt and a backpack made from an explosive mushroom.
  • Voidtaken Goblin: art only. A hood, a tunic, bandaged feet and eyes marked by the Void.

Theory: the merchant lines up with the goblin shop promised in July. The scientist fits a people that, in Simon's words, are tinkerers whose inventions end up in your hands. And the Void goblin follows the path of the Voidtaken Grizzly, which got its own look in the pre-release: a familiar creature, taken by the Void. None of this was announced.

In the files: the Goblin Duke, a goblin with three fight phases already in the stable build, had all of its phases moved to a folder called _Deprecated in the pre-release. Theory: the first dungeon's boss is probably someone else. The boss image Hypixel released, used as this article's cover, shows a huge scrap robot surrounded by goblins.

After Chapter 1

Official, also from the July first look:

  • Chapter 2: the crafting rework, with fewer benches and more work done in the world, like chopping wood and metalworking. And Companions, NPCs who move into your home and help with chores, each with their own quirks and schedules. The post says "if all goes well".
  • Runeforging: finding, crafting and growing your rune collection. No date.
  • Memories and profiles: a new Memories screen and player profile cards, for after Chapter 1.
  • Rescue: the same carry mechanic will let you pick up a downed friend. It comes after Chapter 1.
  • Hardcore: challenge modes that build on the current idea.
  • Boats: proper boat mechanics later on.
  • Minigames: 14 micro-games lasting 60 to 120 seconds, with their own menu tab. Hypixel promises to share the code and assets.
  • Enchantments: not planned for Chapter 1.

Official: at the Early Access launch, Hypixel listed what was still missing: Adventure Mode, with story, deeper progression and dungeons with bosses; official minigames; the complete world on WorldGen V2; and social features like guilds. The friends list arrived in Update 5. Guilds have not.

In the files: the Items/Hypixel/Minigames folder holds models for a shooter mode, with the Rifle, Handgun, Frag Grenade, Reflex Sight, ACOG Scope and Rifle Red Laser, plus the Team Cape and Team Tunic. They have been there since the stable build. Theory: they could be leftovers from old prototypes or groundwork for the promised minigames. There is no way to tell.

The new world of WorldGen V2

Official: the January world generation post explains that Early Access runs on the V1 generator and that V2 will replace it once Orbis is ready. In July, the community spotlight added more: V1 worlds stay playable but stop generating new terrain, and they can no longer be created after Early Access. The same post says something is brewing that will affect the Echo, the current world.

Official: V2 should bring more advanced procedural caves and, later, procedural dungeons, coherent villages and cities, rivers and roads. The level design team has doubled in size.

In the files: the Deeproot biome, shown in the January post, entered the pre-release on September 3 with its own environment and weather. It is already part of the V2 generator's Zone1_Plains1 world structure.

In the files: there is a fragment instance called Portals_Oasis, with its own environment, but no key and no portal type. Today there are five fragments with keys: Fragment: Orbis - The Dread Wade, Fragment: Orbis - Silent Henges, Fragment: Orbis - Tal'Kesi Refuge, Fragment: Orbis - Dragonspire Weald and Fragment: Orbis - Windrider Valley. Theory: the Howling Sands oasis Hypixel showed in January is a good candidate for the next fragment.

In the files: the pre-release also added the Env_Temple_Of_Gaia environment, for a Temple of Gaia, and four goblin portal environments: surface, cave, deep cave and Void cave. Those four match the announced Goblin Breach. The Temple of Gaia is not in any announcement.

Dungeons, encounters and weather on hold

In the files, already in the stable build:

  • Four dungeon instances: Dungeon_1, Dungeon_Goblin, Dungeon_Outlander and Challenge_Combat_1. The goblin one only has a resources folder. The Outlander one already has saved terrain.
  • Weather that never shows up in the normal world: Blood_Moon, four Poisonlands weather types and six Skylands ones, with names like Mushroom Forest, Mystic Forest and Rapid Marsh.

Official: the Blood Moon appears in the Update 7 notes, but only as an example for creators. The Example_Boss encounter forces a Blood Moon in phase 1 and clears the sky when the boss dies.

In the files, pre-release only: the Hunt_Site_D1, Hunt_Site_D2 and Hunt_Site_D3 encounters, numbered like the three versions of the Void bear, and a series of Void anomaly encounters. Official: the first look promised predators you can track and trap and Void assassins that hunt you down.

Theory: Skylands and Poisonlands sound like new zones, but the world generation post warned that the floating islands shown there were only experimental feature packs, unrelated to Adventure content. Treat those names as drafts. An Outlander dungeon, on the other hand, would make sense: since 2019, Hypixel has tied that faction to the history of Zone 3.

Creatures and gear that exist only as art

In the files, in the stable build:

  • Exceptional Trork: a Trork with its own eyes and eyebrows and four haircuts, including a mohawk, braids and a samurai cut.
  • Trork Hatchling: a baby Trork.
  • Void Dragon and Void Necromancer: configured models with no NPC role to put them in the world.
  • Armor with no item: the Ninja, Orbis Knight, White Mage and Necromancer sets, plus capes like Borean and Raven. Of the Orbis Knight set, only the Ancient Knight's Shield became an item.
  • A shuriken, a banjo and a brush.

Official: in the January lore post, Hypixel noted that a lot of old art was left behind. In the world generation post, Simon repeated that not every concept will ship. Theory: some of these models are leftovers from years of development. Others might be the next chapter. The files don't say which is which.

What past updates teach us

Theory, based on the track record:

  • The asset shows up before the content. The Scrap Flamethrower arrived in Update 3 as Creative-only and became the Goblin Burner's weapon in Update 7. The art-only goblins could follow the same path.
  • Pre-releases got longer. Update 5 took ten weeks and Update 6 took thirteen. The gap between stable updates went from weeks, in January, to months.
  • Tools come before dungeons. The Trigger Volume from Update 5 and the Encounter Manager reached creators before any official dungeon. Simon said each encounter they build expands the toolbox for the next one.
  • Estimates slip. What was three weeks away arrived in six.

How to read all this

Hytale is in Early Access, and Hypixel keeps repeating that almost everything can change. What is marked official has a source. What is in the files exists today, but can vanish tomorrow. And theories are just that. When Chapter 1 ships, come back here and check what held up.
