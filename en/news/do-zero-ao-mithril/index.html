From zero to Mithril: the Hytale progression route

The order of pickaxes, benches and ingots in Hytale, from level 5 to 50, with recipes and levels taken from the game files.

In Hytale, the pickaxe drives progression. Each new material needs a tool that already breaks the previous ore, and each tool needs a better bench. The numbers below (item level, recipe and bench tier) come from the wiki's data/items.json, extracted from the game files.

Phase 1: by hand (level 5)

  • The Crude Pickaxe is hand crafted with 2 rubble, 2 Plant Fiber and 2 Stick.
  • With 4 logs and 3 rocks, also by hand, you get the Workbench.
  • The bench makes the Furnace (6 logs and 6 rocks) and the Tanning Rack (6 logs, 3 rocks and 3 Light Hide).

Phase 2: Copper (level 12)

  • At the Furnace, 1 Copper Ore becomes 1 Copper Ingot.
  • The Copper Pickaxe takes 3 copper ingots, 6 logs and 1 fibre at the Workbench.
  • 2 copper ingots, 10 logs and 5 rocks make the Armorer's Workbench.

Phase 3: Iron (level 20)

  • The Iron Pickaxe takes 5 Iron Ingot, 2 Light Leather and 2 Linen Scraps.
  • Linen scraps drop 100% from Trorks such as the Trork Warrior. Light leather comes from small animal hides, like the Chicken, tanned at the rack.

Phase 4: Thorium and Cobalt (levels 30 and 35)

The Workbench must be tier 2 here.

  • Thorium Pickaxe: 6 Thorium Ingot, 2 Medium Leather and 3 linen scraps.
  • Cobalt Pickaxe: 7 Cobalt Ingot, 2 Heavy Leather and 4 Shadoweave Scraps.
  • Shadoweave scraps drop from Outlanders such as the Outlander Brute. Heavy hide comes from bears like the Grizzly Bear.

Phase 5: Adamantite (level 40)

From here on the Workbench needs tier 3. The Adamantite Pickaxe takes 8 Adamantite Ingot, 3 heavy leather and 4 Cindercloth Scraps, dropped by burnt skeletons.

Phase 6: Mithril (level 50)

The Mithril Pickaxe is the only craftable epic pickaxe: 9 Mithril Ingot and 4 Storm Leather, made from Storm Hide at the rack. It breaks copper, iron, silver and gold ore with power 1, twice the Adamantite pickaxe.
