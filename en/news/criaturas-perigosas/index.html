Hytale’s dangerous creatures: health, drops and how to beat them

The threats of all four zones and the Void night, with each one’s health, drops and spawn hours, plus what the official notes teach about combat.

Knowing who attacks, where and how much damage they take before going down prevents most silly deaths. The health values and drops below come from each creature's page on the wiki. Locations and times come from the game's spawn files, and behavior tips from the official update notes. The full list is in creatures.

How to read this guide

  • Health is the total number of points the creature can take.
  • In the files, most surface predators spawn between 6 AM and 6 PM. Wandering skeletons spawn at any hour, and Void creatures only appear at night.
  • Not every predator attacks on sight. Since Update 1, the cautious ones, like the Fox, only attack if you strike first.

Zone 1: Emerald Wilds

The starting zone. In the plains, woods and mountains:

  • Black Wolf: 103 health. It roams in small packs on the plains and, like the swamp predators, also spawns at night, until midnight. Since Update 4, the pack tries to flank its prey and backs off when hurt. Drops Raw Wildmeat and 1 to 2 Medium Hide.
  • Grizzly Bear: 124 health, in forests and mountains. In Update 5 its hitbox grew to cover more of the head, so aim high. Drops 1 to 2 Heavy Hide and 2 to 3 meat.
  • Spider: 60 health, in forests. Drops Venom Sac, which you will need for the Alchemist's Workbench.
  • Marsh Snake (36 health) and Fen Stalker (74) guard the swamps.
  • Trorks live in camps. The Trork Warrior has 61 health and the Trork Chieftain 124. All of them drop 1 to 3 Linen Scraps, and each Trork armor piece has a 1.3% chance. The Trork Hunter usually comes with a Hunting Wolf.

Skeletons wander the whole zone at any hour. The Skeleton Fighter has just 36 health. The swamps add the Skeleton Mage, at 49, which drops 15 to 30 Green Crystal Shards, and the Burnt Skeleton Praetorian, with 226 health. It is worth the fight: 3 to 6 Essence of Fire, a 70% chance of 1 to 3 Thorium Ingot, 30% for Cobalt Ingot and 50% for the Grimoire: Rekindle Embers.

In caves:

  • Cave Spider: 80 health.
  • Goblin Miner: 100 health. Shows up alongside rats and spiders.
  • Abandoned mineshafts mix the Skeleton Soldier (61), the Skeleton Mage and spiders.
  • Volcanic caves bring the Magma Rhino Toad (124), which alternates tongue and headbutt since Update 2, and the Firesteel Golem (224), which drops 2 to 5 Essence of Fire and 1 to 2 Iron Ingot.

In elemental circles, the Root Spirit has 49 health and drops 2 to 3 Essence of Life.

Zone 2: Howling Sands

  • Sabertooth Tiger: 124 health, in the desert, oasis and savanna.
  • Crocodile: 145 health, at oases. Drops 2 to 3 Heavy Hide.
  • Hyena: 103 health, on the savanna and plateaus.
  • Scorpion: 124 health. Drops Venom Sac and has a 50% chance of Sturdy Chitin.
  • Rattlesnake (61), Cactee (61), Scarak Seeker (61) and Vulture (61) round out the surface list.

The skeletons here are the Sandswept Skeleton Guard and the Sandswept Skeleton Assassin, with 61 health, and the Sandswept Skeleton Mage, with 49, which drops 15 to 30 Cyan Crystal Shards.

The Scarak hive deserves care. The Scarak Fighter has 81 health, the Scarak Defender 103 and the Scarak Broodmother 145. Since Update 5, the broodmother only spawns Scarak Louse when none are nearby, so clear the lice and go for her. Since Update 6, Scaraks are immune to poison and environmental hazards like brambles. Poisoned weapons do nothing here. The Sturdy Chitin they drop upgrades the Tanning Rack, the Alchemist's Workbench and the Armorer's Workbench.

In elemental circles, the Thunder Spirit has 249 health and drops Essence of Lightning.

Zone 3: Whisperfrost Frontiers

  • White Wolf: 103 health, on glaciers and mountains. Hunts in packs, like the Black Wolf.
  • Polar Bear: 103 health. It got stronger in Update 2. Drops 2 to 3 Heavy Hide.
  • Snow Leopard: 103 health. Drops 4 to 8 meat.
  • Cobra: 74 health, in forests and tundra.
  • Outlander Berserker (103) and Outlander Hunter (61), who attacks from range. Both drop 1 to 4 Shadoweave Scraps, the material for cobalt gear and tier 3 upgrades.

Frost skeletons range from the Skeleton Scout (61) to the Frost Skeleton Knight (74). The Frost Skeleton Fighter, the Frost Skeleton Soldier and the Frost Skeleton Mage drop Essence of Ice. The Frost Skeleton Ranger has a 2% chance to drop the Ancient Steel Hand Crossbow.

In volcanic caves, the Black Piranha (45) waits in the water. In elemental circles, the Frost Spirit has 81 health and drops Essence of Ice.

Zone 4: Devastated Lands

The toughest zone:

  • Cave Rex: 400 health, in the jungles. It is rare: in the files it has a weight of 1 against 10 for the other jungle creatures. Drops 2 to 3 Heavy Hide.
  • Cave Raptor: 103 health and speed 24, the highest on this list. Do not try to outrun it on foot.
  • Snapdragon: 103 health, also in the jungle.
  • Emberwulf: 193 health, around volcanoes. Drops Heavy Hide and Essence of Fire.
  • Wraith: 193 health, in the wastes. Drops 1 to 3 Cindercloth Scraps and 3 to 5 Essence of Fire.
  • Burnt Zombie (126), Bleached Hound (126), Burnt Skeleton Lancer (103), Incandescent Skeleton Head (81) and Rhino Toad (124) complete the zone.

The sewers under cities, which since Update 4 only generate there, hold the Zombie (49), the Burnt Skeleton Soldier (124) and the Burnt Skeleton Alchemist (103), which has a 10% chance to drop a Antidote.

This zone's Cindercloth Scraps goes into adamantite gear. And remember: since Update 1, fire creatures are immune to fire damage.

At night: the Void

Between 7 PM and 5 AM, in dark spots, Void creatures appear on the surface of the first three zones. They vanish at dawn, and the moon phase changes the odds for each one.

  • Void Larva: 36 health, in groups of 2 to 4.
  • Void Crawler: 74 health, in groups of 1 to 3.
  • Void Eye: 60 health. Drops 4 to 6 Essence of the Void and has a 15% chance of Voidheart.
  • Void Spectre: 30 health, only in the hardest areas, like swamps, deserts and glaciers. Drops 6 to 9 Essence of the Void.

Essence of the Void is what unlocks the Arcanist's Workbench, which needs 20. Voidheart goes into the last backpack upgrade and mithril weapons. A well-lit home keeps spawns away from your door.

The Update 7 goblins

On the pre-release, goblins were rebuilt, each with its own role. Health values come from the wiki:

  • Goblin Scrapper: 20 health, fights in groups.
  • Goblin Lobber: 50, throws bombs from range.
  • Goblin Burner: 80, uses a flamethrower. Drops 2 to 4 Linen Scraps.
  • Goblin Miner and Goblin Guardian: 100 each. The guardian blocks with a shield.
  • Goblin Feastmaster: 350. According to the official Chapter 1 preview, it calls reinforcements mid-fight and charges at you.

In Part 2 of the pre-release, goblins started fleeing Void creatures when outnumbered.

General combat tips

  • Eat before a fight. Cooked Wildmeat gives Health Regeneration I and Health Boost I for 45 seconds.
  • The Health Potion heals 25% instantly and another 35% after 5 seconds, but the effect is removed if you take damage. Drink away from the enemy.
  • Against spiders, snakes and scorpions, carry a Antidote. It cures poison and grants temporary immunity.
  • Since Update 6, stamina starts refilling half a second sooner after blocking with a weapon or shield.
  • Large predators attack spiders on sight, since Part 3 of the pre-release. Sometimes it pays to let them fight.
  • Skeletons do not drown, since Update 1. Luring one into water will not help.
