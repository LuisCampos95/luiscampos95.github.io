Hytale ore route: where and when to mine each one

Which rock each Hytale ore shows up in, which pickaxe breaks each one fastest and which creatures drop ore and ingots.

Every Hytale ore turns into an ingot at the Furnace, one for one. What changes is the rock it shows up in and how strong your pickaxe is against it. The power below is each pickaxe's gathering power on each ore in the game files: the higher, the fewer hits.

Which rock to look in

  • Copper Ore: stone, sandstone and shale. First on the route.
  • Iron Ore: stone, sandstone, shale, slate, basalt and volcanic rock.
  • Silver Ore and Gold Ore: stone, sandstone, shale, basalt and volcanic rock; silver also in slate, gold also in calcite.
  • Thorium Ore: dry mud and sandstone.
  • Cobalt Ore: shale and slate.
  • Adamantite Ore: magma.
  • Mithril Ore: stone.

Which pickaxe for each ore

The rule behind the table: each new pickaxe doubles the power on the previous tier's ore. Mining Thorium with the Iron Pickaxe works, but takes twice the hits of the Thorium Pickaxe.

The order

1. Copper with the Crude Pickaxe. Three ingots already pay for the Copper Pickaxe.
2. Iron, silver and gold with the copper one.
3. Thorium and cobalt with the iron one, until you have the thorium or cobalt pickaxe.
4. Adamantite, in magma, with the cobalt one.
5. Mithril with the Adamantite Pickaxe; with the Mithril Pickaxe, iron and gold break at power 1.

Ore without mining

Some creatures drop ore or ready-made ingots:

  • The Klops Miner drops Copper Ore (75%) and Iron Ore (25%).
  • The Klops Gentleman drops Copper Ingot (75%) and Iron Ingot (25%).
  • The Firesteel Golem always drops Iron Ingot.
  • The Burnt Skeleton Praetorian drops Thorium Ingot (70%) and Cobalt Ingot (30%).

Every ore has its own page under Items, listing who drops it and the recipes that use it.
