Hytale by the numbers: the complete timeline

From the Hypixel server in 2013 to Update 7 in pre-release: every Hytale date, record and update in one list for quick reference.

Every key date in Hytale's history on one page, from the Hypixel server to Update 7. Each milestone has an official source or a press report, listed at the end. The full story, with context and behind the scenes, is in The full Hytale saga.

Before the reveal

  • Around 2011: Simon Collins-Laflamme and Philippe Touchette start Hypixel with Minecraft adventure maps.
  • April 2013: the Hypixel server opens.
  • 2014: Mojang starts enforcing the Minecraft license rules on servers. The Hypixel team decides it wants a game of its own.
  • 2015: development of Hytale begins.
  • 2016: Riot Games meets the team, according to Dylan Jadeja, then president of Riot.
  • 2018: Hypixel Studios is founded, with more than 40 developers and an investment led by Riot.

2018 and 2019: the reveal

  • December 9, 2018: countdown on the official website.
  • December 13, 2018: the reveal trailer drops, along with Hypixel Studios and the first FAQ.
  • January 13, 2019: the trailer reaches 32 million views in a month. The internal goal was 250,000 in the first week.
  • May 3, 2019: the studio says the game will be ready when it's ready, with no beta date.
  • November 27, 2019: first concrete target, playable by everyone in 2021.

2020 to 2024: Riot and the new engine

  • April 16, 2020: Riot Games acquires Hypixel Studios. Noxy becomes CEO, Simon steps down and the studio opens an office in Derry~Londonderry, Northern Ireland.
  • December 10, 2020: the blog admits there is no launch plan.
  • July 1, 2021: launch pushed to 2023 at the earliest.
  • July 22, 2022: the engine will be rebuilt in C++, and the 2023 window is dropped.
  • November 30, 2023: new engine developed fully in-house and first external playtest.
  • July 29, 2024: first images from the new engine.
  • December 17, 2024: a hundred players at once in the Capital, a milestone the legacy engine never reached.

2025: end and new beginning

  • March 28, 2025: last development update under Riot.
  • June 23, 2025: Noxy announces Hytale's cancellation and the closing of Hypixel Studios. The trailer had 61 million views.
  • Late June 2025: Simon says he will approach Riot to buy the game back.
  • August 12, 2025: Simon sends Riot a final counter offer.
  • August 13, 2025: Simon confirms active negotiations with Riot.
  • November 17, 2025: "Hytale is saved". Simon and Philippe buy 100% of the game, rehire more than 30 developers and return to the original C# and Java engine.
  • November 28, 2025: Early Access date announced. More than 300 branches merged and a team of 50 people.
  • December 3, 2025: first FAQ of the new era. No Steam at first, no console and no mobile in Early Access.
  • December 13, 2025: pre-purchase opens, seven years after the trailer.

2026: Early Access

  • January 12: pre-purchases already cover two years of development.
  • January 13: Hytale enters Early Access on Windows, macOS and Linux, with Exploration, Creative and modding. It becomes the most watched game on Twitch, with more than 420,000 viewers.
  • January 17: Update 1, with dinosaurs in the underground jungles and a cheaper backpack.
  • January 24: Update 2, with reorganized pickaxes, farming changes and automatic updates for servers.
  • February 17: Update 3, with taming, map markers and Brazilian Portuguese.
  • March 5: modding contest with CurseForge, with 100,000 dollars in prizes.
  • March 26: Update 4, with more than 500 blocks, proximity voice chat and the emote wheel.
  • April 28: the tools to list servers on the official directory open.
  • May 11: contest winners, from almost 400 approved entries.
  • May 26: Update 5, with controller support, friends list, server browser and Trigger Volume.
  • June 22: Hytale Shared Source announced, with server code, protocol and assets on GitHub.
  • July 16: first look at Chapter 1, with goblins, a dungeon, runes, gadgets and minigames.
  • August 27: Update 6, with Hardcore, spectator, mod browser and VPN-free connections.
  • September 3: Update 7 enters pre-release, with new parts on September 10 and 17. It is the first batch of Chapter 1 content.

Numbers worth remembering

  • 250,000: internal view goal for the trailer's first week.
  • 32 million: trailer views in the first month.
  • Over 63 million: trailer views in September 2026.
  • Over 900: job applications in the first month after the reveal.
  • 64,533: concurrent players in the Hypixel server's Guinness record.
  • 3 years: time spent on the C++ engine before it was dropped.
  • 10 years: the founders' personal funding commitment.
  • Over 300: GitHub branches merged to build the launch version.
  • 8 weeks: from buyback to Early Access.
  • 50 people: team size on the eve of launch.
  • 19.99, 35 and 70 dollars: prices of the Standard, Supporter and Cursebreaker editions.
  • Over 420,000: Twitch viewers on launch day.
  • 100,000 dollars: prizes in the first modding contest.
  • Over 5,000: mods published by March 2026, with 20 million downloads on CurseForge.
  • 7 updates: six stable ones and the seventh in pre-release, in eight months of Early Access.

Dates that vary by source

  • The founding press release gives 2015 as the start of the project. The 2018 FAQ suggests the team split from the server around 2016.
  • PocketGamer.biz reported the modding contest on March 3. The official post is dated March 5.
  • The number of rehired developers went from "over 30" (November 17) to "more than 40" (November 28).
  • The studio never published an official launch player count. The only figure tied to it is Simon's expectation of more than 1 million.
