Every Hytale bench and what each one crafts

From the Workbench to the rune Effigy: each station’s recipe, what it crafts, what every tier upgrade costs and the right order to build them.

In Hytale, each crafting station handles one family of items. Several have tiers, and all of them pull materials from chests up to 14 blocks away horizontally and 6 vertically, a rule that arrived in Update 1. The recipe and upgrade costs below come from the game files. The full list, recipe by recipe, is on the benches page.

The order that works

  • Step 1: crafting by hand makes the crude tools, the Workbench, the Campfire and the Builder's Workbench.
  • Step 2: with the Workbench you make the Furnace, the Tanning Rack, the Farmer's Workbench and the Furniture Workbench, using only logs, stone, fiber and hide.
  • Step 3: with your first copper bars you make the Blacksmith's Anvil, the Armorer's Workbench and the Chef's Stove. All three cost 2 Copper Ingot, 10 logs and 5 stone.
  • Step 4: with the Workbench at tier 2, you unlock the Salvager's Workbench, the Alchemist's Workbench and the Arcanist's Workbench.

How tiers work

An upgraded bench gains new recipes and crafts faster. The Workbench, for example, works 15% faster at tier 2 and 30% at tier 3. On the wiki, each recipe shows the required tier with the tag T2, T3 and so on. In Update 6, fully upgraded benches started applying that speed bonus again.

Basic stations

Crafting by hand

Not a bench, but where everything starts. It has nine recipes: Crude Pickaxe, Crude Hatchet, Crude Sword, Crude Torch, Wooden Chest, Crude Bedroll and three benches. The walkthrough is in the first steps guide.

Workbench

Costs 4 logs and 3 stone, made by hand. It has four tabs: survival, tools, crafting and tinkering. It makes every pickaxe and hatchet, like the Iron Pickaxe, the other benches, the backpack upgrades, the Repair Kit, traps like the Bear Trap, the Rail and the Rail Cart.

  • Tier 2: 30 Copper Ingot, 20 Iron Ingot and 20 Linen Scraps.
  • Tier 3: 30 Thorium Ingot, 20 Cobalt Ingot, 30 Heavy Leather, 50 Shadoweave Scraps and 25 Essence of Fire.

Campfire

Costs 4 Stick and 2 rubble, made by hand. It processes simple food: Cooked Wildmeat, Roast Vegetable and Grilled Fish. It is your day-one kitchen.

Builder's Workbench

Costs 6 logs and 3 stone, made by hand. It has 928 recipes for building blocks: planks, bricks, stairs, slabs, beams, pillars, roofs, doors, windows, gates and simple furniture. A few examples: Hardwood Planks, Marble Brick, Blue Cloth Roof - Sloped and Crude Door. Bring the Crude Builder's Hammer, made at the Workbench, which changes the look of structural blocks.

Processing

Furnace

Costs 6 logs and 6 stone. It smelts ore into bars, one for one: copper, iron, gold, silver, thorium, cobalt, adamantite, mithril, onyxium and prisma. It also turns 2 raw stones into 1 block, like Marble, and makes the Empty Potion Bottle from sand.

  • Tier 2 (added in Update 3): 5 Copper Ingot, 5 Iron Ingot, 5 Thorium Ingot and 5 Cobalt Ingot. Smelts 30% faster and gains an extra input slot.

Tanning Rack

Costs 6 logs, 3 stone and 3 Light Hide. It turns each hide into its matching leather, one for one. There are eight types, from Light Leather to Storm Leather.

  • Tier 2: 5 Light Leather, 5 Medium Leather, 5 Heavy Leather and 5 Sturdy Chitin. Processes 40% faster, with one extra input slot and two extra output slots.

Salvager's Workbench

Costs 6 Iron Ingot, 5 logs and 5 stone, with the Workbench at tier 2. It breaks gear down into basic materials. The Adamantite Cuirass, for example, becomes 6 Adamantite Ore, 2 Heavy Hide and 2 Cindercloth Scraps. Since Update 5, sickles can be salvaged too. It is the right place for duplicate drops and old armor.

Combat

Blacksmith's Anvil

Costs 2 Copper Ingot, 10 logs and 5 stone. It makes swords, longswords, maces, axes, battleaxes, daggers, shortbows and the Iron Hand Crossbow. Copper and iron are tier 1, thorium and cobalt tier 2, adamantite and mithril tier 3. The Iron Sword costs 6 Iron Ingot, 3 Light Leather and 3 Linen Scraps.

  • Tier 2: 20 Iron Ingot, 30 Light Leather, 30 Linen Scraps and 15 Venom Sac.
  • Tier 3: 25 Thorium Ingot, 25 Cobalt Ingot, 20 Essence of Fire, 40 Essence of Ice and 100 Essence of the Void.

Armorer's Workbench

Costs 2 Copper Ingot, 10 logs and 5 stone. It makes helmets, chest pieces, gloves, legs and shields. Tier 1 has cloth, leather, bronze, copper and iron armor, like the Iron Cuirass and the Iron Shield. Thorium and cobalt need tier 2, adamantite and mithril tier 3.

  • Tier 2: 20 Copper Ingot, 20 Bone Fragments, 20 Medium Leather and 100 Azure Log.
  • Tier 3: 25 Thorium Ingot, 25 Cobalt Ingot, 40 Shadoweave Scraps, 40 Sturdy Chitin and 3 Voidheart.

Food, farming and home

Chef's Stove

Costs 2 Copper Ingot, 10 logs and 5 stone. It has three tabs: prepared dishes, baked goods and ingredients. Flour takes 10 Wheat. Dough combines flour, 2 Egg and a Tankard of water. From there you get Bread, pies like the Apple Pie, kebabs and salads. Cheese comes from a milk bucket, and Salt from Salt Block. It has no tiers in the files.

Farmer's Workbench

Costs 6 logs and 20 Plant Fiber. It is the bench with the most tiers, eight in total. It makes seeds, saplings, farming tools, essence, planters and decorative blocks such as grass and moss. Tier 1 already has the Crude Hoe, the Empty Watering Can, the Wooden Bucket, the Crude Shears, the Capture Crate and the Feed Bag. Tier 2 brings eternal seeds, like the Wheat Seed Bag (Eternal), and the Crude Sickle. The Chicken Coop and the Fertilizer Bag need tier 3.

Each upgrade asks for Essence of Life, two crops and one type of log. Tier 2 costs 50 Essence of Life, 5 Wheat, 5 Lettuce and 5 softwood logs. From tier 3 on, Greater Essence of Life comes in, worth 100 essence, and crops climb to 60 Potato and 60 Onion at the last tier. Mature crops drop essence, and from tier 2 the bench itself turns crops into essence.

The Trough - Feeding Bench shows up in the files as a variant of this same station, with no recipe of its own.

Furniture Workbench

Costs 6 logs and 4 stone. It has 153 recipes: chests, beds, lighting, pottery, textiles, village walls and seasonal decor. Examples: Lantern, Kweebec Bed and Blue Cloth. Flower petals, like Red Petals, have come from here since Update 2. The Woodcutter's Block became decorative furniture in Update 1 and is also made here.

Magic and portals

Alchemist's Workbench

Costs 20 stone, 5 Gold Ingot, 10 Venom Sac and 10 Bone Fragments, with the Workbench at tier 2. It has five tiers. It makes potions for health, stamina and energy, alchemy seeds, bombs and morph potions. The Lesser Health Potion and the Antidote are tier 1, the Crystal Fertilizer Bag tier 2 and the Health Potion tier 3.

Upgrades always ask for Silver Ingot, Gold Ingot and a gem:

  • Tier 2: 5 silver, 10 gold, 10 Venom Sac and 1 Emerald.
  • Tier 3: 10 silver, 20 gold, 20 Sturdy Chitin and 1 Zephyr.
  • Tier 4: 20 silver, 40 gold, 30 Shadoweave Scraps and 1 Sapphire.
  • Tier 5: 40 silver, 80 gold, 120 Cindercloth Scraps and 1 Ruby.

Gems have dropped from crystal golems since Update 4: the Earthen Golem drops emerald, the Sandswept Golem zephyr, the Frost Golem sapphire and the Ember Golem ruby.

Arcanist's Workbench

Costs 10 Thorium Ingot, 30 Linen Scraps and 20 Essence of the Void, with the Workbench at tier 2. It is the bench for portals and magic: Ancient Gateway, fragment keys like the Fragment: Orbis - Dragonspire Weald, the Teleporter (2 per recipe), the Healing Totem, the Slowing Totem and the Flame Crystal Staff and Ice Crystal Staff staffs.

The Heart of Orbis

It cannot be crafted. It is the statue where you turn in the Memories you collect by exploring and meeting creatures, in exchange for permanent rewards for everyone in the world. Since Update 1, it explains the system on your first interaction.

Update 7 pre-release benches

  • Runebinder's Lattice: where you slot and organize your combat runes. In the pre-release files it has no recipe.
  • GoblinLab Workbench: makes the goblin gadgets, like the Scrap Glider, the Scrap Drill and the Rocket Boots. The bench itself does not appear as a craftable item in the files.

The Forge and the Loom also exist in the files, but flagged as developer items. Do not count on them in a normal world.

To see who drops each material, check dangerous creatures too.
