Hytale Update 7: everything in the pre-release so far

Second wave of Chapter 1, six new runes, rebuilt goblins, rebalanced food and combat: all four parts of the Update 7 pre-release, explained in detail.

Update 7 is the first wave of Chapter 1 content, and it has been on the pre-release branch since Sept 3, with the official notes arriving in weekly parts. To try it, turn on the pre-release in the launcher under Settings > Pre-Release; for now, Chapter 1 content is mostly reached through Creative Mode. The developers warn that everything is still in development, and that the rune system in particular will change a lot before launch.

This page runs from the newest part to the oldest. Passages marked On file (health, drops, recipes) come from the pre-release files catalogued on the wiki, not from the official notes.

On this page

  • Part 4 · 09/24: the second wave of Chapter 1, with six new runes, rebalanced food and combat, and a lot left hidden for you to discover by playing.
  • Part 3 · 09/17: Memories that only drop in combat, renamed runes, worlds that take less disk space.
  • Part 2 · 09/10: goblins versus the Void, reworked gadgets and the new Creative Inventory search.
  • Part 1 · 09/03: the Chapter 1 debut, with six rebuilt goblins, five runes, goblin gadgets and the new glider.

Part 409/24the second wave of Chapter 1

Official. Part 4 is the second wave of Chapter 1 content. To start, visit the Forgotten Temple, or wait a few days and see what happens. The studio says a lot of what arrived is deliberately left out of the notes, so you discover it by playing. French was also added to the available languages.

Runes

  • Runes are now named only after their ability, and the tooltip says whether it is an Ability or a Modifier Runestone. Ability Runestones are now Rare and Modifier Runestones are Uncommon.
  • The Runebinder's Lattice is now called the Runebinder's Lattice.
  • Two new ability runes: Chain Hook, which throws a hook that damages enemies and reels them in, and Charged Shot, a charged bolt of frost that deals more damage and slow the longer you hold it.
  • Four new modifier runes: Expansion (100% larger area), Extended Duration (2% longer duration), Fork (the projectile splits into more, which cannot split again) and Ricochet (the projectile bounces off enemies).
  • Ground Slam: impact damage from 10 to 15 and spikes from 20 to 35. Spikes launch enemies higher, you are rooted for about half as long, more weapons can use it and the cooldown went from 14 to 18 seconds.
  • Wind Strike: damage from 14 to 8, more weapons can use it and cooldown from 15 to 10 seconds.
  • Enrage got a new animation.

Combat and food

  • Taking damage no longer interrupts eating or drinking potions. While consuming, you move 25% slower (was 60%).
  • All food takes 2.5 seconds to consume. Bread, Popcorn and Cheese restore 10% health (was 15%).
  • Raw fruit, vegetables or crops give a minor health regeneration buff for 1 minute. Raw meat, fish and eggs do not.
  • Cooked food buffs (regeneration, max health and max stamina) last 6 minutes.
  • Attacking with a crossbow slows you by 50%. With a sword, mace or battleaxe, by 25%. With daggers, by 15%.
  • Iron Hand Crossbow: combo shot from 27 to 12.5. Ancient Steel Hand Crossbow: standard shot from 16 to 10.5, combo from 42 to 13.125 and Signature Attack from 123 to 82.
  • Sword, dagger, mace and battleaxe attacks are about 15% faster. Swords knock back less vertically and got new casting animations.
  • Arrows now set off Goblin Boom Barrels.

Creatures

  • Non-elite enemies in Chapter 1 areas flinch when hurt, which interrupts their attacks. Flinch too often in a short time and they become immune for a while.
  • Void creatures were rebalanced with new looks or attacks, and no longer appear in the Echo of Orbis at night.
  • Goblins got new animations: they walk, run, look around while prowling and can run backwards. They also got braver and no longer flee from almost every creature. The Goblin Feastmaster got new animations.
  • The Skeleton Elite is now called Revenant Guardian and retrieved its own armor and weapons. The armor of several skeletons was updated.

World

  • Creative worlds have a new "World Events" option, off by default. You can also use the /worldevent command.
  • Hedera's Lair no longer ends in a void breach.
  • Bushes, vines, leaves and seaweed no longer break with weapons. Player-placed plants drop what they would in the wild (sticks, berries); to get the plant back, use Crude Shears, crafted at the Farmer's Workbench. Shears also gather fallen leaf piles now.
  • The Coconut shrank, "baffling botanists".
  • Something is happening at the Forgotten Temple: sleeping there is impossible for now and the bed cannot be used.

Items, sound and visuals

  • Ruined Giant longswords and shields are now in the Creative Inventory.
  • The Scrap Glider got a falling animation when stamina runs out and new sounds. The Hookshot got sounds too.
  • 39 new exploration tracks: 5 in Zone 2, 10 in Zone 3 and 24 in Zone 4. Zone 4 caves and sewers have their own music.
  • Sun shadows are slightly softer, and the Spyglass has a new overlay.
  • Fixes: the Scrap Drill got a new animation, the spyglass no longer blocks stamina recovery and goblin turrets no longer fixate on threats they have not seen or heard.

For modders and servers

  • Creatures can react to hits and flinch (Component_Instruction_Hit_Interrupt), hide their health bar (HiddenUIComponents) and switch targets when line of sight is lost.
  • Spawn markers search for a position the creature fits in and validate their own placement.
  • A world can block interactions by tag (BlockedRootInteractionTags), and world events toggle per world.
  • Servers must declare the true size of each asset, capped at 256 MiB.
  • Several legacy chunk methods (getBlock, setBlock, getChunk and more) were removed or restricted; the new path is the ChunkStore BlockSection.

A "hardmode" button hints at a hard mode for Fragments, but nothing has been announced.

Part 309/17Memories in combat and lighter worlds

Memories through combat

Some creatures must be defeated in combat before they grant their Memory. Walking up to them is no longer enough. The notes don't say which creatures changed.

Runes and gadgets

  • Runes were renamed to separate Ability Runes from Modifier Runes. The five available at the time were all Ability Runes.
  • The Ground Slam started working with battleaxes, such as the Iron Battleaxe.
  • The Scrap Drill description was updated: it digs tunnels and dismantles metal blocks, which come out intact, but you need to stand closer than with standard tools.
  • The Scrap Glider was remodeled again, got a new idle animation and stopped gaining height from repeated jumps.
  • The Goblin Boom Barrel is thrown with the primary input (left click by default).

Creatures

  • The Goblin Feastmaster now animates climbing, swimming, diving, flying, spawning and sleeping.
  • The Goblin Burner's Scrap Flamethrower plays overheating and reloading sounds, so you can hear when the weapon runs hot.
  • Goblins got better at getting away from Void creatures.
  • The Revenant Guardian's Flowing Slashes attack is faster. It also got new sounds for its attacks and footsteps.
  • Large predators now attack spiders, such as the Spider and the Cave Spider, on sight.
  • NPCs find solid ground in worlds whose terrain sits outside the old fixed height range.

Other items

  • The Stamina Potion cooldown no longer shows up as a debuff.
  • The Root Wand and the Stoneskin Wand fire their beams from the right position.
  • The Windwillow Fruit got reworked particles.

World and portals

  • Portal worlds can stay open with no time limit.
  • Worlds reached through the Ancient Gateway follow the settings picked when the original world was created, such as fall damage and PvP.
  • Environmental damage effects hurt again.
  • Sounds played by trigger volumes no longer come out far louder than intended, and the Play Sound effect shows proper Volume and Pitch labels.

New names:

  • Web Rope is now called Spiderweb Rope.
  • Spider Web - Diagonal is now called Spiderweb Rope - Diagonal.
  • Large Kenolite Stalactite is now called Kenolite Stalactite - Large.

Test kits

The /kit iron and /kit adamantite commands now include food and health potions of their tier. Neither grants longswords, one-handed axes, clubs, spears or staves.

Interface

  • The debug overlay (press F7 twice) was rebuilt. Panels can be moved, resized or hidden one by one, and you can create custom layouts and switch between them.
  • The Return to Crossroads button in Creative Worlds moved.
  • The game no longer falls back to English when your system language has a regional variant, such as French (Canada).
  • Long words in other languages no longer break the Installed Mods button in the mod browser.

Performance and fixes

  • Saved worlds take less disk space. Existing worlds upgrade themselves on first load.
  • Better compression of world data on save lowers memory use on busy servers.
  • Fixed a freeze that could happen when someone joined a brand new world, and a pause of up to two seconds when leaving or canceling a peer-to-peer session.
  • Two memory leaks were fixed, one when joining a world and one after the world map was drawn.
  • Wrong or oversized invite codes no longer crash the game.
  • macOS sign-in works again when Hytale is launched from Finder, Spotlight or Apple Games.
  • Fixed a Windows crash when antivirus software interrupted a world save.
  • Fixed a random crash on the main menu or inventory on PCs with integrated Intel graphics.
  • Fixed missing or wrong textures in worlds with lots of custom content, caused by a full texture atlas.
  • Beam effects no longer make blocks and items flash the wrong texture, and particles and trails appear in the right spot on entities with block models.
  • Looping music and ambience fades no longer stall just before the loop point.

For modders and servers

Cubic worlds. Cubic worlds moved to a new tree heightmap, and BlockSection blobs went to version 7. Older servers can't read them, so a world loaded on this version can't be opened on an older one. In practice, back up before testing.

Protocol. Client and server must run the same protocol version. Part 3 changed portal, item and environment packets.

Memories on kill. MemoriesUnlockedBy takes Proximity (default) or Kill, and MemoriesKillRange sets how far away a player can be and still count. A Variant role needs its referenced template to declare both, the same as IsMemory.

Block lore. A block can show its name and description without being broken. Add this under the block's Use interaction:

"Page": { "Id": "BlockLore" }

The page reads both texts from the block item's TranslationProperties and takes an optional TextAlignment of Left, Center (default) or Right. A block with no description opens nothing.

Ephemeral items. The old portal curse is now called ephemeral items. CursedItems became EphemeralItems, the flag lives in AdventureMetadata, and the command changed:

/cursethis  ->  /ephemeral

Portals

  • PortalType and PortalKey accept a portal with no duration. BreachAtSeconds was removed from VoidEvent, and the Stages list drives the escalation on its own.
  • Instances inherit the settings a player chose for the parent world with InheritWorldSettings under Plugin.Instance.

Audio

  • Audio timing uses a single format. DurationMs: 2000 becomes Duration: { "Ms": 2000 }, and the old spelling no longer decodes.
  • The trigger volume PlaySound effect takes VolumeDb, from -100 to 0, and PitchSemitones, from -60 to 12. Volume caps at 0 dB, so a trigger can only make a sound quieter.

Creatures, items and interactions

  • Encounter asset files must declare the top-level Class key. Role files can still leave it out.
  • The Void Spectre attacks were renamed to Void_Spectre_Charge.
  • Most food models were rebuilt for the new eating animations. Custom animations that reference the old node names may break.
  • Explosions set their damage cause with EntityDamageCause on ExplosionConfig, falling back to Environment when left out.
  • Bigger map markers with IconSize: Major, at 64 pixels. Texture atlases now pack across up to 16 pages for blocks and 4 for the rest, and content that doesn't fit falls back to the missing texture with a log warning.
  • UI pages can reuse the runic console look with the @RunicPanel group in Common.ui.

Server, commands and plugins

  • /chunk height dumps a column's heightmap, and /chunk fixheight can finally correct stored heights that sat too high.
  • Bans and kicks close every connection an account has, Asset Editor included.
  • Plugins can build parent and child entity hierarchies in the ECS, and can block debug screens with PreventDebugScreens.INSTANCE.
  • The hy:ShrinkTextToFit decorator shrinks text that would be cut off in Noesis pages, down to a minimum size of 10.
  • One event listener throwing no longer stops the others. The failure goes to the log.

Part 209/10goblins versus the Void

Goblins versus the Void

Void-aligned creatures, such as the Void Crawler and the Void Spectre, are now hostile toward goblins. Most goblins try to run from them. If enough allies are nearby, they stay and hold their ground.

Goblin tweaks

  • None of them chase their target forever anymore. That covers the Goblin Burner, Goblin Feastmaster, Goblin Guardian, Goblin Lobber, Goblin Miner and Goblin Scrapper.
  • Goblin Burner attacks carry far less knockback. It also stopped hurting its own neck when refueling and no longer taunts every time it reloads its Scrap Flamethrower.
  • The Goblin Feastmaster got new sound effects. When it yells at nearby goblins, they go quiet for a while.

Gadgets

  • The Scrap Drill got quieter when breaking blocks.
  • The Scrap Glider got a new model and texture, and its wings now flap.
  • The Rocket Boots double jump got its own sound.
  • The Hookshot got a new look and firing animation, and the hook no longer lingers in the world after it stops moving.

Explosives and traps

  • The Goblin Boom Barrel tooltip started saying it can be picked up.
  • Hatchets, such as the Iron Hatchet, can now set off explosive blocks, an unconventional way to trigger the blast.
  • The Scrap Flamethrower has less knockback, and it got louder.
  • The Bear Trap now stuns whoever steps in it for two seconds instead of indefinitely. It's made from 3 Iron Ingot at the Workbench.

Runes and controls

  • The Runebinder's Lattice screen got a visual pass, and each rune lists its ability tags in the tooltip.
  • The sound that plays when your Signature Ability is charged was updated and turned down.
  • On controller, abilities use a held right bumper plus a face button (a scheme set up across Parts 1 and 2). RB + Y fires the Signature Move, RB + X the first skill, RB + B the second and RB + A the fourth ability.
  • When rebinding, the screen shows which keys and buttons can be held as part of a combo binding.
  • Hitting a block with a tool too weak to break it plays an impact sound.

World and portals

  • Each portal can cap how many times each player enters. The limit shows up in the Ancient Gateway screen, and the interface makes it clearer when you gain extra time.
  • Explosions went back to damaging creatures and players in worlds where block breaking is off.

Interface

  • Creative Inventory search was reworked to better match what you mean. It forgives one-letter typos ("swrd" and "swodr" both find "sword") and finds items without diacritics: typing "e" matches items with "é".
  • The background of player portraits in the Social Sidebar is blue again.

Performance and fixes

  • The server does less work ticking blocks, fluids and farmland in busy areas.
  • Automatic port forwarding finds the router even when other home devices answer first.
  • Several devices playing Hytale on the same network no longer cause disconnects when joining a world.
  • Rejoining a friend's world after a disconnect works without both players restarting the game.
  • Fixed a crash when a server sent a fluid update before some blocks had loaded, and a crash when returning to the main menu with a machinima scene loaded.
  • Distant sounds that were too loud were turned down, and some sounds now follow your audio settings.

For modders and servers

Cubic worlds. /block commands work at any height, including above Y 320 and below Y 0. NPCs can also find and reserve blocks outside the old 0 to 320 band.

Protocol. Client and server must run the same protocol version. Part 2 changed portal, item and environment packets.

Portals

  • TimeLimitSeconds and AllowedEntryCount on PortalType apply to every key of that type. A PortalKey only needs the fields when it wants to differ.
  • fragment timer add <seconds> adds time to the timer instead of replacing it, which is what triggers the time-gained tag on the HUD.

Node Editor

  • Opens to a start screen with recent files and reopens on the workspace type you used last.
  • Files dropped onto the window open directly, on Windows and macOS.
  • Light and dark themes under File > Preferences, plus a graph minimap from the View menu.
  • Dragged nodes snap to their neighbors and to a grid, with guide lines.
  • ManualCurve and FunctionForY gained an interactive curve graph. Values snap to whole numbers, and holding Alt allows fractions.
  • Material fields use a searchable picker with texture thumbnails.
  • New: the Trig Density node, an option to keep undoing while Ctrl+Z is held, and Brown and Bright Red node colors.
  • Large graphs no longer freeze the editor, copy and paste keeps connections, and a leak that kept every opened graph in memory was fixed.

Creatures, items and interactions

  • The Spawn_Void role was removed, and the Void Fiend takes over. The Void Tentacle animations were renamed, with Bite2 and Bite3 becoming Bite_Left and Bite_Right.
  • Local spawn beacons can be gated by weather with WeatherIds.
  • Items can use a fourth ability slot, and crossbow reload, as on the Iron Hand Crossbow, moved there.
  • ExtraJumpLocalSoundEvent plays a sound only for the wearer on an extra jump, and the Rocket Boots use it. The Hookshot models were renamed to Scrap.blockymodel and Scrap_Projectile.blockymodel.
  • The AttachBeam interaction started drawing the Hookshot rope.
  • Projectiles can get a lifespan with Lifetime, and the debug-only Debug_Movement_Boots were removed.

Plugins

  • WorldChunk.getWorld() was removed, and ChunkPreLoadProcessEvent now exposes getWorld(). Block health now lives on the chunk section, in BlockHealthSection.
  • Volume modifiers above 1.0 in SoundUtil.playSoundEvent2d and playSoundEvent3d now take effect. A plugin passing 3.0 goes up about +7.5 dB, so check those values.

Part 109/03the Chapter 1 debut

The first part brought the core of Chapter 1: rebuilt goblins, the Rune Ability system and a kit of gadgets for getting around the map.

The six Chapter 1 goblins

The goblins were reworked, and each type has a different job in a fight. On file: health, weapons and drops for each one.

  • Goblin Scrapper: a light melee fighter that likes to swarm its target. It's the frailest of the group at 20 health and carries a Scrap Club. The instance version, the Goblin Scrapper, has 100.
  • Goblin Miner: heavy melee with a powerful leap attack. It has 100 health and fights with a Scrap Pickaxe.
  • Goblin Guardian: heavily armored and defends with a shield. It has 100 health and uses a Scrap Washboard Club with a Spiked Scrap Shield.
  • Goblin Lobber: a ranged attacker with an explosive arsenal of bombs. It has 50 health.
  • Goblin Burner: a dangerous ranged fighter armed with a Scrap Flamethrower. It has 80 health and drops 2 to 4 Linen Scraps.
  • Goblin Feastmaster: a gargantuan gobbler of grub, the most imposing of the lot. It has 350 health and drops 2 to 4 Wool Scraps.

Runes in combat

You collect runes and slot them into the Runebinder's Lattice, where you arrange your abilities. The first five:

  • Enrage: channels primal rage that temporarily raises the damage you deal and also the damage you take.
  • Fireball: launches a fireball that explodes on impact.
  • Imbue Poison: coats your weapon in poison. Melee and ranged attacks apply a stacking poison, and each stack has its own duration.
  • Ground Slam: sunders the earth and raises a stone spike that launches foes upward.
  • Wind Strike: charges your weapon with wind, and your next three attacks send out piercing arcs of wind.

When an ability fails to fire, the sound changes with the reason: no charges, on cooldown or wrong weapon type.

Goblin gadgets

The goblins brought their own tools for Chapter 1. On file: all of them are crafted at the goblin lab bench (GoblinLab Workbench), with the recipes below.

  • Scrap Drill: a mining tool that breaks several blocks at once. Recipe: 1 Iron Pickaxe, 20 Stick and 150 Iron Scrap Chunks. It has 150 durability.
  • Spyglass: zooms in so you can watch what's happening far away. Recipe: 5 Sand, 2 White Crystal Shards, 2 Gold Ingot and 3 Copper Ingot.
  • Scrap Glider: the glider that takes you to the skies. Recipe: 20 Medium Leather, 20 Light Leather, 50 Iron Scrap Chunks and 1 Goblin Stool.
  • Rocket Boots: give you a double jump powered by a rocket burst. They go in the legs slot and grant +13 health and 7% physical and projectile resistance, with 120 durability. Recipe: 50 Boom Powder, 50 Iron Scrap Chunks, 20 Plant Fiber and 2 Goblin Candle.
  • Hookshot: reels you in to the block you aim at, or reels a target in to you. Recipe: 10 Hook Anchor, 1 Iron Hand Crossbow, 40 Iron Scrap Chunks and 20 Plant Fiber. It has 80 durability.
  • Hook Anchor: the block the Hookshot latches onto so you can cross gaps. Every 5 Iron Ingot make 5 of them.

Gliding

Gliding, as with the Scrap Glider, was reworked. Pressing and releasing the sprint key switches between diving and climbing. The glider also keeps the velocity you had when you opened it, so deploying it mid-sprint or mid-fall doesn't kill your momentum.

New blocks

Three building sets arrived, along with a batch of goblin decorations (piece counts: on file):

  • Enkindled Copper: 19 pieces on the wiki, with stairs, slabs, roofs, bars and pipes, such as the Enkindled Copper Pipe - Chimney.
  • Enkindled Iron: 18 pieces, following the same pattern as the copper set.
  • Goblin Iron: the largest of the three, with 30 pieces, including Goblin Iron Bars, the Goblin Iron Chain and the Glowing Goblin Iron Pipe.
  • Goblin decorations: Goblin Bed, Goblin Table, Goblin Brazier, Large Goblin Chest, Defaced Statue of a Guardian, Shiny Scrap Pile and graffiti such as the Spider Warning Graffiti.

The gold block set also got new models and a texture polish.

Creatures and Memories

  • Memories gathered during a session are no longer lost when it ends.
  • Charging creatures speed up and slow down in a more controlled way. Close to the target, they only turn to face it instead of restarting the whole charge.
  • Creatures in shallow water no longer wander into deep water on their own. The fix also stopped NPCs from getting stuck near liquids.
  • Creatures cast shadows again. NPCs with oversized textures now render black instead of invisible.

World, portals and rules

  • The Void event in the lair of The Hedera, opened with the Fragment: Orbis - The Dread Wade, was reworked and now escalates after fixed amounts of time.
  • Explosions no longer break blocks in worlds where block breaking is off.
  • Server rule changes apply right away, with no need to leave and rejoin. That includes health bars, combat text, armor hiding and the rules for placing and breaking blocks.
  • Editing an instance lets you break, gather and place blocks, even when the instance normally doesn't allow it.
  • Blocks update again when a neighbor is placed or broken. Breaking a rotated multi-block updates its surroundings, so supported blocks can fall and fluids can flow.
  • Picking up a block no longer triggers the interaction it would have when broken, and the Teleporter works again at extreme heights.
  • Fixed a crash during explosions when blocks and items didn't load fast enough.

Trigger Volumes

For map and adventure makers:

  • Trigger volumes can set your respawn point, move the world spawn or clear your respawn points.
  • A Tag Condition can compare one tag against another, not just against a fixed value.
  • The Show Event Title effect swapped the "Is Major" toggle for a Style option, so you pick the title's look directly.
  • Creative Mode quick settings gained an Activate Trigger Volumes toggle. With it off, you can walk through your own trigger volumes without setting them off.

Other items

  • The Empty Watering Can lost its offensive use. It still waters crops, and watered crops grow 2.5 times faster.
  • The Empty Watering Can and battleaxes are held correctly while you swim.
  • Upgraded workbenches keep their new look after being used, and their crafting animations loop properly again.

Interface

  • The portal panel in portal worlds was redesigned, with a new countdown, portal names and progress bar.
  • Mods can contain whole worlds, which appear in your world list once installed. A world you create from one stays even if the mod is removed.
  • Creating a world from a mod asks for permission first.
  • One-time full-screen announcements can appear after the splash screen, so news about new content is harder to miss.
  • Mod categories in the mod browser show readable names on cards, filters and detail pages.
  • The Online Play screen says in plain words whether your friends will be able to join, and what to do if they can't.
  • The Copy Network Report button copies a short network report with no addresses in it, ready to paste when you ask for help.
  • The bug report ID now has a copy button.
  • The world list always shows the most recent screenshot of each world.
  • The friends list and invitations show up properly even on a slow connection at startup, and the notifications menu opens much faster for players with many pending friend requests.
  • Items with no icon configured show an "Unknown Item" icon.
  • Installing a mod from the mod browser works even if you already dropped its files into the folder by hand.

Performance

  • The world map loads in small pieces instead of one huge image. It uses a fraction of the memory it used to and no longer stutters while open.
  • Entity collision does fewer repeated block lookups, easing the load when lots of players and creatures are moving.
  • Block breaking is lighter on the server, which no longer stops to load chunks while working out what you broke.
  • Multiplayer sessions prefer a direct route when one exists, instead of paths with higher ping.
  • Memory leaks when closing menus that show your character, switching worlds and closing the game were fixed. Long sessions no longer creep up in memory use.

Fixes

  • A single player's connection can no longer break networking for everyone else on the server.
  • Servers close connections cleanly when shutting down, so you don't sit waiting for a timeout.
  • Switching a shared world back to private cuts off access right away.
  • No more crash on older NVIDIA cards, from the GeForce 400 to 700 series, laptop versions included.
  • Fixed crashes when leaving a world or moving between servers, when joining worlds running several large custom skins, and when dropping a stack of zero items.
  • The world list loads even when one of your saves has a broken settings file.
  • Worlds and servers with a saved view distance above the limit start again. The value is now capped at 32 chunks.
  • The world map no longer freezes at high view distances on graphics cards with a smaller texture limit.
  • Transparent parts of the HUD no longer render darker, and timed visual effects pause along with the world in singleplayer.
  • Ambient world sounds stop the moment you leave a world instead of looping over the main menu.

For modders and servers

Protocol. Client and server must run the same protocol version. Part 1 replaced IsMajor with a Style field in the ShowEventTitle packet.

Audio. Music can hand off between tracks by authored rule, with the MusicTransition and Stinger asset types. Handoffs can land on a beat, a bar or a marker.

Creatures and encounters

  • Encounters can force weather for everyone taking part with SetEncounterWeather and ClearEncounterWeather. Example_Boss forces a Blood Moon in Phase 1 and clears it when the boss dies.
  • Encounters can hand out rewards with the GrantReward action, and the new Rewards module accepts custom reward types.
  • The orphan Goblin Hermit and Goblin Scavenger assets were removed. The deprecated phase 1 and phase 3 templates of the Goblin Duke now use the Goblin Scrapper model.
  • The Void Spectre assets were merged under that name, replacing Spectre_Void.

Items and interactions

  • Glider tuning was rebuilt for the new movement state machine. The old values are gone, and Template_Glider gained fields that also cover the dive and climb of the Scrap Glider.
  • Dropped items merge over a configurable distance with MergeRadius.
  • BlockBreakingBypassTag keeps chosen blocks breakable in worlds that ban block breaking. Explosions follow the same rule.
  • Textured beams between entities with the Hytale:Beam builtin and the /beam command.
  • BlockyModels gained a per-face Transparent flag, and blocks gained a Requires Alpha Blending flag.

Server and commands

  • The server refuses to boot if it would write data inside the launcher's install folder, which every update patches or wipes. It exits with code 11 and explains how to move things. Anyone who wants to run there anyway can use --allow-install-dir.
  • /auth login browser and /auth login device work while the server is already signed in, and the old session stays until the new sign-in finishes.
  • /wilderness debug tints the chunks the system counts as wilderness or home green and red on one player's map.
  • The treasure map system was removed entirely, including TreasureMap and TreasureChest. Worlds with a placed treasure chest still load.
  • Several Density types were renamed, such as SmallerThan to LessThan and Select to Selector.

Plugins

  • ClientFeature lost the health bar, combat text, armor hiding and block rules, which now live in the GameplayConfig asset. Plugins naming them stop compiling.
  • Every chunk lookup that returns a WorldChunk is marked for removal. The new route is ChunkStore.getChunkReference and the section store.

All of this is still in pre-release and may change before it reaches the live game.
