The animals of Orbis: who tames, who rides and where they live

Favorite food of every tameable animal, the three mounts, milk, wool and eggs, where each animal lives and what Chapter 1 changes for birds and foxes.

Orbis is not only monsters. The fields have horses and sheep, the desert has camels and flamingos, the ice has penguins and bison, and the sea has a whale with more health than many bosses. Since Update 3, many of these animals can be tamed. This is the guide to who lives where, who accepts an owner and what each one gives.

The cover shows deer in Zone 1, in a 2019 image. Image: Hypixel Studios (hytale.com). Numbers, favorite foods and locations come from the game files cataloged by the wiki.

How taming works

Official. Update 3, released on 02/17, brought the first version of taming. Hold an animal's favorite food and it can be tamed. After that:

  • It accepts pets with an empty hand.
  • It needs to eat every now and then.
  • It does not run from you, unless you hit it.
  • Sometimes it comes over to greet you.
  • Some can be milked, sheared or ridden.

In the files. Almost every tamed animal produces Poop over time. The Feed Bag attracts tamed animals, and since the 06/16 fix the tamed Mosshorn follows it too. Young animals can be tamed, each with its own version.

Who can be tamed, and with what

Mounts

  • Horse: Carrot.
  • Camel: Tomato.
  • Ram: Apple.

Milk, wool, eggs and feathers

  • Cow: Lettuce. Milk it with the Wooden Bucket or the Ancient Bucket.
  • Sheep: Lettuce. Shear it with the Crude Shears for 1 to 3 Wool Scraps. The wool grows back over time.
  • Chicken: Corn. Lays Egg, and shears give 1 to 2 White Feathers.
  • Desert Chicken: Cotton. Also lays Egg.
  • Skrill: Chilli. Lays Egg and gives 1 to 2 Red Feathers.
  • Turkey: Corn.

The rest of the barnyard

  • Goat and Mouflon: Apple.
  • Pig: Turnip.
  • Wild Pig: Aubergine.
  • Boar: Pumpkin.
  • Warthog: Onion.
  • Bison: Cauliflower.
  • Rabbit and Bunny: Carrot.
  • Mosshorn and Plain Mosshorn: Potato.

A funny detail: a tamed Mosshorn can be "milked" with a bucket, but what comes out is moss in several colors, like the Moss, and sometimes Poop.

Catching and bringing them home

The Capture Crate catches small animals so you can bring them back to your base. Caught chickens live in the Chicken Coop, which since Update 3 comes from tier 3 of the Farmer's Workbench. The crate no longer catches dead animals, another Update 3 fix.

Where each one lives

The locations below come from the spawn rules in the files.

Emerald Wilds, Zone 1

  • Plains: Horse, Cow, Sheep, Pig, Chicken, Rabbit, Plain Mosshorn, Stag, Tetrabird and Fox.
  • Forests: Boar, Bunny and Squirrel.
  • Swamps: Wild Pig, Turkey and Mosshorn.
  • Mountains: Goat.

Howling Sands, Zone 2

  • Desert: Camel.
  • Plateaus: Ram, Desert Chicken, Tortoise, Flamingo and Armadillo.
  • Savanna: Warthog, Antelope, Meerkat and Parrot.
  • Oasis: Tortoise and Flamingo.
  • Scrubland: Skrill, Desert Chicken and Armadillo.

Whisperfrost Frontiers, Zone 3

  • Glacial: Bison, Mouflon, Penguin and Snowy Owl.
  • Tundra: Cow, Wild Pig and Moose Bull.
  • Forests: Mouflon, Boar, Rabbit, Moose Bull and Squirrel.

Devastated Lands, Zone 4

  • Jungles: the Trillodon, with 145 health.

Ocean

  • The Humpback Whale has 400 health, the same as a dragon. And when it falls, it drops just 1 Raw Fish.

Small critters and sea life

  • In warm seas: Clownfish, Pufferfish, Crab, Lobster and jellyfish like the Man Of War, the toughest of them, with 74 health.
  • In fresh water: Salmon, Rainbow Trout, Catfish and the aggressive Piranha.
  • In the sky: Bluebird, Sparrow, Brown Owl, Hawk and Pigeon.
  • On the ground: Squirrel, Gecko, Mouse and Meerkat.

What Chapter 1 brings

Official. In the Chapter 1 blog, the studio said it is remaking animal models, textures and animations, toward a more expressive style with clearer silhouettes. Pigs and boars got new animations.

Official. Birds are the first group getting new behaviors. They sleep in nests, fly and land as a flock, take off when you get close and grab seeds from the ground. Owls and other birds of prey swoop down to hunt smaller creatures.

Official. Foxes got some love too. The studio says the design for livestock and pets is still maturing, but work has started: they can follow you on adventures, stalk prey, sleep by campfires and play with their owners.

Official. The short-term goal is to deepen taming and homesteading. In Update 7 Part 3, large predators started attacking spiders, like the Spider, on sight.

Pets in the files

In the files. There are models for a Cat, a Kitten, a Dog and a Corgi, the last one with a red scarf attachment. There is also a Tamed Horse model and a Shaved Sheep one. None of them has an NPC.

Theory. With foxes that follow their owner and cat and dog models ready, the path to pets looks set. When and how, nobody has confirmed. See also the article on the hidden art in the files.

Summary

  • To tame, hold the favorite food. Carrots work for horses and rabbits, lettuce for cows and sheep, apples for goats, mouflons and rams.
  • Mounts today: horse, camel and ram.
  • Milk with a bucket, wool and feathers with shears, eggs from chickens and skrills.
  • The highest health among animals belongs to the whale, at 400.
  • Chapter 1 changes how birds and foxes behave.
