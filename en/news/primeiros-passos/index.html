First steps in Hytale: from your first stick to your first night

What to do on day one: gathering, tools, benches, bed, food and the metal ladder, with every recipe checked against the game files.

Hytale has been in Early Access since January 13, 2026. The stable version is Update 6, and Update 7 is running on the pre-release. This guide covers Exploration mode, where you explore, build, farm and craft your own gear. Every recipe below was checked against the game files catalogued on the wiki.

Before you create the world

Update 6 Hardcore mode can only be picked when you create a world, under the Customize button, and it cannot be changed later. Leave it on Off for your first world. Dying is part of learning.

If you turned on the pre-release in the launcher, it has kept worlds in a separate folder since Update 5. A world opened on the Update 7 pre-release no longer opens on older versions. Keep your main world on the stable build.

The first minutes: everything by hand

There is no bench yet. Look at the ground and gather four things:

  • Stick: comes from bushes, like the Bush, and from the Pile of Sticks.
  • Plant Fiber: grass, bushes and tree leaves.
  • Rubble, like Stone Rubble: loose pebbles and gravel. Any type works in recipes (rubble).
  • Tree Sap: comes from sap globs on trees.

These sources are in the game's drop tables. Logs and stone come right after, with your first tools.

Crafting by hand

The crafting by hand menu has nine recipes. The three tools cost the same: 2 rubble, 2 Plant Fiber and 2 Stick.

  • Crude Hatchet: fells trees. Any log works in the early recipes (logs).
  • Crude Pickaxe: breaks stone and ore (stone).
  • Crude Sword: 6 damage per swing and 80 durability. It holds until your first metal weapon.
  • Workbench: 4 logs and 3 stone.
  • Campfire: 4 Stick and 2 rubble.
  • Crude Torch: 1 Plant Fiber, 1 Tree Sap and 1 Stick make 4 torches.
  • Wooden Chest: 10 logs.
  • Crude Bedroll: 3 Plant Fiber and 2 Light Hide.
  • Builder's Workbench: 6 logs and 3 stone.

The order that works: hatchet, pickaxe, Workbench and Campfire. The sword can wait a few minutes if you spawned away from predators.

Your first bench

Once the Workbench is down, the game really opens up. The recipes that matter on day one:

  • Furnace: 6 logs and 6 stone. Turns ore into bars, one for one.
  • Tanning Rack: 6 logs, 3 stone and 3 Light Hide. Turns hide into leather.
  • Farmer's Workbench: 6 logs and 20 Plant Fiber.
  • Furniture Workbench: 6 logs and 4 stone.
  • Crude Shortbow: 4 Stick and 6 Plant Fiber. The Crude Arrow takes 4 Stick and 1 rubble, 4 arrows at a time.

An Update 1 rule changes how you lay out your base: benches pull materials from chests up to 14 blocks away horizontally and 6 vertically. Put your chests next to the benches and stop hauling everything in your inventory.

What each station makes is in the guide to every bench.

Shelter, bed and your first night

A placed Crude Bedroll sets your respawn point. Carry one until you have a fixed home. You can sleep from 7:30 PM, and sleeping through the night gets you out of bed on its own.

Night brings the Void creatures. In the spawn files they appear between 7 PM and 5 AM, only in dark spots, and vanish at dawn. Walls around you and a lit Crude Torch cover the basics. Torches go straight from the utility slot to the ground, even with a pickaxe or hatchet in hand.

Food without waste

Raw food heals little. Raw Wildmeat restores 5% health and nothing else. Run everything through the Campfire:

  • Cooked Wildmeat: 5% health, Health Regeneration I and Health Boost I for 45 seconds. Accepts any meat.
  • Roast Vegetable: 5% health, Health Regeneration I and Stamina Boost I for 45 seconds.
  • Grilled Fish: same effect as cooked meat.

Wild Berries give 5% and Health Regeneration I. Almost every game animal drops meat, like the Boar, the Goat and the Rabbit.

Once you have copper, the Chef's Stove costs 2 Copper Ingot, 10 logs and 5 stone. There, the Meat Skewer takes 1 Stick and 4 meat and heals 10%, with Health Regeneration II and Health Boost II for 2:30.

Tool and material progression

The metal ladder is the same for tools, weapons and armor: crude, copper, iron, thorium and cobalt, adamantite and mithril. Ore becomes bars in the Furnace, one for one: Copper Ore gives Copper Ingot and Iron Ore gives Iron Ingot.

Pickaxes come from the Workbench:

  • Copper Pickaxe: 3 Copper Ingot, 6 logs and 1 Plant Fiber.
  • Iron Pickaxe: 5 Iron Ingot, 2 Light Leather and 2 Linen Scraps.
  • Thorium Pickaxe and Cobalt Pickaxe: need the bench at tier 2.
  • Adamantite Pickaxe and Mithril Pickaxe: need the bench at tier 3.

Do not skip steps. Since Update 2, a low-tier pickaxe barely scratches high-tier ore, and Adamantite Ore requires the Thorium Pickaxe or the Cobalt Pickaxe.

Upgrading the Workbench to tier 2 costs 30 Copper Ingot, 20 Iron Ingot and 20 Linen Scraps. Scraps drop from Trorks, skeletons and zombies, like the Trork Warrior, the Skeleton Fighter and the Zombie. Tier 3 asks for 30 Thorium Ingot, 20 Cobalt Ingot, 30 Heavy Leather, 50 Shadoweave Scraps and 25 Essence of Fire.

Leather comes from the Tanning Rack. Light Hide comes from small animals, like the Chicken. Medium Hide comes from the Boar, the Cow and other large animals.

Backpack

The backpack starts locked. Unlock Backpack is made at the Workbench for 8 Iron Ingot and 8 Medium Leather. Then come Backpack Upgrade I, with the bench at tier 2, and Backpack Upgrade II, at tier 3.

Tips that save you frustration

  • Rolling on landing reduces fall damage.
  • The Repair Kit repairs tools, weapons and armor, even ones stored in the backpack. The repaired item loses up to 10% of its maximum durability.
  • Use the right tool. Hitting a block with a tool too weak to break it only makes an impact sound.
  • Exploring and meeting creatures earns Memories. You turn them in at the The Heart of Orbis for permanent rewards for everyone in the world.
  • Want to check a recipe? They are all in items, benches and creatures.
