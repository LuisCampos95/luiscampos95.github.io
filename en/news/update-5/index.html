Hytale Update 5: controllers, friends and servers

Update 5 brought controller support, a friends list with Discord, server discovery and the Trigger Volume tool for no-code scripted events.

Update 5 shipped on May 26, after ten weekly pre-release parts between March 26 and May 25. It is a systems update. There are almost no new creatures, but tools are everywhere: the game now supports controllers, has a friends list and an official server showcase, and creators got the Trigger Volume, which builds events without a single line of code.

The notes also spelled out what each kind of release is for. Updates bring systems, tools and polish between the big releases. Chapters are the big moments, with new content, new mechanics and the start of the Cursebreaker arc story. Chapter 1 is in production, and the studio says more and more of the team is working on it.

Main changes

  • Trigger Volume: invisible 3D volumes that fire effects when something happens inside them. It is the backbone for ambushes, puzzles and story beats without a plugin.
  • Controller support: Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo Switch controllers work in game and in menus, with grid navigation.
  • Social Sidebar: a friends list on the main menu, with online status, invites, favorites, blocking and friends imported from Discord.
  • Server Discovery: community servers inside the game, with search, tags, favorites and staff-picked highlights.
  • Sound that respects walls: occlusion muffles audio based on the material between you and the source, and diffraction bends sound around doors and corners.
  • Over an hour of new music in the Emerald Wilds, Howling Sands and Whisperfrost Frontiers.
  • New Creative placement modes and a 3D prefab preview before placing.
  • Simplified Chinese in game, with Dutch, Finnish, Italian and Norwegian open for translation on Crowdin.

Trigger Volume

Think of a tripwire that can be a box, sphere or cylinder of any size. Players can't see the volume during normal play. When something happens inside it, like someone walking in, a creature walking out, an arrow flying through or a block being broken, it reacts with the configured effect.

There are over 20 built-in effects: play a sound, spawn a visual effect, change the velocity of whoever passes, change the weather, teleport, switch game mode, adjust the inventory, spawn an NPC and place a prefab. Set Music, Give Item, Run Root Interaction and Set Game Mode were added during the pre-release. One volume can fire several effects, and delays let you chain them into sequences.

Conditions decide when an effect fires. The studio gives examples such as firing only once, firing after 5 seconds standing in the volume, or only when the block is a specific type. You can also check permissions, game mode, inventory, cooldowns and random chance. Each effect is flagged as a success or a rejection effect.

Everything is set up through a UI, with visual gizmos showing where each volume sits. Volumes can be grouped, duplicated with Shift+D and saved inside prefabs, so a whole trap can be reused in another build. The Trigger_Volume_Showcase prefab has ready-made examples. For programmers, there is an API for custom events, conditions and effects.

Controllers

Support arrives in a first version. Plug in an Xbox, PlayStation or Nintendo Switch controller to move, fight, explore and navigate menus. The movement stick dead zone goes from 5% to 95% in Settings. Some advanced features still need a keyboard and mouse.

Likaos, the developer who led the work, explained the choice. The ideal was to wait for the game's new UI, but that would leave players without a controller for months. The team built a temporary version on top of the current UI. Instead of a virtual cursor, menus use grid navigation, because a big share of UI time in Hytale goes into the inventory. Dragging items with a controller works thanks to the split between pressing and holding.

The announced next steps are gyro aiming, Steam Input, a UI designed for gamepads and deeper rebinding.

Social Sidebar

From the main menu, you can add friends, see who is online, invite them to your world and join theirs. Discord friends become Hytale friends, and the sidebar shows their updated avatar when they change their look. To use it, re-link your Discord account on the Hytale account page, with a second login method already set up, then enable the integration in the game settings.

Favorite friends stay at the top of the list. Blocking someone cuts both chat and voice, and blocked players are muted automatically. Friend requests and invites show up as toasts, with a hotkey to accept or dismiss without opening the sidebar. The "Allow Friend Requests" option declines new requests. The feature is not available on parent-managed accounts and went live shortly after launch, with no game restart needed.

Server Discovery

Owners register their server under "Server Profiles" on the Hytale account page. It only shows up if it meets the Server Owner Policies and passes manual review. In game, players can browse staff-picked highlights, search by title or description, filter by tags and see how many people liked or favorited each server. Favorites and recently played servers are saved. It is also unavailable on parent-managed accounts.

Sound and music

Occlusion casts rays between the sound source and the listener and muffles audio based on the materials in between. Diffraction makes sound bend around corners and through doorways instead of just going muffled. Audio also changes between first and third person: in first person, a torch in your hand plays its sound from its own position.

The soundtrack gained over an hour of music across the Emerald Wilds, Howling Sands and Whisperfrost Frontiers, with day and night exploration tracks, short pieces and ambient fragments. One of them, "Persevere", was featured in the notes. Cave music in those regions now crossfades between layers instead of swapping whole tracks. In voice chat, avatars move their mouths while the player talks.

Creatures

  • The old Lava Shellfish is now called the Lava Coelacanth.
  • The Scarak Broodmother only spawns a Scarak Louse when none are nearby, which cuts endless swarms.
  • The Crab and the Lobster can breathe in water. Air-breathing creatures also no longer spawn submerged.
  • The Skeleton no longer falls through the floor when leaving a coffin.
  • The Grizzly Bear hitbox now covers more of its head, which makes hits easier to land.
  • The Moose Bull no longer notices players the moment they join the world.
  • Predators such as the Black Wolf and the White Wolf no longer lose momentum mid-chase.
  • Encountering Scaraks, such as the Scarak Fighter, unlocks their Memories again.
  • Submerged NPCs no longer lock in place, NPCs hold their configured item in both hands, and they animate properly when strafing.
  • Modded NPCs using ActionSpawn aim better when launching other NPCs at a target.

Items and equipment

Combat

  • The Deployable Turret "had a change of heart", in the studio's words, and now treats whoever placed it as an ally.
  • A placed Healing Totem heals 5 health per second for 10 seconds.
  • Shortbows and crossbows with no durability can't attack or use signature abilities, and broken crossbows can't reload. That covers weapons such as the Iron Shortbow and the Iron Hand Crossbow.
  • Shortbow and crossbow arrows break blocks that only take one hit, such as tall grass.
  • Blocking with a spear now shows the blocking UI.
  • The Fishbone Harpoon throws the correct model again.
  • Holding the Adamantite Hatchet no longer prevents torch placement.
  • Weapon tooltips show damage data. It is a first pass meant for comparing weapons, not a full breakdown.
  • Burn, Freeze and Poison visual effects were reworked, and unarmed attacks got new particles.

Tools and farming

  • The Capture Crate shows the captured NPC's icon in the inventory. It only applies to captures made after the update. It also no longer resets the captured creature's cooldowns.
  • The Empty Watering Can and the Fertilizer Bag can no longer be repaired.
  • The Copper Sickle, Iron Sickle and Rusty Steel Sickle can be salvaged at the Salvager's Workbench.
  • At the Alchemist's Workbench, the Blood Leaf Seed Bag, Storm Sapling Seed Bag and Azure Kelp Seed Bag no longer require a Voidheart.
  • The Crystal Fertilizer Bag got cheaper at the Alchemist's Workbench: the recipe went from 50 to 25 crystal shards.
  • The Fish Bait (Wild) got a new model.

Inventory and repair

  • Chests now stack to 25 instead of 10.
  • Repair kits such as the Repair Kit work on items stored in the backpack. The Crude Repair Kit got a new description.
  • Backpack items no longer lose durability on death in worlds set to have no inventory penalty.
  • An item no longer counts as an ingredient in its own recipe.
  • New descriptions for: Adamantite Ingot, Cobalt Ingot, Copper Ingot, Iron Ingot, Thorium Ingot, light, medium and heavy leather, Bloodcap Spawn Bag, Blood Leaf Seed Bag, Zone 3 Taiga Portal, Small Stamina Potion, Stamina Potion and Large Stamina Potion.
  • The Wild Wisteria Sapling description now says it yields Goldenwood, not Darkwood.
  • The Scarak Spit Clump and the Small Scarak Spit Clump break in a single hit.
  • The Winter Bauble is held correctly.
  • The icons for bronze pipes and the Zinc were updated.

World and blocks

Building

  • Bookshelves stack: the Bamboo Bookshelf, Feran Bookshelf and Lost Civilization Bookshelf can sit on top of each other.
  • Vertical Rope can be extended downwards by placing more rope beneath it.
  • You can sit on the Royal Magic Couch.
  • New blocks: Gold Pipe - Large, Gold Pipe - Long and Gold Pipe - Large Mouthpiece.
  • Bamboo, temple, Lost Civilization, Sandswept and tavern shelves take more than one hit to break.
  • Black, gray and white Build blocks and the Build lightsources no longer break in one hit, but a pickaxe speeds things up.
  • Crouching while placing a block puts it next to non-solid blocks such as leaves instead of replacing them.
  • Pressing Alt no longer ejects you from bed.
  • Lanterns such as the Lantern, and opened coffins, now need a tool or a fist to break.
  • The Lost Civilization Bed requires six blocks of support, like other beds that size.
  • The Wall Poster and the Small Wall Poster can only go on solid blocks.
  • The Kweebec Wardrobe and the Feran Platform follow the same placement rules as other wardrobes and platforms.
  • The Scarecrow hitbox shrank so it no longer breaks when crops grow.
  • The Stained Windows became a Developer item and left the Creative Inventory. Blocks already placed stay in the world.
  • The Winter Lights and the Winter Wreath no longer emit light when off, and the Winter Garland no longer shows an interact option.
  • Dozens of blocks got new breaking particles and sounds, including Sandswept, Lost Civilization, Scarak hive and temple furniture, gravels, rubble and the Statue of a Slumbering Deity.

Farming and nature

  • Crops check for room before moving to the next growth stage. Without space, they reset to the start of the current stage.
  • Opening and closing doors breaks crops and soft blocks in the way, and they drop their items.
  • Six grass types are now crafted at the Farmer's Workbench: Cold Grass, Dry Grass, Full Grass, Lush Grass, Sunny Grass and Withered Grass.
  • The Shale Gravel and the Marble Gravel can also be crafted at the Farmer's Workbench.
  • Goldenwood shows up more often in Zone 4, including in some cities and villages.
  • The Sandylion drops White Petals instead of Red Petals.
  • Trees that got stuck while growing grow again, apple trees included.
  • Freed ore stopped "attempting a daring escape upwards" through neighboring blocks, in the studio's words. It now drops politely, as intended.
  • The Furnace no longer spits out its contents or moves when sprayed by the Empty Watering Can.
  • The Small Scrap Pile, Scrap Pile and Large Scrap Pile can be broken with weapons in Adventure Mode, and all of them drop trash.
  • Large structures spawn on flatter ground and no longer float.
  • Fishing Traps placed before Update 4 could leave invisible collision and ghost outlines. Punching or interacting with them clears the leftovers.

New names

  • Every "Grey" in English names became "Gray", including hair color and jean fabric options.
  • Wisteria became Wild Wisteria, as in the Wild Wisteria Tree Trunk and the Wild Wisteria Roots.
  • The Stone Brazier became the Earth Brazier, and the Common Pink Flower became the Pink Pitcher Flower.
  • Iridescent Processed blocks got "Brick" in their names, as in the Iridescent Processed Brick - Ornate.

Interface and quality of life

  • Chat commands have autocomplete: Tab cycles suggestions and Shift+Tab shows variants.
  • The emote wheel got new visuals and nine animations, including a high five and a dance-off.
  • Game news appears in a carousel on the main menu.
  • The inventory shows how many discovered Memories still need restoring.
  • Item grids highlight where the cursor sits, in the inventory, backpack and workbenches.
  • Alt+B opens the Asset Editor, and Ctrl+F searches inside server custom pages.
  • Keybinds can use modifiers with mouse buttons.
  • In Creative, the "Clear Inventory" button wipes inventory, backpack, equipped slots and the utility wheel when pressed twice.
  • The prefab list shows a 3D preview you can rotate, tilt, zoom and pan. Servers can use this preview in their own UIs.
  • Bug reports accept video through the "Add Screenshots" button and got a translation category. The feedback button opens the Feature Requests site.
  • Items can have per-stack names and descriptions with colors, bold text and parameters. That opens the door to shops with prices, RPG stats and loot with lore.
  • Simplified Chinese is in the game. Dutch, Finnish, Italian and Norwegian can be translated by the community on Crowdin.
  • The UI no longer suggests taming the corpse of a creature you just killed. "Sorry, necromancers", the studio joked.

Performance and stability

  • Player movement was rewritten as a hierarchical finite state machine. Nothing changes in game yet, but new movement mechanics get easier to build.
  • Void maps run better, and the new World-Gen V2 thread allocation keeps servers steadier under heavy load.
  • Item container operations got faster.
  • Memory leaks in the loading screen and UI were closed, along with dozens of crashes.
  • The launcher now keeps older versions around longer. If a mod hasn't updated, you can go back to Update 4 through Launcher, Settings, Patchline, v0.4.
  • The release patchline still uses %HYTALE%/UserData/. Other patchlines now store data in %HYTALE%/data/, such as %HYTALE%/data/pre-release/, and no longer share worlds, mods and settings with the release.

Notable fixes

Hotfixes ran from 0.5.1 on launch day to 0.5.7 on July 22.

  • 0.5.1 (May 26): servers boot again even with a malformed ServerVersion.
  • 0.5.2 (May 27): the social bar stopped jittering, server addresses are hidden by default and Server Discovery uses the protocol version.
  • 0.5.3 (May 29): landing rolls reduce fall damage again, and sprinting is kept while crouch-sliding. A deadlock when inviting friends to a singleplayer world was also fixed.
  • 0.5.4 (June 5): renamed cosmetics caused a "Connection Closed" error that blocked joining. The client now repairs the avatar and says what changed. Chests vanished next to an open chest, and tamed animals despawned when many of the same kind were nearby.
  • 0.5.5 (June 16): the biggest batch. The Chicken and the Skrill lay eggs again. Eternal Corn yields 2 to 4 per harvest instead of 1 to 2. Newly planted Aubergine and Lettuce return their seeds. Cooled Magma now works as an input at the Builder's Workbench.
  • 0.5.5 (June 16), creatures: the Trork Hunter without a wolf left its combat loop, tamed Mosshorn follow the Feed Bag, sand dungeon skeleton variants such as the Sandswept Skeleton Guard give the right Memories again, and The Hedera lost the stray red coloring on its face.
  • 0.5.5 (June 16), world: sand falls again, your character no longer faces north after a world map teleport, death markers show up at any distance and players get out of bed after a night skip. The Snowy Fir Leaves and the Feed Bag rotate to face you when placed.
  • 0.5.6 (June 17): a bug from 2021 surfaced. Collision and repulsion were saved by asset index, not by name. A 0.5.5 fix changed the load order, and players started getting stuck inside each other. The game now saves the name and converts old worlds on its own. Entities created while 0.5.5 was live may need a manual touch in the editor.
  • 0.5.7 (July 22): researcher 0x90 showed that a malicious server could run code on a player's computer through a crafted PNG. The flaw was fixed the same day it was reported, there is no sign it was used against players, and the bounty was 20,000 dollars.

For servers and modders

Creative tools

  • Placement modes: Replace swaps the block you're looking at for the one in hand. Type Replace only swaps the material and keeps the shape. Extrude fills a line as you click and drag, Draw paints freely on any surface and Fast Place places faster.
  • Extra options: Eraser breaks blocks as you drag and works alongside the modes above. Free Place allows any direction, No Physics ignores placement rules without collapsing neighbors and Highlight Block Target outlines the block you're aiming at.
  • Extrude and Flood merged into one tool, with "Same Material" and "Same Shape" filters, extrusion strategies and a Fill mode for empty space.
  • The Smooth Brush was folded into the Sculpt Brush, which smooths about 6 times faster and has Full3D, Heightmap and Flat modes. The old behavior lives on as OldSculpt.
  • The mask panel saves and loads presets, inverts types, accepts commands such as >Rock_Stone and now filters fluids.
  • The Paste Tool got a quick-settings panel, F flips the selection and V switches from the Selection Tool to the Paste Tool.
  • The Entity Tool got undo and redo, NPC scaling, right-click cycling between overlapping entities, Shift plus scroll for precision and a "Freeze on Spawn" option.
  • The "Fog" checkbox in Quick Settings hides fog at the edge of view distance.
  • Warps created by a Teleporter are protected from /warp set and /warp remove unless you add --force.

Permissions and commands

  • Permissions overhaul: no longer tied to game mode. You can give a friend a role instead of using /op, with inheritance, namespaces and autocomplete. Every command moved into new groups.
  • /locate finds or teleports you to biomes, zones, regions and prefabs.
  • /kit adamantite and /kit iron replace your gear and inventory with a full kit of weapons, blocks and food. /kit debug gives test armor and flags.
  • /shrink fits the selection to non-air blocks, and /knockback applies knockback for testing.
  • /worldgen 2 create sets up a WorldGen V2 asset pack with a starter biome, enabled by default.
  • The Node Editor now runs on macOS and Linux.

Content and data

  • Falling blocks got their technical groundwork. ExplodeFallingBlockImpact makes a block explode when it hits another solid block, and ExplosionConfig on BlockType centralizes explosion data.
  • Music containers live in Server/Audio/MusicContainers/, with the SingleTrack, Random, Sequence and Horizontal types. The Music field on AmbienceFX is deprecated.
  • The Test_Dummy_Damage test NPC, with 100 health and immune to knockback, and the Gym_Arena instance were added.
  • Items gained TextureComputedColor, a weighted average of their texture colors.

API changes

  • ServerVersion became a semver range such as >=0.5.0 <0.6.0 or ^0.5.0. The old format loads as a wildcard with a log warning. >=0.5.0 does not match 0.5.0-pre.3.
  • HudManager#setCustomHud was removed. The API is now keyed: addCustomHud(PlayerRef, CustomUIHud) and getCustomHud(key), and several HUDs can coexist.
  • Player no longer implements CommandSender or PermissionHolder. Use PlayerRef.
  • LivingEntity#canBreathe became the BreathingComponent, and BreathingCheckEvent overrides NPC breathing.
  • addForce was renamed addVelocity on BlockEntity, Velocity, MotionController and Role.
  • DisplayNameComponent is now runtime-only. The saved data is PersistentDisplayName.
  • RunCommandEffect, CommandInteraction and PermissionBypassSender were removed.
  • Damage resistances use ResistanceModifier, with Flat and Percent modes.
  • SwitchActiveSlotEvent became InventoryActiveSlotRequestEvent, and custom vector types moved to JOML.
