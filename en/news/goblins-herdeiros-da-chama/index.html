LoreOrbisHytalePublished September 26, 2026 · updated on September 26, 2026 at 04:00 PM (Brasília time)

Goblins think they inherited a kingdom

They live in Enkindled ruins, hoard scrap like relics and dream of a great flame. Every goblin Duke picks an obsession, and almost all of them end badly.

The goblins of Chapter 1 live in the ruins of an Enkindled settlement and are sure all of it belongs to them. The Guttering Hollows Fragment text describes the scene better than anyone:

"The old caverns resound with the clamor of goblins who hold court in their murky depths, mistaking a graveyard for a kingdom, and every scrap of ruin for an artifact worth hoarding."

Every Duke, an obsession

Among goblins, Duke isn't about blood. It's a title someone takes on along with an obsession, and everyone else follows. Some become Feastmasters and try to recreate lost cuisine. The current Makinomagistra, the Goblin Makinomagistra, chose technology, and her Memory even praises the result: "an uncharacteristic degree of success for a Duke". In other words, failure is the norm.

And the little guys pay first. The Goblin Scrapper Memory:

"Chaos given physical form. When a new goblin dons the title of a Duke, the scrappers are the first to be drawn in by their obsession and often the cause of its premature end."

The fad that erased the past

Goblins once had a culture of their own. The Goblin Miner Memory says mining is one of the few aspects of ancient goblin civilization to survive "the cultural shift brought on by the Dukes' fascinations". And even that only half survived: today's miners don't understand the art their ancestors mastered.

In short: the goblins traded their own history for their dead neighbors'. And according to the Crude Pickaxe, they still haven't figured out what a pickaxe is for.

The scrap museum

The rest is a collection. Scraps, mundane leftover materials, are valued "above almost all else". The Bundled Treasure, actual treasure, is understood by few goblins, and even Feastmasters use it as decor. And Boom Powder takes so much care that the foremost goblin inventor bears the scars of a boomshroom experiment gone wrong.

The great flame

All of this has a goal. Goblin Memories talk about a "great flame" of legend, left by the civilization that inspired them. Lobbers treat every bomb as a rehearsal, and Feastmasters think the perfect recipe leads to it. The full quotes are in Crucible: the city the fire erased.

Theory: the goblins became a cult of the flame that ended the Enkindled. They copy statues, dishes, bombs and fire without understanding any of it, hoping to bring back the greatness of whoever lived there. They just forgot to read the ending: the great flame of legend is most likely the same one a rite turned into a curse, the one that turned their kingdom into a graveyard.

Open questions

  • Who was the first Duke, and what was their obsession?
  • If a goblin ever lights the great flame, will anything be left of the Guttering Hollows?
  • Do the goblins know the civilization they imitate burned to death?
