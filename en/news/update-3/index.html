Hytale Update 3: taming and Brazilian Portuguese

Four weeks into Early Access, Update 3 brought taming, map markers, sickles, a climbing overhaul and Brazilian Portuguese in-game. The notes also set the weekly pre-release rhythm and the Cursebreaker chapters.

Update 3 landed four weeks into Early Access and was the first one that felt like a content update. For Brazilian players, the headline is simple: Hytale now has Brazilian Portuguese in-game, alongside Russian. Both translations had help from community translators, and the studio said more languages would follow in the coming weeks.

The notes also set the release rhythm from then on. Weekly pre-releases for testing, bundled into a stable update every two to six weeks. In parallel, the studio is building chapters, larger drops that tell the Cursebreaker arc in Exploration Mode. The plan is to move chapter by chapter until the last one takes the game to full release. The team grew as well, and several hires came straight from the modding community.

Main changes

  • Taming: some friendly animals can be tamed by holding their favorite food.
  • Map markers: custom names, icons and colors, with sharing in multiplayer.
  • Brazilian Portuguese and Russian as in-game languages.
  • Avatar presets: up to 5 saved looks to swap at any time.
  • Sickles, which harvest several crops in a single swing.
  • Climbing overhaul, with side-stepping, dropping down and rotating around ropes.
  • Fire spread and the Scrap Flamethrower, Creative only for now.
  • Grass spreading across the terrain on its own.
  • Anchor UI, which lets server plugins add elements to the client interface.

Creatures

Taming

Taming arrived as an early version, but it already changes how you deal with animals. Holding an animal's favorite food lets you tame some of the friendlier ones. After that, they can be petted with an empty hand and need to be fed once in a while. A tamed animal won't run from the player unless it gets hit, and it will occasionally come over to greet you. Some can also be milked, sheared or mounted.

Other changes

  • The Rhino Toad had its attacks reworked and gained a green variant, the Green Rhino Toad.
  • Several NPCs switch targets more intelligently.
  • The Klops Merchant no longer trades signs.
  • Ophidiophobia mode now replaces the Hatworm with the Silk Larva model.
  • NPCs inside a Chicken Coop no longer transform into other creatures.
  • The Capture Crate can no longer capture dead NPCs.
  • Some fish spawned in lava. Not anymore.
  • The Slothian color gradient was fixed.

Items and equipment

Necromancy

The Grimoire: Rekindle Embers, added in Update 2, became far more useful:

  • Summons no longer take damage from their summoner, and vice versa.
  • They respond when the player is attacked, not only when the player attacks.
  • They are faster and more aggressive.
  • Raised minions are more varied and now include a ranged variant.

Weapons

  • Damage and upgrade inconsistencies across some weapon tiers were adjusted.
  • Guarding stamina costs were misconfigured on some drop-only weapons. Fixed.
  • Every spear type stacks up to 5 and can be thrown.
  • Bow and crossbow ballistics were reworked, with better accuracy and randomized hitbox offsets.
  • Shortbows and crossbows pull ammo straight from the backpack.
  • Ingots and the Stick sit properly in the hand.

Benches and recipes

  • The Chicken Coop dropped from Tier 5 to Tier 3 at the Farmer's Workbench, and its craft time fell from 4 to 2 seconds.
  • Tier 4 of the Farmer's Workbench now asks for Drywood logs instead of the Redwood Log. Tier 6 flipped: it asks for the Redwood Log instead of Drywood.
  • The Builder's Workbench takes 2 seconds to craft instead of 3.
  • Crude ammo, weapons and tools craft instantly.
  • Dough is now made with the Tankard, which is refillable and crafted at the Farmer's Workbench.

World and blocks

New blocks and textures

  • Incandescent Volcanic Rock is back.
  • New crack textures for thorium, basalt, magma and dry mud, such as Cracked Basalt, Cracked Magma and Cracked Mud. Four ore blocks got updated crack variants.
  • The limestone set gained Limestone Gravel, Limestone, Limestone Rubble, Limestone Rubble - Medium, Limestone Stalactite - Large and Limestone Stalactite - Small.
  • Cobalt Ore has a new texture.

Furnace

The Furnace gained Tier 2, with a new model, textures and animations. The Tier 1 version got updated visuals too.

Regions and encounters

  • The Taiga got more complex spawn locations, especially near rivers.
  • Encounters were reworked across every zone, with new environments for Trork in Zone 3 and for villages, forests and other Zone 4 biomes.
  • A new Outlander village environment was added.
  • Zone 1 weather VFX improved.
  • Several prefabs lost the workbenches and the Chicken Coop they had.
  • Spawn marker deactivation range was standardized at 150.

Gameplay

  • Doors no longer close when an entity or player is in the way.
  • The Teleporter requires you to move a short distance away before using it again.
  • The Minecart no longer moves when stuck.
  • The Large Kweebec Chest accepts blocks on top.
  • Fire spreads like a fluid, and the Scrap Flamethrower unleashes flames on nearby foes. Creative only for now.
  • Grass spreads on its own, thanks to a random block ticking system.
  • Fluids, mounts and crafting improved, as did the teleporter, NPC reputation, NPC objectives, farming, Memories, camera and bed modules.

Interface and quality of life

Map and compass

  • Custom markers with name, icon and color. You can share them in multiplayer and delete them from the map context menu.
  • New gameplay settings show or hide markers on the map and compass.
  • Marker icons were updated, with higher resolution and clearer text.
  • The compass shows different icons depending on height: above or below you.
  • In Adventure mode, the teleport option is hidden on markers.
  • Player, spawn and home icons were redrawn.

Inventory and benches

  • Shift-dragging transfers items between inventories.
  • The sort keybind works in the player inventory.
  • A "Take All" button on processing bench outputs.
  • The utility wheel quick swap includes the inactive slot.
  • The workbench-to-chest connection radius text is clearer.

Languages and fonts

Brazilian Portuguese and Russian kick off the first phase of localization. To support non-Latin languages, the client pulls missing glyphs from fallback fonts, with Noto Sans covering Cyrillic. Item tooltips were rebuilt, and some UI elements grew to fit translated text.

Avatar

  • Presets: edit, save and swap between up to 5 looks. The button shows how many presets you have and asks for confirmation before swapping with unsaved changes.
  • Tweed Hat, Mage Shirt, and Styled Flats, Wedge Shoes and Shaped Sneakers.
  • Construction Hard Hat, Spiked Bracelets, Hairband and two skirts, Plaid and Punk.
  • Black and Red Iron color variants for metals.
  • Six new haircuts, including Spikey Mohawk, Magical Pigtails and Puffy Bubble Braids.
  • Four facial hair styles: Split Moustache, Pinstripe Moustache, Anchor Beard and Viking Beard.
  • Two eye shapes and two makeup options, Blush and Eyeliner.
  • Overpants are now called Legwear.

Other tweaks

  • A notification and sound play when other players try to sleep.
  • A Window Size option in video settings.
  • Feedback now separates bug reports from other suggestions.
  • Teleporter warps are sorted alphabetically.
  • Long server and mod lists are easier to navigate.
  • Death messages were rewritten.

Audio

  • Three output modes: stereo speakers, stereo headphones and 3D audio headphones.
  • Sword swing and impact sounds were updated.

Performance and stability

  • World generation got faster, and client assets got smaller.
  • The world map and chunk updates now use their own network streams.
  • World saving is atomic, and backups are safer. Corrupted worlds show warnings.
  • Crashes fixed: when undoing and redoing huge creative tool changes, when a skin fails to save, on Linux without Nautilus, when overriding read-only asset packs, when regenerating chunks and in worlds with plugins.
  • Several memory leaks were closed.

Notable fixes

  • Security: directory traversal in worlds and prefabs, and an exploit that let users run commands on behalf of other connected players.
  • Respawning inside a portal world left the player in a zombified state.
  • Changing worlds while mounted left stuck states on the Minecart and the Horse.
  • Chunk saving deadlocked when a world was removed.
  • The Volcanic Cobble spawned in the wrong places, and the Adamantite Ore - Magma texture tiled badly.
  • The Darkwood Planks - Decorative dropped the wrong item. The Bottletree and the Crystalwood Log dropped items with the wrong ID.
  • Some apple trees floated, and fire spread above 256 blocks.
  • Village furniture required a pickaxe to break.
  • Upside-down stairs did not connect properly.
  • Dying could leave carried items unusable because of durability.
  • The player hitbox while sitting and sleeping was adjusted.
  • Water audio played only in the left ear, and HRTF panning was extreme.

For servers and modders

  • Anchor UI: server plugins can insert elements into client pages, such as the world map, at predefined points that will grow over time.
  • Mods can be enabled and disabled from UserData/Saves/<save>/mods.
  • Server version checks for plugins and asset packs. New asset packs are enabled by default.
  • Mod validation issues block server startup unless flagged. In singleplayer, validation can be skipped.
  • Warnings for duplicate plugins and for worlds with missing mods.
  • Server configs no longer enable every mod by default, and client worlds now have backups on by default.
  • The Creative Hub is now Crossroads, with a "Return to Crossroads" button on the map of creative worlds.
  • A new /layer command applies layering to a selected area.
  • The Paste tool supports holding the key, undo and redo, and moves more smoothly in several directions. The draft brush is now a sphere.
  • Machinima got a playback speed control.
  • New debug flags: VisAiming, VisLeashPosition and VisFlock, plus 3D audio source and NPC sensor visualization.
  • WorldGen V2 gained the YSampled Density node, exact material rotation, a Bound PositionProvider for spawn meshes, configurable spawns in portal biomes and a performance boost.
  • HideFromTooltip arrived in EntityStatType, and light radius was renamed to bright radius with a default of 1.
