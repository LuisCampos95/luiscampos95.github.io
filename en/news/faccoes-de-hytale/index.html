Hytale's factions: who lives in Orbis and who wants you dead

Kweebecs, Trorks, Ferans, Scaraks, Outlanders, Goblins, the undead, the Void and hidden peoples: culture, types, drops and rivalries.

Orbis isn't an empty map waiting for the player to show up. Every zone has owners: villages of walking trees, camps full of monster hunters, hives that glow in the dark and masked clans that don't take visitors. Here are all of Hytale's peoples, one by one.

The information comes from two places, and the text always says which is which. The first is official: hytale.com blog posts since 2018, the 2026 patch notes and the lore the game itself shows in item descriptions. The second is the game files, catalogued here on the wiki: health, drops, where each creature spawns and how it treats the player and other factions. When something comes only from the files, the text says "according to the game files". When it's a fan reading, it says "the community believes".

One warning before we start. Since 2023, Hypixel has said that every faction went through a full rework. In the July 2026 Chapter 1 post, the team confirmed that all of them, Kweebecs included, will be reworked again in future chapters. What's in the game today is a snapshot.

Who hates whom in Orbis

The game has attitude files that define who each group attacks, respects or ignores. They alone are enough to draw the map of rivalries:

  • Kweebecs vs Trorks. Each side marks the other as hostile.
  • Ferans vs Scaraks. Ferans also hunt spiders, scorpions and snakes. Scaraks target Ferans.
  • Goblins vs skeletons and spiders. Skeletons return the favor and attack goblins.
  • The Void vs almost everyone. Void creatures are hostile to Ferans, Goblins, Kweebecs and the player. Trorks and Outlanders are not on the list.
  • Hierarchy. Trorks revere their chieftain. Outlanders revere the Marauder.

One detail says a lot. According to the game files, there is a Kweebec prisoner, and Trorks have a rule to ignore it instead of attacking. Add the Trork Cage to that and the conclusion is clear: Trorks take Kweebecs hostage.

Kweebecs

Who they are

Kweebecs are Orbis' tree folk. The first NPC blog post, from 2018, introduces them as some of your earliest allies. Their whole culture revolves around a life cycle that Hypixel detailed across three posts.

It all starts with Seedlings, seeds that drop from the branches of the Elders at the heart of the village. They're energetic, rush around and keep getting into trouble. As they grow, young Kweebecs leave the village in search of knowledge. When they come back, they become mature Kweebecs: artisans, cultivators and sometimes warriors. In old age, they put down roots and turn into trees called Elders, which slumber for long stretches while village life goes on around them.

Treesingers are the exception. They resist the transformation and become the bridge between the young and the sleeping Elders, passing down the village's stories. Kweebecs never meet their parents: they learn about the past from the Treesingers.

Some mature Kweebecs range further. These are the Razorleaves, who head into Orbis' most dangerous forests to hunt down threats to their home. According to the 2025 post, that's how a peaceful people survived marauding Trorks and even Varyn's invasion.

There's one more lovely idea in the 2023 post. An artist suggested that Kweebecs use music to coax plants into useful shapes. That became Sungwood, the ornate wood of Kweebec clothing, weapons and homes.

Where they live

In the forests of the Emerald Wilds. According to the game files, villages change style with the forest: oak, autumn, azure and swamp. There are also redwood villages in the first region of the Whisperfrost Frontiers, and Update 1 gave the Kweebecs there their own loot tables. The Forgotten Temple has its own community, with patrols, a merchant and even a scared Kweebec.

How they treat the player

They're neutral. In the Chapter 1 post, Hypixel says Seedlings may follow you around and Saplings watch over their community and the flora and fauna of the Emerald Wilds.

According to the game files, Saplings run away when they see a player holding an axe or a hatchet. That fits the official text on the Crude Hatchet: Kweebecs accept that other cultures need wood, as long as nobody walks into their woodlands with a blade bared. Another detail from the files: rabbits count as the village's pets.

Types

  • Kweebec Seedling: the seed, with 20 health.
  • Kweebec Sproutling: 29 health, with a patrolling version.
  • Kweebec Sapling: 74 health. The orange (Kweebec Sapling) and pink (Kweebec Sapling) versions have 38.
  • Kweebec Rootling: 62 health.
  • Kweebec Razorleaf: the warrior, with 105 health and the Leaf Spear.
  • Kweebec Elder: 38 health.
  • Rootling Merchant: the merchant, with 50 health.

The files also keep models in other colors with no behavior, including one wearing a hard hat: Hard Hat Kweebec Sapling.

Items and trade

No Kweebec catalogued here has a drop list. What they have is trade. The merchant takes Essence of Life for spices, salt, dough, berry bushes, salads, skewers and pies like the Apple Pie. Recipes such as the Recipe: Meat Pie cost Greater Essence of Life.

Tied to the Kweebecs: Kweebec Helm, Kweebec Cuirass, Wooden Kweebec Staff, a full furniture set with Kweebec Bed, Kweebec Lantern and Carved Kweebec Figure, the Kweebec Plushie and the Egg Spawner - Kweebec Razorleaf. The Kweebec Ancient block uses the model of an old Kweebec, with and without a beard, which recalls the bearded Treesingers of the old design.

In the patch notes

  • Update 1: Kweebecs became immune to cactus and bramble damage.
  • Update 3: the large Kweebec chest now supports blocks on top.
  • Update 6: the large chest counts as wood when broken, and the plushie got its own sounds.
  • Chapter 1 post (July 2026): new behaviors for Seedlings and Saplings.

Trorks

Who they are

Big, rowdy and organized by class. The 2018 post describes Trorks as formidable enemies that come in many types, from eagle-eyed sentries to powerful chieftains, each with its own behavior pattern. In the 2025 post, Hypixel calls them Orbis' most dangerous monster hunters.

The same post says the Trorks' aggression puts them in the path of an old adversary once again in the forests of Earthglade. It doesn't name that adversary. The community believes it's the Kweebecs, and the attitude files point the same way.

Where they live

According to the game files, across most of the Emerald Wilds: plains, birch and aspen woods, flower fields, gullies, autumn and moss forests. Their camps come with tents, burrows, bonfires, storehouses, traps and warning signs. Update 3 mentioned "Zone 3 Trorks" and Update 1 touched the loot of Trorks in the Whisperfrost Frontiers, so they show up in the cold too. In the 2025 post, their official home is the Central Grasslands, the heart of the old Zone 1.

How they treat the player

They're hostile. The game files show a tribe with its own routine:

  • Sentries fire warning shots with their spears before attacking for real. They also throw spears at suspicious bushes.
  • Meat on the ground gets attention. A Trork that sees meat lying within 15 blocks goes after it and eats it. In theory, you can use that as a distraction.
  • Warriors spar with each other, sleep and greet their companions.
  • Hunters and shamans walk with wolves. The hunter's wolf (Hunting Wolf) has 38 health; the shaman's (Hunting Wolf) has 118.

Types

  • Trork: 42 health, empty-handed.
  • Trork Brawler: 61, with a club and rocks.
  • Trork Mauler: 61, with a mace.
  • Trork Warrior: 61, with a battleaxe.
  • Trork Guard: 61, with a spear and daggers.
  • Trork Sentry: 61, the spear thrower.
  • Trork Hunter: 61, with a sword, an axe and a wolf.
  • Trork Elder: 61, with a fire crystal staff.
  • Trork Elder: 74. In game, this Trork and the shaman share the same name.
  • Trork Chieftain: the chieftain, with 124 health.

The files also hold two models with no behavior: Trork Hatchling and Exceptional Trork.

Items and drops

Every Trork drops 1 to 3 Linen Scraps. Each piece of the Trork warrior armor has a 1.3% chance: Trork Warrior Helm, Trork Warrior Cuirass, Trork Warrior Gauntlets and Trork Warrior Pants.

Weapons drop at 15%: the Stone Trork Battleaxe from the warrior and chieftain, the Stone Trork Spear from the guard and sentry (2 to 4 at once), the Stone Trork Sword from the hunter and the Stone Trork Mace from the mauler. The arsenal also includes the Trork Staff, the Stone Trork Daggers, the Stone Trork Axe and the Stone Trork Longsword. Builders get the Egg Spawner - Trork.

In the patch notes

  • Update 1: the shaman got its final summon animation.
  • Update 4: the hatchets Trorks throw stopped spinning forever.
  • Update 6: a hunter without its wolf no longer gets stuck in combat.

Ferans

Who they are

Ferans are the desert people, with huge fox-like ears. Hypixel has shown little about them in blog posts, but the game says a lot through item descriptions.

The Fragment: Orbis - Windrider Valley fragment says a Feran values freedom above all else. The Thorium Ingot reveals that Ferans don't use thorium themselves, but keep watch over its deposits in the deserts and badlands. Should their age-old enemies rise again, those reserves would be the first to be harvested. The text doesn't say who those enemies are. The community bets on the Scaraks, since the files set both peoples as enemies.

The Venom Sac completes the picture: antidotes the Ferans distilled from venom were once prized across the lands.

In 2023, Hypixel shared another Feran with a tease: an interesting outfit for a desert dweller, unless Orbis' factions aren't tied to a single biome.

Where they live

In the Howling Sands. According to the game files, there are villages in all three regions of the zone, from the Golden Steppes to the Desolate Basin, plus huts in oases. The Windrider Valley portal also has Feran buildings, and the Forgotten Temple hosts two of them.

How they treat the player

They're neutral. According to the game files, they go on alert and watch before deciding to attack, and Scaraks are the clearest trigger for a fight. Even civilians have an attack sequence: they won't take a beating quietly.

Types

  • Feran Cub: the cub, with 29 health.
  • Feran Civilian: 49 health.
  • Feran Burrower: 61, with bone daggers.
  • Feran Longtooth: 61, with a bone spear.
  • Feran Sharptooth: 61, with a bone sword and axe.
  • Feran Windwalker: 61, with a bone staff.

Items and drops

Adults drop Bone Fragments. At 15%, you get the Feran Bone Daggers from the burrower and the Bone Sword from the longtooth and sharptooth. The same arsenal includes the Bone Spear, the Bone Staff and the Feran Bone Axe.

The Feran furniture set is one of the biggest in the game, with the Feran Bed, Feran Chandelier and Feran Torch, among others. It also has the grimmest decor: Pile of Feran Skulls and Feran Rib.

In the patch notes

  • Update 1: fixes to placements, textures and interiors in Feran villages.
  • Update 4: Feran ribs can now be placed correctly.
  • Updates 5 and 6: Feran bookshelves, platforms and double doors were adjusted.

Scaraks

Who they are

Intelligent insects, not a mindless swarm. In the Summer 2024 post, Hypixel explained that Scaraks aren't psychic and don't have a hive mind. They communicate through light and gesture, like some real insects, and live in a strict hierarchy with a chain of command. That's what makes their almost uncanny coordination possible.

The cyan glow is the faction's signature: it's on their bodies, their hives and even their food. The team wanted them to feel almost alien, a foreign intelligence with its own evolution and goals, whose presence in Orbis remains a mystery.

The game adds a dark past. The Statue of the Founding Builder describes the Scaraks as a fallen people. In their oldest ruins, statues of the Founding Builder still give off a sense of disapproval over the atrocities committed under the first and last Scarak Empress. The statue belongs to the Primordial Scarak furniture set.

Where they live

In the Howling Sands. According to the game files, hives are concentrated in the Badlands and the Desolate Basin, with a few in the Golden Steppes. Seekers fly freely over deserts, plateaus and scrubland, lice show up in caves, and Scarak dungeons have their own version of each type.

How they treat the player

They're hostile. According to the game files, their favorite target is the Ferans.

Types

  • Scarak Louse: 21 health.
  • Scarak Seeker: 61, the flyer.
  • Scarak Fighter: 81, with a patrolling version.
  • Scarak Imperial Guard: 81.
  • Scarak Defender: 103.
  • Scarak Broodmother: the hive's queen, with 145. Dungeons have the Fledgling Scarak Broodmother, with 124.

Items and drops

They all drop Sturdy Chitin (the louse only 30% of the time). The broodmother drops 2 to 3 Venom Sac, and the seeker has a 50% chance to drop one.

From the hives come the Glowing Scarak Larva, the Large Scarak Hive Door, the Medium Scarak Egg Sack, the Scarak Cocoon and the Scarak Spit Clump. From the ancient ruins, pieces like the Primordial Scarak Brazier.

In the patch notes

  • Update 4: Scarak cocoons stopped making noise after bursting.
  • Update 5: meeting Scaraks now unlocks the right Memories, and spit clumps break in one hit.
  • Update 6: Scaraks became immune to brambles and poison.

Outlanders

Who they are

Humans who broke away from the rest of humanity. The 2019 post describes the Outlanders as a hostile, defensive and territorial faction. The endless fight against the cold and the beasts bred a brutal pragmatism, and the wooden masks they wear at all times reflect that coldness.

They're isolationists. Clan leaders man the walls and watchtowers, and strangers are never welcome. Rangers hunt and ambush with bows. Their expeditions take them to ancient battlefields and ritual sites.

The darkest part is their magic. The Outlanders explored darker forms of magic than other peoples. At night, the campfires of the most devoted burn green. It's said they've achieved a measure of mastery over death itself, drawing that power from the mystical waters of the wilds. Priests wield water magic and lead their civilization.

The colossal masked warrior from the announcement trailer is one of them, and the 2019 post treats him as the faction's greatest threat.

The game's texts fill in the story. The Essence of Ice says the Outlanders rejected the flame, split from their brethren and turned to the ice. The Statue of The Watcher says the whispers weren't what drove them to the cold, but the whispers were already there when they arrived. And the Shadoweave Scraps was devised before their first communion with the Void. The secret of the fabric has been lost.

Where they live

In the Whisperfrost Frontiers. According to the game files, most of their buildings sit in The Everfrost, the coldest region, with camps, forts, towers, gates, totems, ice caves and even boats. Update 3 integrated a new Outlander village environment. The Fragment: Orbis - Dragonspire Weald fragment warns of frozen forests that not even an Outlander dares enter, no matter how the whispers beckon.

Types

  • Outlander Unsworn: 81 health, with a cobalt pickaxe.
  • Outlander Initiate: 81.
  • Outlander Hunter: 61, with a bow.
  • Outlander Stalker: 61, with a crossbow.
  • Outlander Berserker: 103, with an axe.
  • Outlander Marauder: 103, with a sword. The one the others revere.
  • Outlander Brute: 124, with a battleaxe.
  • Outlander Priest: 103, with a club.
  • Outlander Sorcerer: 103, with a staff.

Priests and sorcerers keep hunting wolves: the priest's (Hunting Wolf) has 107 health, the sorcerer's (Hunting Wolf) has 118. According to the game files, the ranged fighters attack birds, and berserkers spar with each other.

Items and drops

Every Outlander drops 1 to 4 Shadoweave Scraps. The initiate, priest and sorcerer also drop Essence of the Void. According to the game files, the link to the Void shows up even in their loot.

The "Doomed Outlander" weapons are rare: Doomed Outlander Daggers at 15% from the initiate, Outlander Doomed Shortbow at 5% from the hunter, Doomed Outlander Sword and Doomed Outlander Shield at 2.5% each from the marauder. The stalker has a 5% chance to drop the Iron Hand Crossbow. The arsenal also includes the Doomed Outlander Axe, Doomed Outlander Battleaxe, Doomed Outlander Club, Doomed Outlander Staff and Doomed Outlander Fang Daggers. For decoration, there's the Outlander Banner.

The Cobalt Ingot carries an Outlander legend: the metal is said to be cold to the touch even when molten.

In the patch notes

  • Update 3: a new Outlander village environment and a fix to how their houses anchor.

Goblins

Who they are

The faction of the moment. In the July 2026 post, Hypixel explained why it picked goblins for Chapter 1: their tech is janky on purpose, and they're a fun faction to start Early Access with. They come in many sizes, roles and personalities. They look disorganized, but there's something going on under the chaos.

Not every goblin wants you dead. According to the post, some are friendly and one of them runs a shop. The files already have the Goblin Merchant model.

The game's texts have goblin humor. The Crude Pickaxe says only one thing stands between the goblin dukes and total control of the underground: they haven't figured out what a pickaxe is yet. The Bangstick is called the goblins' greatest invention. Or their worst.

Where they live

Underground. According to the game files, there are goblin caves in the Emerald Wilds, groups in volcanic caves of the Emerald Wilds and the Howling Sands, and the Goblin Lair, a dungeon with entrances through a library and an abandoned mining village.

Chapter 1 brought the Goblin Breach, an event that opens a temporary portal in your world. On the other side is an unstable fragment of Orbis taken over by goblins, with a growing Void presence. The world is regenerated on every visit, and the Void eventually pushes you out. In the files, that place is called the Guttering Hollows.

How they treat the player

Most are hostile. According to the game files, goblins pick up and eat food left on the ground. The thief digs through trash piles, runs from whoever chases it, trips and drops what it's carrying. Miners revere lobbers.

Goblins hate spiders and skeletons, and they write it on the walls: the Spider Warning Graffiti and Skeleton Warning Graffiti are warnings they paint. Since Update 7, they run from Void creatures unless they have enough allies nearby.

Types

  • Goblin Scrapper: the frailest, with 20 health. A light fighter that swarms its foes. The instance version, Goblin Scrapper, has 100.
  • Goblin Thief: 30 health.
  • Goblin Lobber: 50, the bomb lover.
  • Goblin Burner: 80, with the Scrap Flamethrower.
  • Goblin Miner: 100, with a powerful leap and the Scrap Pickaxe.
  • Goblin Guardian: 100, armored, with the Scrap Washboard Club and the Spiked Scrap Shield.
  • Goblin Feastmaster: the biggest, with 350. It calls in reinforcements mid-fight and wrecks its surroundings.
  • Goblin Duke: the old boss, with 226 health and the Goblin Duke's Scrap Mace. It now sits in the deprecated content folder.

The files also keep the Goblin Makinomagistra, Voidtaken Goblin and Goblin Boss models.

Items and drops

In the current catalog, only the burner (Linen Scraps) and the feastmaster (Wool Scraps) have drops, 2 to 4 each. The big goblin legacy is the gadgets: Scrap Drill, Spyglass, Scrap Glider, Rocket Boots and Hookshot. There's also the Scrap Club, the Scrap Lightbulb Staff, the Goblin Iron block, the Goblin Throne and the Stolen Chest.

In the patch notes

  • Update 3: the goblin flamethrower and the fire spread system arrived.
  • Update 7: the six reworked goblins, the gadgets, the flight from the Void and dozens of sound and animation tweaks. It's all in our Update 7 guide.

Undead

Who they are

The undead aren't in the game's faction folder, but they work like one. According to the files, skeletons have their own attitude: friendly to each other and hostile to goblins. The Undead category in Memories is the largest of all, with 61 creatures.

Their origin shows up in the game's texts. The Bone Fragments recalls that Orbis' most advanced civilizations shared one trait: the hubris that brought them down. The bones come from a cursed creature. The Cindercloth Scraps comes from Crucible, scoured by a cursed flame. The scraps, salvaged from charred bones, still give off heat.

Skeletons by region

Each zone has its own family. According to the game files:

  • Common, roaming the Emerald Wilds and part of the Whisperfrost Frontiers: Skeleton, Skeleton Fighter, Skeleton Soldier, Skeleton Archer, Skeleton Ranger, Skeleton Scout, Skeleton Mage, Skeleton Archmage and Skeleton Knight.
  • Sandswept, in the Howling Sands: Sandswept Skeleton Soldier, Sandswept Skeleton Guard, Sandswept Skeleton Assassin, Sandswept Skeleton Archer, Sandswept Skeleton Ranger, Sandswept Skeleton Scout, Sandswept Skeleton Mage, Sandswept Skeleton Archmage and the dungeon Sandswept Skeleton Warlock.
  • Frost, in the Whisperfrost Frontiers and the glacial henges: Frost Skeleton Soldier, Frost Skeleton Fighter, Frost Skeleton Knight, Frost Skeleton Archer, Frost Skeleton Ranger, Frost Skeleton Scout, Frost Skeleton Mage and Frost Skeleton Archmage.
  • Burnt, in the Devastated Lands and the sewers: Burnt Skeleton Soldier, Burnt Skeleton Knight, Burnt Skeleton Lancer, Burnt Skeleton Archer, Burnt Skeleton Wizard, Burnt Skeleton Alchemist and Burnt Skeleton Gunner.
  • Incandescent, in the Charred Woodlands and volcanoes: Incandescent Skeleton Fighter, Incandescent Skeleton Footman, Incandescent Skeleton Mage and the Incandescent Skeleton Head.
  • Pirates, placed in structures: Skeleton Pirate Captain with the Cutlass, Skeleton Pirate Gunner and Skeleton Pirate Striker.

Two skeletons deserve extra care. The Burnt Skeleton Praetorian has 226 health and, according to the files, also roams the forests, mountains and swamps of the Emerald Wilds, far from home. The Revenant Guardian, with the same health, got faster attacks and new sounds in Update 7.

Zombies and other dead

Zombies follow the same regional logic: Zombie, Sandswept Zombie, Frost Zombie and Burnt Zombie. The aberrant ones are another story: Small Aberrant Zombie with 126 health, Large Aberrant Zombie with 341 and Aberrant Zombie with 400.

The group is rounded out by the Ghoul, the Wraith of the Cinder Wastes, which carries the Void Scythe, the Wraith's Lantern, the Werewolf, the Shadow Knight with 400 health, the Bleached Hound, the Skeleton Horse and Armored Skeleton Horse, and even an undead farm: Undead Chicken, Undead Cow and Undead Pig.

Items and drops

Common skeletons drop Bone Fragments and Linen Scraps. The fighter has a 10% chance to drop the Scrap Sword, and the knight 5% for the Rusty Steel Sword and the Rusty Shield. Frost skeletons add Essence of Ice to the loot. The fire dead drop Cindercloth Scraps and Essence of Fire, and burnt skeletons also have 1.3% per piece of ancient steel armor.

The praetorian and the elite share the best loot: Thorium Ingot at 70%, the Grimoire: Rekindle Embers at 50%, Cobalt Ingot at 30% and each ancient steel armor piece, like the Ancient Steel Helm, at 3.8%. And yes, zombie limbs become weapons: Zombie Arm and Zombie Leg.

In the patch notes

  • Update 1: skeletons stopped drowning.
  • Update 5: skeletons coming out of coffins no longer fall through the floor.
  • Update 6: all zombies generate Memories again.
  • Update 7: the Revenant Guardian got faster and received new sounds.

Void creatures

What the Void is

The corrupting force of Orbis. The 2025 post names Varyn's invasion as one of the great challenges in the world's history. The Ancient Gateway adds another piece: a curse keeps Gaia's Orbis locked away, but there are ways to pierce the Void that veils it.

The Essence of the Void admits nobody knows whether the creatures produce the essence or need it to exist. The Voidheart explains the split the game itself uses: not every creature is born of the Void, but those taken and corrupted may bear the same shriveled heart. In Memories, that becomes two categories: Voidspawn and Voidtaken.

Voidspawn

  • Void Larva: 36 health.
  • Void Crawler: 74.
  • Void Eye: 60. According to the game files, it can summon other creatures.
  • Void Spectre: 30.
  • Void Fiend: 500 health and the Void Longsword.
  • Void Tentacle: 100.
  • Crystals in three sizes, from 25 to 150 health: Void Crystal, Void Crystal and Void Crystal.
  • Void Stutter: 1 health.

According to the game files, Voidspawn appear between 7 PM and 5 AM in dark places and vanish during the day. There are three danger tiers by region. Plains, savannas and tundras get larvae, crawlers and eyes. Swamps, azure forests, deserts, oases and glaciers also get the spectre. The moon phase changes the odds: the spectre doesn't show up in the first two phases. There are also Void invasion portals, which release eyes, crawlers and larvae around the player.

Voidtaken

The The Hedera is the only creature in the Voidtaken Memories category. The hunt bears were taken too: Voidtaken Grizzly, Voidtaken Scarback and Voidtaken Duskmane, with 300, 400 and 500 health. We covered their new look in this post. The files also hold corrupted models with no behavior: Voidtaken Goblin, Void Guardian Golem, Void Dragon and Void Necromancer.

Items and drops

The spectre drops 6 to 9 Essence of the Void, and the eye 4 to 6. The Voidheart always drops from the entombed spawn, at 15% from the eye, 5% from the spectre, 1% from the crawler and 0.5% from the larva. Also tied to the Void: Void Crystal Shards, Void Crystal Daggers, the Void Grimoire, Void Tar, the Void Liana and the Void Invasion Portal.

In the patch notes

  • Update 5: Void maps got lighter, and three seed bag recipes no longer require a Voidheart.
  • Update 7: the Void became hostile to goblins, the entombed spawn replaced an older creature and the Void event in Hedera's lair now escalates over time.

Other peoples and intelligent creatures

Klops: a people or a faction?

The Klops are intelligent and have homes and jobs, but the game files don't treat them as a faction. They sit in a separate folder, outside the faction folder that holds the Kweebecs, Trorks, Ferans, Goblins, Outlanders, Scaraks and Tuluks. They also have no attitude file and no Memories category of their own. In the official blogs and notes we checked, the only mention is a single line in Update 3. Community wikis usually call them a neutral faction. The safer reading is different: a small, peaceful people with no defined role yet.

According to the game files, the Klops live in underground homes dug into the caves of the Emerald Wilds, the Howling Sands and the Whisperfrost Frontiers, complete with bedroom, bathroom and cellar. There are three types, all with 74 health and neutral:

  • Klops Gentleman: drops Copper Ingot (75%) and Iron Ingot (25%).
  • Klops Miner: drops Copper Ore (75%) and Iron Ore (25%).
  • Klops Merchant: found in the Forgotten Temple. Its shop, in the current files, has a single trade: an Under Construction Sign for a sign. It looks like a placeholder.

A warning for diggers: according to the game files, Klops react when blocks near them are changed, go on alert and may come after you. Breaking a Klops home has consequences.

Hedera

The The Hedera is a Voidtaken boss with 226 health. Its lair sits behind the Fragment: Orbis - The Dread Wade fragment, and the portal's tips speak of a ravenous menace at the heart of the swamp. According to the game files, there are forests and monuments tied to it in the first region of the Whisperfrost Frontiers. Update 6 fixed its eyes and mouth, and Update 7 changed the Void event in its lair.

Yetis

The Yeti is a creature, not a people: the files list it among the beasts, in the Mythic Memories category. It has 226 health and drops Heavy Hide and Essence of Ice. According to the game files, there are yeti camps in the Boreal Reach. The 2019 zone 3 post already listed a yeti among the locals.

Slothians

A sloth people with no life in the game yet. The files have the Slothian and the variants Slothian Elder, Slothian Kid, Slothian Monk, Slothian Scout, Slothian Villager and Slothian Warrior, only as models. Their role is abstract, with 193 health and no actions. According to the files, their camps sit in the Charred Woodlands of the Devastated Lands, among jungle trees. Update 3 fixed their colors.

Tuluks

The Tuluk Fisherman is the only Tuluk with a role in the game, and it's a placeholder, with 100 health and no behavior. Oddly, Tuluks have their own folder inside the factions. No spawn file places them in the world. The base model is the Tuluk.

Saurians and Bramblekin

Saurians exist as models (Saurian, Saurian Hunter, Saurian Rogue and Saurian Warrior) and have their own weapons: the Adamantite Saurian Saber, Adamantite Saurian Spear and Adamantite Saurian Daggers. No behavior. The community believes they'll live in the Devastated Lands.

The Bramblekin and the Bramblekin Shaman have an abstract role, with 103 health, and no appearance in the world.

Official, files or guesswork

To wrap up, the yardstick we used:

  • Official: the Kweebec life cycle, Treesingers and Sungwood; Trork classes; Outlander culture; Scarak light-based communication; the Chapter 1 goblins; and the item lore the game shows.
  • According to the game files: health, drops, where each creature spawns, the attitudes between factions and behaviors like fleeing from axes, meat distracting Trorks and the goblin thief who trips.
  • Community guesswork: that the Ferans' age-old enemies are the Scaraks, that the Trorks' old adversary in Earthglade is the Kweebecs and that the Saurians will live in the Devastated Lands.
