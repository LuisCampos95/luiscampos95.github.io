Hytale Update 4: 500 blocks and voice chat

Ten weeks into Early Access, Update 4 brought over 500 blocks, proximity voice chat, an emote wheel, trees that grow in stages, reworked potions and crystal golems that drop gems.

Ten weeks into Early Access, Update 4 was the biggest package so far. The studio called it a foundation update. The new blocks are the palette for the worlds, biomes and dungeons still in production. Voice chat, emotes and the social improvements are the first pieces of a larger social layer that includes minigames.

The notes also pointed to the New Worlds Modding Contest, run with CurseForge: a US$100,000 prize pool for 65 winners. The studio said it uses the contest to find talent, especially in worldgen, NPCs and custom experiences.

Main changes

  • Over 500 blocks. About half can be crafted in Exploration mode, and the other half lives in the Creative inventory.
  • Proximity voice chat. Volume fades with the distance between players.
  • Emote wheel on the X key, with nine animations.
  • Saplings that grow into trees. Every species grows in stages, and the Apple Sapling arrived.
  • Reworked potions in five sizes, with two-step healing.
  • Sickles in copper and iron: the Copper Sickle and the Iron Sickle.
  • Revolve, a new Creative tool.
  • Seven new hairstyles, including afros, dreads and star puffs.

Voice chat

Your voice lives in the world. People close by sound loud. People who walk away fade out. Underwater or in echoing places, the sound changes. When someone talks, their character portrait pops up on the HUD and reacts along: it grimaces mid-fight against Trork and dances when the player uses the emote wheel.

  • It is off by default and enabled from the audio settings. There is also a toggle keybind, unbound by default.
  • You can pick the input device, input level, noise suppression and input mode, with optional push-to-talk.
  • The server operator sets the distance at which voices fade.
  • Any player can be muted from the list next to the map.
  • Accounts with parental controls are left out. The studio will only enable it once there is a system that gives parents control over voice.

The team behind it says it wants to add echo cancellation and sound occlusion by blocks. A friends list, invites, parties and easier ways to join worlds are also planned, with no date or commitment.

Creatures

  • The Black Wolf and the White Wolf hunt as a pack. They periodically try to flank their prey and retreat when injured.
  • Each crystal golem drops its own gem: the Earthen Golem drops Emerald, the Ember Golem drops Ruby, the Frost Golem drops Sapphire, the Sandswept Golem drops Zephyr and the Thunder Golem drops Topaz. Elemental golems also got updated animations and interactions.
  • The Moose Bull and the Moose Cow are 20% slower. The official notes mention "a recent incident" the studio is not prepared to discuss.
  • The Skrill now lays eggs.
  • The Goat and the Kid got new animations.
  • Defeated NPCs trigger death particles and drop their items when the body disappears.
  • The Burnt Skeleton Praetorian respects gravity better during charge attacks.
  • NPCs no longer follow items from an infinite distance.
  • Hatchets thrown by Trork no longer spin forever.
  • The Scarak Cocoon and the Spider Cocoon stop making noise after bursting.

Items and equipment

Potions

Potions were rebuilt in five sizes, and all of them now have quality levels. Health potions heal part instantly and the rest after 5 seconds:

  • Lesser Health Potion: 15% + 15%, 30% total. Stacks to 5.
  • Small Health Potion: 15% + 30%, 45% total. Stacks to 10.
  • Health Potion: 25% + 35%, 60% total. Stacks to 5.
  • Greater Health Potion: 25% + 50%, 75% total. Stacks to 10.
  • Large Health Potion: 35% + 55%, 90% total. Stacks to just 1.

Stamina potions are consumed faster and act instantly: the Lesser Stamina Potion restores 30%, the Small Stamina Potion 45%, the Stamina Potion 60%, the Greater Stamina Potion 75% and the Large Stamina Potion 90%.

Energy potions generate signature energy over 30 seconds: the Lesser Energy Potion 30%, the Small Energy Potion 45%, the Energy Potion 60%, the Greater Energy Potion 75% and the Large Energy Potion 90%.

Across all three families, Lesser and regular stack to 5, Small and Greater stack to 10, and Large stacks to 1. In practice, the strongest potion takes a whole slot.

  • Crafting requirements for health, stamina and energy potions were reworked.
  • The Small Potion Bottle and the Large Potion Bottle are now crafted at the Alchemist's Workbench.
  • Regular and Large potions have new models, and iridescent potions have new textures.
  • Blue, green, iridescent, pink, purple and orange potions became decorative items, such as the Decorative Potion (Blue), Decorative Potion (Green), Decorative Potion (Iridescent), Decorative Potion (Pink) and Decorative Potion (Purple).

Farming

  • The Copper Sickle and the Iron Sickle are crafted at the Farmer's Workbench. Holding the input swings the sickle repeatedly.
  • The Empty Watering Can waters a 3x3 area when charged, and its capacity went from 20 to 50. It can be placed on the ground while crouching, returns an empty can when broken and only waters with the secondary action, like the hoe.
  • The Crystal Fertilizer Bag is crafted at the Alchemist's Workbench and is now required by blood, storm and azure crops.
  • Seeds for those crops are now crafted at the Alchemist's Workbench with new recipes. The plants no longer drop seed recipes.
  • The Blood Leaf, the Storm Sapling and the Azure Kelp each have unique growth requirements.
  • The Salvager's Workbench turns those plants into their petals: Blood Petals, Storm Petals and Azure Petals.

Other items

  • Pink Crystal Shards were added and drop from the Pink Crystal.
  • The Fishing Trap can sit closer to other blocks and in shallow water, and always shows its placement preview.
  • The bow can no longer be drawn without ammo.
  • Hatchets, pickaxes and shovels lose durability when used on wood.
  • Previews for the Crude Bedroll, Crude Repair Kit and Repair Kit were updated.
  • Sword impacts and the Harvest Trophy got new visual effects.
  • Some names changed: the middle seaweed is now Stacked Seaweed, and the red and white cobble blocks are now Red Sandstone Cobble and White Sandstone Cobble.

World and blocks

The studio's rule for blocks is to complete the set. Every new rock comes with its raw form, cobble, bricks and related pieces such as roofs, stairs and beams. A big part of the work was finishing sets that were only half done.

New sets

  • Cloth gained 40 blocks, with stairs and half slabs for every color.
  • Cooled Magma gained over 30 blocks, including bricks, cobble, stalactites and rubble.
  • Chalk gained over 20, with cobble and rubble.
  • Slate Brick and Limestone gained over 10 each. Limestone also got expanded textures.
  • Dead coral, such as the Dead Coral Block, arrived as a Creative set.

Rock and soil

  • Almost every natural rock got half slabs, connecting stairs and beams.
  • Every rock type got gravel and gravel half slabs, such as Gravel and Gravel - Half Slab.
  • Cooled magma and chalk got stalactites and rubble, such as the Cooled Magma Stalactite - Large and the Chalk Stalactite - Large.
  • Dirt and grass went through a consistency pass: full grass, stairs and half slabs for every dirt type, plus Wet Dirt and Lush Dirt.
  • Colored clay got new textures.

Trees

Saplings actually grow now. Every species has seeds at the Farmer's Workbench and grows in stages until adulthood. Each sapling has its own texture, and seed bag icons were redone.

  • New species: the Apple Sapling, the Camphor Sapling, the Spiral Sapling and the Frostwood Sapling, redesigned from scratch.
  • Apples no longer grow on oaks. They come from the apple tree now.
  • Stormbark trees lost their Stormbark Roots.
  • Fire, ice and bamboo trees gained branches and roots, such as Fire Roots and Bamboo Roots. Their wood can be crafted into stairs and half slabs.

Roofs, fences and walls

  • Roofs from every build set connect dynamically.
  • Hide and leather tent roofs got steep and shallow versions, such as the Hide Tent Roof - Steep and the Leather Tent Roof - Shallow.
  • Yellow, green, white and dark blue cloth roofs arrived, plus modern cloth roofs in Creative.
  • Fences and walls support T-junctions and four-way junctions. It was a highly anticipated feature.

Cybercity and Creative

The first batch of sci-fi blocks is Creative only:

  • Modern iron, copper, zinc and bronze metal sets with pipes: Iron, Copper, Zinc and Bronze, plus pieces like the Bronze Pipe - Long.
  • Colored light source blocks.
  • Neon signs such as the Neon Sign - Fruit, windows such as the Prototype - Cybercity Window, posters and the Arcade Machine.
  • Five greyboxing block sets for blocking out structures.
  • The Red Flag and the Orange Flag, seasonal decorations and the Moving Box.

The Creative inventory gained Wood, Metal, Cloth and Technical subcategories and dropped the Structural one.

Other world changes

  • Workbenches keep processing even when their chunk is unloaded, and resume when a full output slot is emptied.
  • They now use in-game time instead of real time. If world time is stopped, a warning tooltip shows up.
  • Workbenches show how many items are queued and the rarity of what you can craft.
  • A new backup management screen tries to recover broken worlds.
  • Oasis portal spawns were adjusted to keep players inside the basin, and the biome runs lighter.
  • Fireflies show up more often in the Zone 1 plains.
  • Pillars can be placed sideways, and the Lumberjack Lamp can face different directions.
  • Decorative chains such as the Iron Chain, and brick beams, can hold more hanging blocks. Chandeliers hang from more block types.
  • Windows and cloth roofs no longer need support blocks.
  • The Blue Winter Seaweed releases bubbles, and the oxygen it grants changed.

What the studio plans for blocks

The notes share a few plans, with no dates. Some sets that are Creative only today may reach Exploration mode, as long as they fit the progression, biomes and benches. Very modern blocks, such as Cybercity, may stay Creative only or become part of a minigame. The connection system should grow to cover pipes, branches, roofs, signs, chandeliers, torches and plants. Internal block IDs should also be cleaned up later, but only after a migration plan that won't break worlds. Each NPC faction should get themed furniture.

Gameplay

  • Several crafts got faster: the Builder's Workbench went from 2 seconds to instant, and the Campfire, Crude Bedroll and Wooden Chest went from 1 second to instant. The Crude Torch is instant too.
  • The Alchemist's Workbench gained Tiers 3, 4 and 5. The Farmer's Workbench lost Tiers 9 and 10.
  • More sapling varieties can be crafted at the Farmer's Workbench.
  • Every full rock, wood and metal block can become half slabs and stairs at the Builder's Workbench. Colored cloth blocks refine correctly there again.
  • The Gold Ingot can become Gold Brick at the Builder's Workbench.
  • Runic sets left the Builder's Workbench and remain in the Creative inventory only.
  • Crops growing in sunlight no longer get an extra bonus from nearby artificial lights.
  • With a hoe and a shield equipped, tilling takes priority over blocking.
  • Ammo is no longer consumed in Creative.

Interface and quality of life

Emotes and avatar

The emote wheel opens by holding X, and the key can be rebound. There are nine emotes: Wave, Wave Double, Laugh, Tongue, Chicken, Kill, Punch and Dab left and right. Five of them are new, and some loop, with an icon marking which ones.

  • New hairstyles: Afro, Big Afro, Frizzy Buns, Long Dreadlocks, Puffy Twin Dreads, Dreads Fade and Star Puffs.
  • Puffy Bubble Braids, Afro Puffs and Puffy Bun were updated.
  • The avatar has a new animation for falls from great heights.
  • Renamed: one of the Basic Sandals is now Leather Sandals, the Long Sleeved Tunic is now Cotton Tunic and Puffy Ponytail is now Puffy Bun.

Inventory and controls

  • You can run multiple Hytale instances at once. The studio advises against opening the same world twice.
  • Holding the Drop Item button and hovering over the inventory drops whole stacks in Adventure mode. In Creative, it destroys them.
  • The backpack shows how many slots are in use.
  • Gatherable items show a "Pick" hint. Doors, fences and trapdoors show "Open".
  • Personal, shared and player markers are always visible on the map.
  • Settings were reorganized into clearer categories.
  • Support for the Windows ClickLock accessibility feature.

Languages

  • Ukrainian was added.
  • Support for Chinese fonts, Traditional and Simplified, at higher quality.
  • On launch, the game detects whether it needs to load the Chinese font when "Use System Language" is on.
  • Different apostrophes are unified, so item search works the same in any language.

Audio

  • Genesis returned to Zone 4, and Traveling Band now plays at night in Zone 1.
  • Zone 4 caves and sewers got placeholder music and ambience.
  • Zone 1 mountain ambience changes with altitude.
  • Rain has its own sound on water and on windows.
  • Pausing the game muffles sounds instead of silencing them.
  • Recording and restoring Memories now plays sound effects.
  • The stereo headphones mode blends in a center source so audio feels less stuck in one ear.

Performance and stability

  • The world map uses half the bandwidth it used before.
  • Item containers were reworked into block components, which cuts lag and avoids chest issues when chunks unload.
  • WorldGen V2 uses less memory when loading prefabs, which speeds up asset reloads and world creation.
  • World generation and chunk reference storage are faster.
  • Creative tools are much faster and preview more blocks.
  • Many crashes are gone, including at extreme coordinates, with mounts, during world transitions, with nameless map markers and with corrupted preview images.

Notable fixes

  • Worlds turned transparent at sunset.
  • Zone 4 sewers now always generate, and only, under cities. Trees no longer overlap Zone 4 structures.
  • Redwood biomes now appear inside Taiga portal worlds.
  • Portal devices refund fragments if the portal world fails to generate.
  • Lava burns entities again.
  • Wheat is no longer edible, and Stacked Seaweed can no longer be eaten.
  • Mounts no longer sprint forever in fluids.
  • Eating no longer resets mouse sensitivity.
  • Charged mace swings with low mouse sensitivity could leave the player stuck.
  • Client-predicted arrows got stuck in ladders.
  • Salt and the Tropical Wood - Fence dropped something entirely different.
  • Skewers such as the Meat Skewer are held properly.
  • The Crude Shovel now asks for Stone Rubble in its recipe.
  • The Feed Bag and the Fertilizer Bag moved to the Tools category.
  • Gravel and the placed Cane Ornament were unbreakable.
  • The Tavern Planter takes several hits and breaks faster with a hatchet.
  • The Feran Rib and the Green Crystal - Small can be placed correctly.
  • Redwood now counts as softwood.
  • Larkspur drops the right items.
  • The Frozen Castle Platform has a larger hitbox, and the Royal Magic Bed got a better hitbox and sleeping position.
  • The Lumberjack Painting no longer prompts an interaction.
  • Stacked slabs consume and drop the right number of items.
  • Players now enter the Minecart, instead of the cart teleporting under them.
  • The Tier 1 Furnace is less transparent at certain angles.

For servers and modders

Custom emotes

The studio published the structure for adding emotes:

  • .blockyanim animation in Common/Characters/Animations/Emote/.
  • .png icon in Common/Icons/Emotes/.
  • .json config in Server/Emote/.
{
  "Animation": "Characters/Animations/Emote/MyEmoteName.blockyanim",
  "Name": "My Emote Name",
  "Icon": "Icons/Emotes/MyEmoteName.png",
  "IsLooping": true
}

Server and plugins

  • Voice commands: .voice on the client and /voice on the server.
  • A new mod management screen in the world settings, with version, icon and a warning when a mod targets another game version. MOD_ERROR (code 9) exits link straight to it.
  • A fallback server for when players disconnect unexpectedly.
  • A new PlayerRemovedFromWorldEvent, for custom leave messages.
  • CommandInteraction runs a command from an interaction.
  • Custom HUD UI support for any item.
  • Spawn settings on the PortalType asset, including SpawnProviderOverride, to decide where the return portal appears.
  • /world settings disabledfluidtickers stops fluids from spreading.
  • Minimap radius limits per world and server-wide.
  • Commands accept complex strings, such as /say or /ban <reason> with several apostrophes. Any unique substring of an argument works too.
  • Commands to bootstrap the auto-update layout from a bare JAR.
  • The server started moving to the JOML math library.
  • Asset packs: you can pick the mods folder, save prefabs into a pack through the new browser, and pack prefabs override internal ones. Modded worlds got an icon.

NPCs and data

  • A new VisPath debug flag and the /npc descriptors command.
  • NPC templates expose DeathParticles, DropDeathItemsInstantly and DeathAnimationTime, and actions can be disabled with Enabled.
  • Conditional entity effects and a new NPC separation mode, backwards compatible with existing configs.
  • Rock beams are now children of Stone - Beam, and gravel blocks were reparented to Gravel and Gravel - Half Slab.
  • Crystal shards got their own resource type, with every color parented to White Crystal Shards.
  • Resource types reference item IDs directly, and HardRocks became Rocks.

Creative tools

  • Revolve pastes rotations of the selection any number of times, or a full turn around a center point.
  • /replace was overhauled: swap and regex became subcommands, and the "from" argument accepts masks such as >Block and !Block.
  • The Creative Tools inventory was reorganized into five pages: common tools, specific tools, brush filters, prefab editing and machinima.
  • The Anchor Tool merged into the Prefab Selector, with SHIFT+R. ALT+T jumps between the prefab editor world and the last world visited. /pedit back does the same.
  • The Grass Brush is back, the Tint brush supports blending and opacity, and the material selector shows percentages.
  • The Laser Pointer can now point others toward the sky.
