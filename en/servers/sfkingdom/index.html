📈 How to climb the ranking

  1. The ranking follows the likes the server gets on the official Hytale server list, inside the game. Each player can like once.
  2. Favorites count for “Trending this week”: we add up the likes and favorites gained in the last 7 days.
  3. Ask your players to like and favorite the server: a reminder in the server chat, on Discord and on your website makes a difference.
  4. Add the badge to your website and Discord (under “Own this server? Promote it” at the bottom of the page): whoever clicks lands on this page, with the IP ready to copy.
  5. The numbers here are refreshed every 3 hours from hytale.tools, which mirrors the official list.
