Privacy policy

Who we are

OrbisHytale (orbishytale.com) is a fan-made Hytale wiki run by the Hytale Brasil community. We are not affiliated with Hypixel Studios. For anything about privacy, talk to us on our Discord: discord.orbishytale.com.

What you can use without an account

Almost everything: items, creatures, crafting, skins, servers, tutorials and sounds work without signing in or signing up.

What stays in your browser only

  • Favorites: the pages you favorite are kept in your browser's local storage (localStorage). They are not sent to us.
  • Language: your chosen language is kept in your browser too.

You can erase all of it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What we store on our server

  • Counters: how many people favorited each page and how many different people looked up each skin. So the same person is not counted twice, we store a scrambled code (an irreversible hash) made from your internet address (IP). We do not store the IP itself, and the code cannot be turned back into it. These codes expire on their own: 30 days for favorites, up to 1 year for the skin ranking.
  • Looked-up nicknames: a nickname you search on the skins page goes into the most-searched ranking if the character exists. Nicknames are public in Hytale.
  • Comments: when you comment we store the text, the date, the public name and picture of the account you signed in with, and that account's ID, so moderators know who wrote it.

Signing in with your Hytale account

Signing in is optional and only needed to comment. It runs on the official Hytale account system — there is no OrbisHytale account, and we never create a username or password for you. When you sign in, Hytale gives us your nickname and your profile id; the picture shown is your skin's face.

We never receive your password: you type it only on Hytale's own site. After signing in, your browser keeps a session cookie (ow_s) that lasts up to 30 days or until you click Sign out. It is the only cookie on the site, and it is not used for tracking or advertising.

Content loaded from elsewhere

The site is served by external providers, which receive your internet address when delivering each page, as happens on any website. Some parts also load straight from the source: skin images and models, and tutorial videos (in no-cookie mode). In those cases your browser talks to those services directly.

Visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

To know how many people visit each page we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies, builds no profile and does not follow you across sites: it counts visits in aggregate (page, country, browser, load time). We do not use Google Analytics or social media pixels.

Discord bot and rankings

  • Discord bot: when you use a command or take part in the Monthly League, the bot stores your Discord account ID, your points, and the date and reason for each point. Message text is read on the spot to count and is not stored; for images posted as builds we keep only a fingerprint (hash) to spot repeats. Terms searched in commands go into daily counters, without who searched, and are deleted after 60 days.
  • Skin ranking: the nicknames searched on the skins page (only ones that exist) and how many different people searched each one.
  • Likes and favorites: page favorites add up on a public counter that never says who favorited. Server likes come from the public server list, not from our site.

Moderation

Off-topic, offensive or spam comments are deleted, and the account may be blocked from commenting.

Contact

For anything about privacy: Discord (discord.orbishytale.com) or email brasilhytale@gmail.com.

Your rights (LGPD / GDPR)

You can ask to see, correct or delete your comments and session data, and you can sign out at any time. Ask us on Discord (discord.orbishytale.com); we reply within 15 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
